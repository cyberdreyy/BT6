## Question

- Entrypoint: one JSON-RPC call or websocket subscription from a single client, at most once per CLUSTER_SLOT_TIME_TARGET/2

- Fast validation: Proptest `create_leader_updater` across full integer ranges; assert checked arithmetic and no wrap in release mode.
