In lib.rs, trace where ActionReceiptMetadata.actions (receipt_manager.rs, populated via append_action_*) get converted into the final Vec<Action> of an on-chain ActionReceipt, and confirm the action ORDER is preserved exactly as appended (no re-sorting, deduplication, or reordering pass), because action_validation.rs's DeleteActionMustBeFinal and DelegateActionMustBeOnlyOne checks are order-sensitive; if any conversion step reorders actions (e.g. to batch similar action types for gas accounting), a contract could append DeleteAccount first and a Transfer second, relying on the conversion step to reorder DeleteAccount to the end, defeating the intended-final-action invariant and letting funds move to a beneficiary post-hoc rather than atomically with account deletion.
