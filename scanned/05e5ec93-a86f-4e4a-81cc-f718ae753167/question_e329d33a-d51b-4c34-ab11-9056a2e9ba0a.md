Preconditions: pre-restart BlockInfo{state: GloballyAccepted, signed_self: Some(t), valid: Some(true)}; post-restart deserialization yields signed_self: None due to a field mismatch, and valid remains Some(true) (not Some(false)). Equality broken: signed_self semantics before restart (already responded) == signed_self semantics after restart. Impact: Critical - the signer re-signs or re-broadcasts a signature-equivalent response for a block that has already reached global consensus, which if replayed against a since-superseded sibling at the same height, produces two conflicting signatures at one height.
