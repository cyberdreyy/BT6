[access_keys.rs] Function: action_transfer_to_gas_key vs action_withdraw_from_gas_key balance bookkeeping. Attacker action: a third-party sponsor account calls Transfer (deposit) into the attacker's gas key via TransferToGasKeyAction with deposit=D expecting D to only fund gas costs metered by the runtime's fee schedule; attacker then withdraws via WithdrawFromGasKeyAction directly to account.amount() without ever executing a metered receipt. Preconditions: WithdrawFromGasKeyAction reachable outside a nested DelegateAction (RejectWithdrawFromGasKeyInDelegate only blocks the delegate path, not a direct signed transaction). Sequence: TransferToGasKeyAction(D) -> gas_key_info.balance += D; WithdrawFromGasKeyAction(D) -> gas_key_info.balance -= D; account.set_amount(account.amount()+D). Invariant: value conservation - a gas key's balance must only be consumed by metered gas usage, not moved 1:1 to the free balance without having funded any receipt. Scoped impact: Fee payment bypass - the sponsor's deposit intended to cover gas fees is fully liquidated by the account owner with zero gas actually burnt, bypassing the entire fee-consumption quantity the sponsor funded it for. Proof idea: apply-path integration test where account A funds account B's gas key by D, B immediately withdraws D to its own balance in the next block with zero receipts executed against that gas key, assert D is fully recovered while metering_totality (gas burnt attributable to the gas key) is 0.
