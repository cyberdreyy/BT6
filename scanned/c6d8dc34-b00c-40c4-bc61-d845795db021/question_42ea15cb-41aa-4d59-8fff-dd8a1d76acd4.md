[Target: NakamotoBlockHeader::compute_voting_weight_threshold] Attacker submits a BlockProposal timed so the signer's local view of total_weight (from a RewardSet with a signer who unstaked) differs from the total_weight the node used at verify_signer_signatures time (post-unstake RewardSet). Preconditions: a signer's stake update transaction (as in pox-5 stakeUpdate) confirms between when the miner drafted its proposal and when signatures are tallied. Call sequence: signer computes threshold = compute_voting_weight_threshold(stale_total_weight); node recomputes threshold = compute_voting_weight_threshold(new_total_weight); the two thresholds diverge. Equality: total_weight (and thus threshold) at proposal-processing time in the signer == total_weight at block-acceptance time in the node's chainstate. Impact: Critical - a block signed against a lower stale threshold could be broadcast and accepted by the signer set as finalized while the node computes the newer, higher threshold requires more weight, producing a block finalized with mis-counted weight. Proof idea: Rust test in stacks-signer/v0/signer_state.rs adjusting total_weight input between two calls to compute_voting_weight_threshold and asserting a signer refuses to broadcast_signed_block unless recomputed against the latest RewardSet.
