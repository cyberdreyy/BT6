The `signer_pending_pre_commit_responses`/`signer_pending_signature_responses`/`signer_pending_rejection_responses` tables (schema 19) each keep only the 3 most recent rows per `signer_addr` via `evict_old_pending_*_responses` triggers (`LIMIT -1 OFFSET 3`). If a victim signer restarts mid-tenure with more than 3 outstanding pending responses buffered for a given peer signer address (e.g., because the attacker's proposal caused an unusually long unresolved-proposal backlog), can replaying/reinserting older buffered responses after restart trigger eviction of pre-commit responses that were about to cross the signing threshold, permanently starving that block of enough recorded pre-commits and wedging the signer into never finalizing a valid, otherwise-signable block? Impact: High, restart-amplified eviction causes a liveness wedge (LIVENESS/DOMAIN). Proof idea: seed 4+ pending pre-commit rows for one `signer_addr` pre-restart order-dependent, restart, replay in a different order, and assert the row needed to reach threshold was evicted, then assert the block never transitions out of `PreCommitted`/`Unprocessed`.
