Target enum `VestingInformation::VestingHash` collapse to `U128(0)` in `LockupContract::get_locked_amount` (match arm `_ => U128(0)`), file `lockup/src/getters.rs`, exercised by lockups created through `LockupFactory::create` with `VestingScheduleOrHash::VestingHash`. Attacker is owner of a fresh lockup created with a private `VestingHash` and `transfers_timestamp` in the past. Preconditions: `lockup_information.transfers_information == TransfersEnabled` with `transfers_timestamp <= block_timestamp`, `release_duration = None`. Call sequence: attacker calls `get_locked_amount()` (view) then `owner::transfer(get_liquid_owners_balance(), receiver_id)`. Binding broken: `unvested_amount` used inside `get_locked_amount` equals `U128(0)` for `VestingInformation::VestingHash(_)` even though the real (still-hidden) vesting schedule has non-zero unvested balance, so `get_locked_amount() == unreleased_amount` instead of `max(unreleased_amount, real_unvested_amount)`. Scoped impact: Critical, tokens that are contractually still unvested under the hidden schedule leave the account before the foundation ever reveals/terminates it. Proof idea: cargo test constructing `VestingInformation::VestingHash(hash)` with a known `VestingScheduleWithSalt` whose real `get_unvested_amount(schedule)` is nonzero, assert `get_locked_amount().0 < get_unvested_amount(schedule).0`.
