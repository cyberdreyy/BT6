Target: StakingContract::internal_stake and StakingContract.total_stake_shares (staking-pool/src/internal.rs). Attacker action: a delegator calls stake(amount) immediately after a competing delegator's deposit() lands in the same block. Preconditions: total_staked_balance>0, total_stake_shares>0. Call sequence: internal_stake() computes num_shares via num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount) then charge_amount via staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares), debiting account.unstaked by charge_amount but crediting total_staked_balance by stake_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares) - two different rounding directions on the same num_shares with no reconciling assertion that charge_amount's aggregate equals stake_amount's aggregate. Binding that breaks: SETTLEMENT - value debited from delegator (charge_amount) == value the delegator is later entitled to redeem (implied by stake_amount credited to the pool); the STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND absorbs the difference silently with no independent per-call accounting of how much of that fund has been consumed. Scoped impact: High - repeated stake/unstake cycles from many unprivileged delegators can drain the STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND below the 1-yoctoNEAR-per-action budget it was sized for, and once drained the total_staked_balance vs total_stake_shares price-never-decreases guarantee (verify_stake_price_increase_guarantee in tests) can be violated for the next delegator. Proof idea: cargo test looping stake()/unstake() calls via the Emulator and asserting STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND-equivalent balance stays >= 0 and share price is non-decreasing.
