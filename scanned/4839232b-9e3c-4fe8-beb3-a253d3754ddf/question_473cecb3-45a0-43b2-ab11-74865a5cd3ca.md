Target: StakingContract::on_stake_action (ext_self trait, staking-pool/src/lib.rs). Attacker action: an unprivileged delegator triggers a stake() call whose resulting internal_restake() staking action fails on-chain (e.g. amount below the protocol minimum staking threshold) purely by choosing a very small stake amount. Preconditions: total_staked_balance below minimum validator staking threshold after unstake_all by other delegators. Call sequence: internal_restake() promise .stake() fails, on_stake_action callback issues a corrective unstake, but total_stake_shares and each account's stake_shares were already updated in internal_stake() before the promise even executed. Binding that breaks: SETTLEMENT - stake_shares credited to the delegator's Account == shares actually backed by a successful validator stake; because internal_stake() mutates account.stake_shares synchronously and the actual staking Promise is asynchronous and fire-and-forget aside from the corrective callback, shares can exist that were never backed by a real stake if on_stake_action's corrective unstake itself races with a withdraw(). Scoped impact: Critical - stake_shares outstanding can exceed the amount actually staked at the validator, letting the delegator later unstake and withdraw NEAR that was never truly locked, against other delegators' funds. Proof idea: cargo test simulating PromiseResult::Failed for on_stake_action and asserting total_stake_shares still reflects the failed stake amount unless explicitly corrected.
