Preconditions: attacker times a proposal right at the reward-cycle rollover boundary. Call sequence: submit_block_for_validation under cycle N -> signer rolls to N+1 -> handle_block_validate_response checks self.reward_cycle mismatch -> silently ignored, no rejection sent, block_info left in Pending state, no wedge detection. Equality: LIVENESS/DOMAIN - every valid block must reach a bounded decision (accept or reject) within its (chain, cycle) binding; a response for cycle N delivered to a cycle-N signer instance must not vanish silently. Impact: High, signer wedged (never responds) for a legitimately valid last-minute block of the outgoing cycle, though only High since it stalls rather than mis-signs (escalate to Critical only if the outgoing cycle's threshold was reachable and now permanently blocked).
