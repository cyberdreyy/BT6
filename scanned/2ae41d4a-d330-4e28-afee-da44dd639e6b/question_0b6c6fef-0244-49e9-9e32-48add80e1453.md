Target: Allocator::new_chunk (alloc.rs:120-125) and allocation_class/allocation_size (alloc.rs:58-78). Verify numerically for every size in 1..=MAX_ALLOC_SIZE that allocation_size(allocation_class(size)) >= size and that the inverse mapping allocation_class(allocation_size(sc)) == sc for all sc < NUM_ALLOCATION_CLASSES, since any size_class collision would mean two logically different-sized allocations recycle from the same freelist and a smaller reused block could be handed out to satisfy a larger MemTrieNode write, overrunning into a neighboring victim account's node.
