Target: `ProposalCallbackReceiver::notify_proposal_result` contract and the signer's `signerdb.rs` write ordering relative to network broadcast of `BlockResponse::Accepted`. Attacker action: none required from attacker beyond normal proposing; the audit targets a wedge where, if the signer broadcasts its `Accepted` `BlockResponse` (and thus a partial commit visible to other signers/miner) BEFORE durably writing the corresponding 'signed' record to `signerdb.rs`, a crash between broadcast and write leaves the signer, on restart, unaware it already signed - and it could sign a conflicting block at the same height on restart. Preconditions: process crash between broadcast and signerdb commit. Call sequence: validate Ok -> sign -> broadcast -> (crash) -> restart -> re-evaluate same height with no signerdb record -> sign a different candidate. Equality: UNIQUENESS - distinct blocks signed per (cycle, tenure, height) == at most one; write-before-broadcast ordering must guarantee this even across a crash. Impact: Critical (double-sign at one height). Proof idea: stacks-signer test that signs, simulates a crash before the signerdb write completes (by controlling write ordering in a test harness), restarts a fresh `SignerDb` load, and asserts the signer either recovers the original decision or refuses to sign a second candidate at that height.
