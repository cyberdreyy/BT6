# Q3967: no expiry in the signed statement in SiweApi.ts

## Question
The statement built in src/client/auth/SiweApi.ts carries Issued At but no expiration; can an attacker replay a signature captured months earlier through SiweApi.init?

## Target
- File/function: [src/client/auth/SiweApi.ts](src/client/auth/SiweApi.ts) - SiweApi.init, loginWithSiwe, linkWithSiwe, unlinkWallet, generateSiweMessage
- Entrypoint: privy.auth.siwe.init(wallet, domain, uri) then loginWithSiwe(signature, wallet, message)
- Attacker controls: domain, uri, chainId, walletClientType, connectorType, full message override, signature
- Exploit idea: Sign once, store the message and signature, replay after a long delay.
- Invariant to test: Authentication statements must carry an expiry the client enforces.
- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - account takeover: an attacker gains authenticated control of another user's Privy account or session.
- Fast validation: Unit test: assert SiweApi.init rejects a message whose Issued At is older than a short window.
