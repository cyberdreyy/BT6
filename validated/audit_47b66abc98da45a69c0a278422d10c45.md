### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` can be front-run/back-run to steal a disproportionate share of donated same-bank emissions - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
The Acala report describes a fixed reward amount being split among whatever shares happen to be deposited at the moment of a periodic accumulation, with no minimum holding period, letting an attacker deposit right before the split and withdraw right after to capture a disproportionate share of the reward. marginfi-v2's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction has the same structural weakness: it is a **permissionless** call that instantly re-prices `asset_share_value` for **all current depositors** based on `total_asset_shares` at the moment of the call, with no vesting, no time-weighting, and no restriction on depositing/withdrawing around the call.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` [1](#0-0)  lets any signer ("depositor") transfer same-mint tokens into the bank's liquidity vault, and then immediately spreads that amount across the pool by recomputing `asset_share_value`: [2](#0-1) 

The new share value is `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, where `total_asset_shares` is read at the instant the instruction executes. This is functionally identical to Acala's `accumulate_incentives`, which divides a fixed reward among whatever shares exist at accumulation time — except here the "reward" is an on-chain, permissionless, atomically-executed deposit rather than a periodic on_initialize hook. Both mechanisms share the same root cause: a fixed value is distributed pro-rata to instantaneous share balances, with no minimum holding period or time-weighting to prevent last-moment share inflation.

Because ordinary `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` have no lockup, cooldown, or unbonding period (confirmed via `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_account/deposit.rs` and `withdraw.rs`), a user can:
1. Observe (or anticipate, since emissions funding is often done on a schedule per `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call.
2. Deposit a large amount of the bank's underlying asset immediately before that call, inflating their share of `total_asset_shares`.
3. Let the emissions deposit execute, which raises `asset_share_value` proportionally, crediting the attacker with an outsized fraction of the donated emissions relative to their honest holding duration.
4. Withdraw immediately afterward, realizing the gain with negligible exposure time and no loss (deposits/withdrawals of the same asset are lossless aside from any accrued interest, which is negligible over such a short window).

This crowds out honest, long-term depositors from same-bank emissions/incentive deposits, exactly the value-leak pattern described in the referenced report.

### Impact Explanation
Emissions/incentive funds that third parties (protocol, group admin, or any funder) donate to reward depositors can be redirected disproportionately to a short-term, opportunistic depositor rather than the long-term liquidity providers the incentive was meant to reward. This is a value-redirection issue with direct financial effect: honest depositors receive a smaller share of the incentive than their time-weighted contribution warrants, while the attacker captures value with minimal capital lock-up and risk.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is explicitly documented and implemented as **permissionless** ("Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions..."), and `lending_account_deposit`/`withdraw` carry no cooldown. Exploitability depends on the attacker being able to predict or race the emissions-deposit transaction (e.g., via Jito bundles/atomic composition in the same transaction, or by anticipating a recurring/scheduled emissions funding cadence as implied by the emissions documentation). This mirrors the same external assumptions (transaction ordering/timing control, available capital) that the original Acala judge accepted as reasonable for a Medium-severity finding, since the attacker can, in the simplest case, bundle their own deposit, the emissions-deposit trigger, and their withdrawal atomically in a single transaction alongside the legitimate funder's action, or via same-slot ordering control.

### Recommendation
- Time-weight or vest emissions contributed via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, e.g., by accruing the deposited amount gradually into `asset_share_value` over a defined window rather than atomically, similar to how interest is compounded continuously via APR rather than as an instantaneous lump-sum split.
- Alternatively, restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` such that depositors who join immediately before the call cannot fully participate (e.g., snapshot eligible shares prior to the funding transaction, or require a minimum holding period before a balance is eligible for the pro-rata share-value bump).
- Consider requiring emissions funding to only be composable in the same transaction as no external deposits (or applying a cooldown on deposit/withdraw around emissions funding events).

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors for (or bundles with) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` call on a target bank.
2. In the same transaction (or immediately preceding block/slot), attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large sum `X`, inflating `total_asset_shares`.
3. The `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes: `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` [3](#0-2) , crediting attacker's shares with a portion of `amount` proportional to `X / (total_assets + X)`.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to realize the gain, having held the position for only the duration of one or two instructions, versus long-term depositors who receive a diluted share of the same fixed `amount`.

This is directly demonstrated by the existing test `emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value` [4](#0-3) , which shows the emissions amount split purely by each depositor's share of `total_asset_shares` at the moment of the call — regardless of how recently those shares were acquired.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);
