### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Enables Front-Run Sniping of Instant Share-Value Increases - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that instantly injects funds into a bank's liquidity vault and raises `bank.asset_share_value` pro-rata for all shares outstanding *at the moment of the call*, with no warmup period, vesting, or time-weighted distribution [1](#0-0) . Because deposits and withdrawals have no lockup and can be sequenced back-to-back (subject only to a post-withdraw health check), a bot can watch for a pending/anticipated emissions deposit, deposit a large amount into the same bank immediately beforehand to inflate its own share count, let the emissions deposit execute (raising `asset_share_value` for everyone including the attacker), and withdraw immediately afterward — capturing a slice of the injected value without ever having provided meaningful liquidity or duration exposure. This mirrors the reported `distributeDividends` sandwich pattern: sandwiching a deposit/withdraw around a value-distribution event to unfairly capture rewards.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, using whatever `total_asset_shares` exists at call time [2](#0-1) . There is no snapshot of shareholders as of some prior block, no minimum holding period, and no per-user accrual weighted by time-in-pool — the full amount is distributed instantaneously and proportionally to whoever holds shares in that instant.

`lending_account_deposit` has no cooldown/warmup: any signer can immediately mint shares via `BankAccountWrapper::deposit` [3](#0-2) . `lending_account_withdraw` likewise permits withdrawing back out immediately, subject only to a standard health check at the end of the instruction [4](#0-3) , not any time-based restriction tied to deposit recency.

Because `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is fully permissionless (any depositor with the funding tokens can call it) and its accounts (`bank`, `mint`, `emissions_funding_account`) are visible on-chain before finalization, an attacker capable of transaction ordering (e.g., via priority fees/Jito bundles, or simply co-locating their own deposit/withdraw ix around a known/expected emissions-funding transaction) can:
1. Deposit a large sum into the target bank (inflating their share of `total_asset_shares`).
2. Allow/trigger the `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call, which raises `asset_share_value` for all current shares, including the attacker's just-added shares.
3. Withdraw immediately, realizing gains proportional to their (temporary, inflated) share of the pool — diluting the return earned by genuine long-term depositors who were exposed to the bank's risk over time.

### Impact Explanation
Genuine depositors who bore actual duration/risk exposure in the bank receive a diluted share of externally-funded emissions/incentive deposits, while a sniping bot captures value it did not economically earn. This is a value-redirection issue: capital is transferred from legitimate long-term liquidity providers to the transient sniper. The magnitude scales with the size of the emissions deposit and the sniper's ability to temporarily dominate `total_asset_shares`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is constrained by the same practical factors the original report's resolution cites: the attacker needs available capital to temporarily dominate share count, must pay any relevant transaction/priority fees, and needs visibility/control over transaction ordering relative to the emissions-deposit call. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless and used by the protocol to inject "same-bank emissions directly into the liquidity vault" [5](#0-4) , so such calls are a known, recurring on-chain event type rather than a rare admin-only action, increasing the attacker's opportunities relative to the original report's scenario.

### Recommendation
Introduce a time-weighted or checkpoint-based distribution for `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (e.g., require a minimum holding duration for shares to qualify for the newly injected value, or distribute over a vesting/streaming window) rather than an instantaneous pro-rata re-valuation based on shares held at the exact moment of the call.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or anticipates) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call on bank `B`.
2. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` on `B` with a large amount, minting shares at the current `asset_share_value`.
3. The `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction executes, recomputing `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` using the now-inflated `total_asset_shares` that includes the attacker's deposit [6](#0-5) .
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) immediately, realizing the uplifted `asset_share_value` and exiting with a profit proportional to the injected emissions amount times their temporary share fraction, at negligible holding-time cost.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

    bank.update_bank_cache(&group)?;

    msg!(
        "Deposited {} same-bank emissions into liquidity vault",
        amount
    );

    Ok(())
}
