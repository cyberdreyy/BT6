### Title
Unfunded instant-withdraw claims pay out from underlyings earmarked for other claimants — (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
Analogous to a parser trusting a declared length larger than the actual buffer, `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` pays out the full declared receipt amount `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` without checking that the request was actually funded by `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`. The only guard in `_transferFundedClaim` protects `defaultRecoveryReserve` (zero pre-default), so any underlying held by the strategy — including funds already collected for other users' normal withdraws via `collectWithdrawFunds`, or borrower repayments awaiting `sendInterestAndDeposits` — can be spent by a claim that was never funded.

### Finding Description
`requestInstantWithdraw` burns CDO principal and mints a receipt while only incrementing `pendingInstantWithdraws`/`instantWithdrawsRequests`; no underlying moves into the strategy at request time (`IdleCreditVault.sol:356-375`). Funding arrives later when the CDO calls `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, which decrements `pendingInstantWithdraws` and pulls cash from the CDO (`IdleCreditVault.sol:398-403`). However `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:380-393`) has no epoch gate and no check against `pendingInstantWithdraws` or a per-request funded flag — it immediately burns the receipt and calls `_transferFundedClaim(_user, amount)`, which transfers underlying as long as `balance - defaultRecoveryReserve >= amount` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:897-907`). The "declared" claim size is therefore never bounded by the "actual" funded buffer, exactly the OOB-read shape of the reference report.

### Impact Explanation
In a running epoch where the strategy holds underlying collected for other users' withdraw receipts (via `collectWithdrawFunds`) or borrower repayments not yet forwarded to the CDO, an unprivileged user (KYC lender / tranche holder) requests an instant withdraw and claims before funding is collected. They are paid at par from other claimants' earmarked funds; when the legitimate claimants later call `claimWithdrawRequest`, their `_transferFundedClaim` reverts on insufficient balance — direct theft of funded withdraw amounts, quantified as `min(claimAmount, strategyBalance)`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires the strategy to hold underlying at claim time, which is the normal state between `collectWithdrawFunds`/`collectInstantWithdrawFunds` calls and subsequent user claims, and whenever borrower funds sit in the strategy before `sendInterestAndDeposits`. The attacker's only cost is depositing to obtain tranche tokens (KYC). Caveat: whether `IdleCDOEpochVariant.claimInstantWithdrawRequest` forwards claims unconditionally — or gates on its own funding check via `getInstantWithdrawFunds` — could not be fully verified in the available iterations; if the CDO enforces funding, the strategy-level hole is defense-in-depth only.

### Recommendation
In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, track per-request or aggregate funded instant withdrawals (e.g., a `fundedInstantWithdraws` counter incremented in `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`) and pay at most `min(instantWithdrawsRequests[_user], fundedInstantWithdraws)`, or revert until funded — mirroring the epoch gating already applied to normal receipts.
