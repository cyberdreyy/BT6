### Title
Stale unfunded instant-withdraw receipt is paid at par from pooled funded-withdraw reserves - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
Analogous to the Discourse stale-reset-link bug (an old credential stays valid after the state it was bound to has changed), an instant-withdraw receipt created in an earlier epoch remains payable at par in a later epoch even though the epoch that was supposed to fund it under-funded it. `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` pays the aggregate `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` from the vault's whole funded balance, while funding for that receipt is tracked only via the global `pendingInstantWithdraws` counter and per-epoch mappings that the claim path ignores.

### Finding Description
`requestInstantWithdraw` mints a 1:1 receipt and records it per epoch (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch`) while only bumping a single global `pendingInstantWithdraws`. Funding arrives through `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, which reduces `pendingInstantWithdraws` by whatever the borrower actually repaid at stopEpoch — a partial collection is valid (no revert if `_amount < pendingInstantWithdraws`).

`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` then does:

- `amount = instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` (aggregate across epochs)
- `_burn(_user, amount)`
- `_transferFundedClaim(_user, amount)`

`_transferFundedClaim` sends vault underlying while only sparing `defaultRecoveryReserve`. It does not check that this user's epoch was actually funded, nor does it touch `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` or `pendingInstantWithdraws`. The vault balance at that point legitimately contains underlying collected via `collectWithdrawFunds` for normal funded withdraw receipts of other users.

So a receipt whose funding was short in epoch E stays fully "valid" in epoch E+1 and can be redeemed against reserves belonging to unrelated funded claims — the stale-credential-survives-state-change pattern.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft / insolvency. If epoch E instant withdrawals are under-funded (borrower repays less than the instant bucket, e.g., a lossy stop), the remaining receipt holders keep a par-denominated claim. A claimant who waits until the vault later holds funded normal-withdraw proceeds can redeem their unfunded instant receipt at par, draining underlying reserved for other users' funded claims and leaving those claims unpayable. Loss equals the unfunded instant residual, bounded by the vault's funded balance.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an epoch where instant withdrawals are enabled and the borrower repays less than the pending instant basis while other funded reserves later accumulate in the vault — plausible in lossy stops or partial repayments. The per-epoch ledgers (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`) exist precisely to distinguish epochs, but the non-default claim path never uses them, so no guard stops the cross-epoch drain. Attacker only needs to be an ordinary LP requesting an instant withdraw.

### Recommendation
Track funded instant-withdraw balances per epoch (or haircut instant receipts when `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` under-funds the bucket, analogous to `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch` for normal receipts), and make `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` pay only against the funded portion of the requester's epoch, decrementing `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` accordingly.

### Proof of Concept
A Foundry fork test (extend `test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol` harness):

1. Enable instant withdrawals via `cdoEpoch.setInstantWithdrawParams(delay, aprDelta, false)`; attacker deposits AA and starts epoch E.
2. Attacker calls `requestInstantWithdraw(100e6)` via the CDO.
3. Stop epoch E with `stopEpochWithDuration` such that the borrower repays only part of the instant bucket; owner calls `collectInstantWithdrawFunds(60e6)` — `pendingInstantWithdraws == 40e6` remains.
4. Next epoch, an honest user's normal withdraw request gets funded via `collectWithdrawFunds`, so the vault holds underlying reserves; attacker does not claim yet.
5. Attacker calls `cdoEpoch.claimInstantWithdrawRequest()`; assert the vault transfers the full `instantWithdrawsRequests[attacker]` (100e6), consuming 40e6 of reserves earmarked for the honest funded claim, whose subsequent `claimWithdrawRequest` then reverts/underpays.
