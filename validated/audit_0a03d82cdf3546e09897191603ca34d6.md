### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows a flash-depositor to snipe pro-rata emissions rewards from existing depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
The Wheel bug's root cause is a design that locks in an outcome-determining ratio (stake share) *after* an unlimited, unprivileged accumulation window, letting an attacker who can freely add to the numerator right before the snapshot capture almost the entire payout. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` in marginfi has the same shape: it is fully permissionless, instantaneously converts an incoming token transfer into a pro-rata boost of `asset_share_value` based on `total_asset_shares` *at the instant the call executes*, and there is no minimum holding period between depositing into a bank and withdrawing again. An attacker can therefore inflate their share of the pool immediately before an emissions/incentive drop lands and cash out immediately after, capturing reward value that was intended for depositors who held their position over time.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented and implemented as permissionless: [1](#0-0) 

It computes the payout multiplier purely from the bank's current `total_asset_shares` at call time and applies it globally to `asset_share_value`: [2](#0-1) 

There is no time-weighting, vesting, or snapshot mechanism protecting this distribution — whoever holds shares at the moment the `amount` lands gets a proportional cut, exactly like the Wheel contract computing win probability from `stakeByPlayer` at the moment `startSession` executes. Because `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` impose no cooldown or lock-up between actions (deposits/repays skip the risk engine entirely, per the README: "Deposits and Repays require no Risk Engine check, as they can only improve health"), an attacker can: [3](#0-2) 

1. Observe (or predict, e.g., via the documented recurring "Wednesday" reward cadence for time-based campaigns, or by watching the mempool/queued transaction that funds `emissions_funding_account`) that a same-bank emissions deposit of value `X` is about to be submitted to a given bank.
2. Front-run it with a large `lending_account_deposit` into that same bank, inflating `total_asset_shares` in their favor relative to the pre-existing, long-term depositors.
3. Let the emissions deposit transaction execute — `asset_share_value` rises for the whole pool, and the attacker's newly-added shares capture a disproportionate fraction of `X` relative to their time actually at risk in the bank.
4. Immediately withdraw, realizing the captured reward with no meaningful holding period.

This diverts value that the funder (protocol/admin/community) intended for genuine long-term depositors to a flash-capital attacker, exactly mirroring how the Wheel attacker inflated `stakeByPlayer[msg.sender]` during the cooldown window to capture (near) 100% of a payout meant to be probabilistically shared.

### Impact Explanation
Any bank receiving periodic "same-bank emissions" incentives (a documented, intended usage pattern per `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md` and the Kamino integration guide's "we will distribute rewards... using a fair and proportional method") is exposed to reward-sniping by capital-rich actors who never intended to be genuine liquidity providers. This causes durable, direct financial harm to legitimate depositors (diluted rewards) and undermines incentive-alignment goals of the emissions program, with no compensating benefit to the protocol. The severity scales with the size of the funded emissions drop and the capital available to the attacker (potentially unbounded, subject only to bank `deposit_limit`).

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition — permissionless deposit/withdraw with no cooldown, combined with a permissionless, instant pro-rata reward mechanism — is present in the shipped code with no additional privilege required. Exploitation only requires transaction ordering control (bundling in the same transaction/atomic block as the emissions deposit, or fast front-running), which is realistic on Solana especially if the attacker can compose the flow into a single transaction alongside the (permissionless) `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call itself, since nothing prevents the attacker from being both the funder and depositor in one atomic transaction sequence, or piggy-backing on someone else's funding transaction landing in the same slot.

### Recommendation
- Require a minimum holding/vesting period (or time-weighted share accounting, as already partially implied by the "real-time pro-rata" continuous-emissions design described in `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`) before newly-deposited shares are eligible to receive `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` boosts.
- Alternatively, restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to a privileged/admin-controlled schedule combined with a deposit-lock or snapshot-based distribution (e.g., distribute based on shares held over the preceding window, not shares held at call time).
- Consider disallowing deposit-then-withdraw within the same transaction/slot as an emissions deposit, or applying a decayed vesting curve for newly added liquidity's share of a lump-sum reward.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has two long-term depositors together holding `total_asset_shares = S` worth `V` in underlying tokens.
2. Attacker observes a transaction funding `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=X)` for bank `B` (e.g., a scheduled admin/community incentive drop) about to land.
3. In the same transaction (or immediately prior, front-run), attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with amount `D >> V`, becoming the dominant shareholder of `B`.
4. The emissions deposit executes: [4](#0-3) 
`asset_share_value` rises uniformly; the attacker's shares (now `D/(D+S)` of the pool) receive `X * D/(D+S)` of the reward instead of the smaller share they would have earned had they been a genuine long-term holder.
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (no cooldown, deposits/withdraws have no lock) to realize the captured value, having held the position for a negligible amount of time.

**Uncertainty note:** I was not able to fully verify (within tool budget) whether `lending_account_withdraw` enforces any hidden same-block/same-slot restriction after a deposit, nor whether off-chain tooling enforces a minimum delay before executing scheduled `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` calls that would mitigate front-running in practice. Confirming these operational mitigations would require inspecting the withdraw instruction's account constraints and any off-chain scheduler code in full, which exceeded the available search budget.

    check!(

    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
