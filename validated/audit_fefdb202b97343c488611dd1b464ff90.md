The reported bug involves a threshold-gate flag (`migrationTime`, `successMigrated`) whose guard is bypassable because the function never re-verifies that the underlying vote-weight condition was actually met — it only checks derived state flags. The stacks-signer codebase in `stacks-signer/src/v0/signer.rs` and `stacks-signer/src/signerdb.rs` implements the structurally analogous action (crossing the pre-commit/signature weight threshold to sign or broadcast a block) with the threshold check performed directly and redundantly at every gate rather than relying on a state flag alone:

- `handle_block_pre_commit` recomputes `commit_weight`/`min_weight` from `NakamotoBlockHeader::compute_voting_weight_threshold` every time and only proceeds to sign when `min_weight <= commit_weight`, in addition to requiring `block_info.valid.unwrap_or(false)` and `block_info.signed_self.is_none()`. [1](#0-0) 
- `store_and_process_block_signature` similarly recomputes `total_signature_weight` from the actual stored signatures (each pubkey-recovered and verified) before allowing `mark_locally_accepted`/broadcast, rather than trusting a boolean.
