### Title
Unprivileged lockup owner can bind a lockup contract to a self-controlled fake "staking pool" whitelist, permanently diverting unvested tokens from Foundation clawback - ([File: lockup-factory/src/lib.rs])

### Summary
`LockupFactory::create` lets the caller (any account, not the Foundation) override the factory's `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` with an arbitrary account of their choosing. That attacker-chosen value is baked into the deployed `LockupContract`'s immutable `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` field, which is later trusted unconditionally by `select_staking_pool`/`on_whitelist_is_whitelisted` to vet staking pools. This breaks the custody binding the whitelist is supposed to enforce: "staking pool the lockup delegates to == a pool that guarantees recoverability of delegated (including unvested) tokens." An owner can point their own lockup at a whitelist contract they control that always answers `true`, then delegate/deposit unvested tokens to a fake "staking pool" contract they control that lies about balances, escaping the Foundation's `terminate_vesting` clawback.

### Finding Description
`LockupFactory::create` (`lockup-factory/src/lib.rs:107-166`) accepts an optional `whitelist_account_id` parameter from the caller with no restriction on who can call `create` or what account is supplied: [1](#0-0) 

This value — fully attacker-controlled — becomes the `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` field passed into the newly deployed lockup contract's `new()` constructor: [2](#0-1) 

The lockup contract stores this value at initialization time with no cross-check against a canonical/foundation-controlled whitelist account: [3](#0-2) 

`select_staking_pool`, the owner's method for choosing where to delegate lockup funds (including unvested/locked funds under a vesting schedule), relies exclusively on this attacker-supplied `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` to decide whether a staking pool account is trustworthy: [4](#0-3) 

and the callback blindly accepts whatever boolean the "whitelist" contract returns: [5](#0-4) 

The entire security model documented in `whitelist/README.md` depends on the whitelist being controlled by the NEAR Foundation, guaranteeing that any staking pool a lockup can delegate to "should guarantee that the delegated tokens can not be lost or locked" and can always be recovered back to the lockup: [6](#0-5) 

Because `create()` lets the lockup owner substitute this trusted whitelist with a whitelist contract they themselves deploy and control, the owner can get their own lockup to "whitelist" an arbitrary account they control as a legitimate staking pool. The owner can then deposit and stake unvested (locked, Foundation-clawback-eligible) balance into this fake pool via `deposit_to_staking_pool`/`stake` (`lockup/src/lib.rs`), which physically transfers NEAR out of the lockup account to the attacker-controlled "pool" account.

The equality that should hold is:
`unvested_amount clawback-recoverable by Foundation == unvested balance actually held/recoverable by the lockup`

Once tokens are pushed to a self-controlled fake pool, that equality breaks: `get_account_balance()` on the lockup no longer reflects the true location of funds, and the Foundation's termination flow depends entirely on querying the (attacker-controlled) staking pool contract for balances: [7](#0-6) 

A malicious "staking pool" contract can respond to `get_account_staked_balance` / `get_account_unstaked_balance` with `0` (or any understated figure), causing `termination_prepare_to_withdraw` to conclude there is nothing left to recover, while in reality the owner has already extracted or permanently controls the tokens through their fake pool contract.

### Impact Explanation
This is a Critical-severity finding: it allows locked/unvested tokens — funds that are explicitly reserved for the NEAR Foundation to claw back on `terminate_vesting` — to be released early and permanently to the (malicious) lockup owner, bypassing the vesting/termination schedule entirely. This matches the "locked or unvested tokens released early" and "funds permanently frozen/diverted from the intended custodian" impact classes: the Foundation's recoverable unvested balance no longer matches what the lockup can actually deliver, and the divergence is caused entirely by an unprivileged party (the lockup owner) at lockup-creation time, not by a Foundation/owner/multisig misconfiguration that the system is documented to prevent.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high for any account that intends to abuse its own lockup: `create()` on the factory can be called by anyone who attaches the minimum deposit, with no check that `whitelist_account_id` matches the Foundation's official whitelist, and no cross-check is later performed by the deployed lockup contract. Deploying a trivial "whitelist" contract (always returns `true` for `is_whitelisted`) and a trivial fake "staking pool" contract (accepts deposits, reports lower balances) requires no special privileges — only standard NEAR account/contract deployment capability, well within reach of any lockup owner with a vesting schedule they want to escape.

### Recommendation
- Remove the caller-supplied `whitelist_account_id` override in `LockupFactory::create` (`lockup-factory/src/lib.rs:115,129-133`), or restrict its use to Foundation-approved callers/values only; always use the factory's own `self.whitelist_account_id` for lockups that carry a vesting schedule / foundation account.
- In `LockupContract::new`, when a `vesting_schedule`/`foundation_account_id` is present, enforce that `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` equals a canonical, hardcoded (or Foundation-registered) whitelist account rather than accepting an arbitrary value supplied at construction time.
- Consider having `terminate_vesting`/`termination_prepare_to_withdraw` independently validate the staking pool used for delegation against the canonical whitelist at termination time as well, not just at `select_staking_pool` time.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker (a prospective lockup owner) deploys `EvilWhitelist`, a contract exposing `is_whitelisted(..)` that always returns `true`.
2. Attacker deploys `EvilPool`, a contract mimicking the staking pool interface (`deposit_and_stake`, `get_account_staked_balance`, `get_account_unstaked_balance`, `unstake`, `withdraw`) but that always reports `0` balances regardless of actual funds received, and never actually returns funds.
3. Attacker calls `LockupFactory::create(owner_account_id = attacker, ..., vesting_schedule = Some(...), whitelist_account_id = Some(EvilWhitelist))`, attaching ≥ `MIN_ATTACHED_BALANCE`. This deploys a lockup contract whose `staking_pool_whitelist_account_id` is `EvilWhitelist` instead of the Foundation's official whitelist (`lockup-factory/src/lib.rs:107-166`).
4. As owner, attacker calls `select_staking_pool(EvilPool)` on their lockup (`lockup/src/owner.rs:12-41`); `EvilWhitelist.is_whitelisted` returns `true`, so `on_whitelist_is_whitelisted` accepts `EvilPool` as the selected staking pool (`lockup/src/owner_callbacks.rs:6-25`).
5. Attacker calls `deposit_and_stake`/`stake` to move the full (including unvested) lockup balance to `EvilPool`.
6. NEAR Foundation later calls `terminate_vesting` (`lockup/src/foundation.rs:15-47`) and `termination_prepare_to_withdraw` (`lockup/src/foundation.rs:58-127`); these query `EvilPool.get_account_staked_balance`/`get_account_unstaked_balance`, which return `0`, so the Foundation's clawback flow concludes there is nothing to recover — while the tokens, in reality, sit permanently under the attacker's control in `EvilPool`.

    pub fn create(

            account_id.into()
        } else {
            self.whitelist_account_id.clone()

    #[init]
    pub fn new(
        owner_account_id: AccountId,
        lockup_duration: WrappedDuration,
        lockup_timestamp: Option<WrappedTimestamp>,
        transfers_information: TransfersInformation,
        vesting_schedule: Option<VestingScheduleOrHash>,
        release_duration: Option<WrappedDuration>,
        staking_pool_whitelist_account_id: AccountId,
        foundation_account_id: Option<AccountId>,
    ) -> Self {
        assert!(
            env::is_valid_account_id(owner_account_id.as_bytes()),
            "The account ID of the owner is invalid"
        );
        assert!(
            env::is_valid_account_id(staking_pool_whitelist_account_id.as_bytes()),
            "The staking pool whitelist account ID is invalid"
        );
        if let TransfersInformation::TransfersDisabled {
            transfer_poll_account_id,
        } = &transfers_information
        {
            assert!(
                env::is_valid_account_id(transfer_poll_account_id.as_bytes()),
                "The transfer poll account ID is invalid"
            );
        }
        let lockup_information = LockupInformation {
            lockup_amount: env::account_balance(),
            termination_withdrawn_tokens: 0,
            lockup_duration: lockup_duration.0,
            release_duration: release_duration.map(|d| d.0),
            lockup_timestamp: lockup_timestamp.map(|d| d.0),
            transfers_information,
        };
        let vesting_information = match vesting_schedule {
            None => {
                assert!(
                    foundation_account_id.is_none(),
                    "Foundation account can't be added without vesting schedule"
                );
                VestingInformation::None
            }
            Some(VestingScheduleOrHash::VestingHash(hash)) => VestingInformation::VestingHash(hash),
            Some(VestingScheduleOrHash::VestingSchedule(vs)) => {
                VestingInformation::VestingSchedule(vs)
            }
        };
        assert!(
            vesting_information == VestingInformation::None ||
                env::is_valid_account_id(foundation_account_id.as_ref().unwrap().as_bytes()),
            "Foundation account should be added for vesting schedule"
        );

        Self {
            owner_account_id,
            lockup_information,
            vesting_information,
            staking_information: None,
            staking_pool_whitelist_account_id,
            foundation_account_id,
        }
    }

    pub fn on_whitelist_is_whitelisted(
        &mut self,
        #[callback] is_whitelisted: bool,
        staking_pool_account_id: AccountId,
    ) -> bool {
        assert_self();

            is_whitelisted,
            "The given staking pool account ID is not whitelisted"

    pub fn termination_prepare_to_withdraw(&mut self) -> Promise {
        self.assert_called_by_foundation();
        self.assert_staking_pool_is_idle();

        let status = self.get_termination_status();

        match status {
            None => {
                env::panic(b"There is no termination in progress");
            }
            Some(TerminationStatus::UnstakingInProgress)
            | Some(TerminationStatus::WithdrawingFromStakingPoolInProgress)
            | Some(TerminationStatus::WithdrawingFromAccountInProgress) => {
                env::panic(b"Another transaction is already in progress.");
            }
            Some(TerminationStatus::ReadyToWithdraw) => {
                env::panic(b"The account is ready to withdraw unvested balance.")
            }
            Some(TerminationStatus::VestingTerminatedWithDeficit) => {
                // Need to unstake
                self.set_termination_status(TerminationStatus::UnstakingInProgress);
                self.set_staking_pool_status(TransactionStatus::Busy);
                env::log(b"Termination Step: Going to unstake everything from the staking pool");

                ext_staking_pool::get_account_staked_balance(
                    env::current_account_id(),
                    &self
                        .staking_information
                        .as_ref()
                        .unwrap()
                        .staking_pool_account_id,
                    NO_DEPOSIT,
                    gas::staking_pool::GET_ACCOUNT_STAKED_BALANCE,
                )
                .then(
                    ext_self_foundation::on_get_account_staked_balance_to_unstake(
                        &env::current_account_id(),
                        NO_DEPOSIT,
                        gas::foundation_callbacks::ON_GET_ACCOUNT_STAKED_BALANCE_TO_UNSTAKE,
                    ),
                )
            }
            Some(TerminationStatus::EverythingUnstaked) => {
                // Need to withdraw everything
                self.set_termination_status(
                    TerminationStatus::WithdrawingFromStakingPoolInProgress,
                );
                self.set_staking_pool_status(TransactionStatus::Busy);
                env::log(b"Termination Step: Going to withdraw everything from the staking pool");

                ext_staking_pool::get_account_unstaked_balance(
                    env::current_account_id(),
                    &self
                        .staking_information
                        .as_ref()
                        .unwrap()
                        .staking_pool_account_id,
                    NO_DEPOSIT,
                    gas::staking_pool::GET_ACCOUNT_UNSTAKED_BALANCE,
                )
                .then(
                    ext_self_foundation::on_get_account_unstaked_balance_to_withdraw(
                        &env::current_account_id(),
                        NO_DEPOSIT,
                        gas::foundation_callbacks::ON_GET_ACCOUNT_UNSTAKED_BALANCE_TO_WITHDRAW,
                    ),
                )
            }
        }
    }
