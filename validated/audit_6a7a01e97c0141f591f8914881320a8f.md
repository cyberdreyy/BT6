### Title
Transient ERC4626 share-price inflation via donation poisons `totalInterestDueNow` and mints phantom yield into tranche prices - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol)

### Summary
SSRF is a trusted component issuing a request to an attacker-influenced endpoint and acting on the response. The analog here is `stopEpoch` pricing the entire epoch off `IProgrammableBorrower.totalInterestDueNow()`, which reads the external ERC4626 vault's live `convertToAssets` at a single point in time (`_currentVaultAssets`, `ProgrammableBorrower.sol:546-549`). An unprivileged attacker who is also a depositor of that ERC4626 vault can transiently inflate `convertToAssets` (donation / direct asset transfer into a vault whose `totalAssets` counts raw balance, or share-price manipulation in the underlying markets), causing `_vaultNetInterest` to report phantom `vaultInterest` (`ProgrammableBorrower.sol:337-347`). The CDO then treats that phantom PnL as real epoch yield, minting strategy tokens / tranche shares at an inflated price that the attacker redeems for real underlying before the price reverts.

### Finding Description
In minted-interest mode, `IdleCDOEpochVariant.stopEpoch` calls `totalInterestDueNow()` (via `_calcInterestWithApr`/borrower-facing reads), then `onStopEpoch`, then `getFundsFromBorrower` pulls only the cash shortfall while `mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` mints the reported interest into `getContractValue` (`IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:408-430`). `totalInterestDueNow` (`ProgrammableBorrower.sol:330-334`) equals `vaultInterest + borrowerInterestAccruedNow + bufferInterest - loss`, where `vaultInterest` is `convertToAssets(shares) + epochWithdrawnFromVault - epochStartVaultAssets - epochDepositedToVault + bufferedVaultDelta`. Nothing snapshots or smooths `convertToAssets`; the epoch baseline `epochStartVaultAssets` is fixed at `onStartEpoch` (`ProgrammableBorrower.sol:214-219`), so a same-transaction/temporary inflation of the share price at stop time flows 1:1 into `_grossInterest` and therefore into `_updateAccounting` tranche prices. The `try vault.withdraw` guard only catches withdrawal failure for the shortfall amount, not valuation manipulation, and `setVault`/`nonReentrant`/KYC checks do not constrain a legitimate co-depositor of the vault.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft / solvency break: phantom `vaultInterest` mints real claim value. An attacker holding AA (or BB) tranche tokens deposits before the epoch, donates X underlying into the vault just before `stopEpoch` (manager-triggered, publicly callable at `epochEndDate`), gets tranche prices uplifted by ~X routed through `_updateAccounting`, withdraws at the inflated price, then removes the donation. Other tranche holders and pending withdraw receipts absorb the shortfall; the pool's solvency invariant (sum of claims ≤ real assets) is broken by the minted-but-nonexistent yield. Loss equals the injected donation amount minus what the attacker recovers of it from the vault.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium: requires (a) minted-interest programmable-borrower mode (the deployed Gauntlet USDC configuration), (b) an ERC4626 vault whose `convertToAssets` is manipulable by a co-depositor — true for naive ERC4626s and vaults with thin underlying markets; MetaMorpho-style vaults whose `totalAssets` derives from market positions rather than raw balance resist direct donation but remain manipulable through market-side price moves. The attacker needs only to be a tranche holder and vault user — both explicitly unprivileged roles — and must sequence a donation around the honest manager's `stopEpoch` call (MEV/backrun at `epochEndDate`).

### Recommendation
Do not price an epoch from a single `convertToAssets` read. Realize vault PnL only when assets are actually withdrawn/redeemed (account `epochWithdrawnFromVault` at redemption-time asset amounts only), or bound `vaultInterest` by the net of realized withdrawals minus `epochDepositedToVault`/`epochStartVaultAssets` cash flows and treat the residual share position at cost basis until redeemed. Alternatively, use a manipulation-resistant valuation (e.g., price-per-share averaged over the epoch or a `maxWithdraw`-style redeemable floor) and skim/ignore direct donations.

### Proof of Concept

```solidity
// test/foundry/DonationInflationPoC.t.sol — fork: mainnet, programmable-borrower
// credit vault with minted interest (see test/foundry/ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol setup)
function testDonationInflatesEpochInterestAndSteals() external {
  _setUpProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault(GAUNTLET_FORK_BLOCK, GAUNTLET_USDC_PRIME);
  uint256 amount = 10_000 * oneScale;
  vm.prank(owner);
  cdoEpoch.setIsInterestMinted(true);

  // Honest depositor funds the pool
  idleCDO.depositAA(amount);           // test contract = honest LP
  // Attacker LP joins too
  deal(USDC, attacker, amount, true);
  vm.startPrank(attacker);
  underlying.approve(address(idleCDO), amount);
  idleCDO.depositAA(amount);
  vm.stopPrank();
  _startEpochAndCheckPrices(0);

  // Replace/assume vault V where convertToAssets counts balanceOf(V)
  // (MockStopEpochLiquidityVault in ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol:24-60
  //  already satisfies this — deposit path uses balanceOf-based share math)
  uint256 attackShares = aaTranche.balanceOf(attacker);
  uint256 priceBefore = cdoEpoch.virtualPrice(address(aaTranche));

  vm.warp(cdoEpoch.epochEndDate() + 1);
  // Attacker front-runs manager stopEpoch: donate D underlying to the vault
  uint256 donation = 500 * oneScale;
  deal(USDC, attacker, donation, true);
  vm.prank(attacker);
  underlying.transfer(address(vault), donation);   // inflates convertToAssets

  vm.prank(manager);
  cdoEpoch.stopEpoch(0, 0);                        // mints phantom _grossInterest

  uint256 priceAfter = cdoEpoch.virtualPrice(address(aaTranche));
  assertGt(priceAfter, priceBefore, "phantom donation minted into AA price");

  // Attacker redeems at inflated price, then pulls the donation back out of V
  vm.prank(attacker);
  idleCDO.withdrawAA(attackShares);                // receives > fair share
  // Post-condition: remaining LPs / pending receipts are short by ~donation
  // once vault.convertToAssets reverts to real value.
}
```
Key trace: `vault.convertToAssets` ↑ ⇒ `totalInterestDueNow` ↑ ⇒ `mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` mints nonexistent yield ⇒ `_updateAccounting` raises tranche price ⇒ attacker `withdrawAA` extracts real underlying belonging to other LPs.
