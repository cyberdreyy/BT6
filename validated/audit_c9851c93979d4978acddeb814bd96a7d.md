The GHSA-4h98-2769-gh6h bug class requires a single `bytes`-argument recovery function that accepts *both* the traditional 65-byte `r||s||v` format *and* the EIP-2098 64-byte compact `r||vs` format, combined with replay protection keyed on the signature bytes themselves (rather than a nonce or the signed message). In this codebase:

- `RecoveredActorId::recover_k1` strictly requires a 65-byte signature (`signature.len() != 65` is rejected) and further enforces EIP-2 low-`s` canonicalization, rejecting malleable high-`s` forms rather than accepting an alternate compact encoding. [1](#0-0) 
- `Eip8130Signed::recover_eoa_sender_with` also hard-requires exactly 65 bytes with `v ∈ {27,28}` and explicitly documents rejecting non-canonical encodings to prevent txid malleability.
