### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` enables JIT-deposit MEV theft of reward top-ups from long-term depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a fully permissionless instruction that transfers same-mint tokens into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and raises `asset_share_value` in proportion to `total_asset_shares` at the instant the instruction executes [1](#0-0) . Because the value increase is distributed strictly pro-rata to shares held at call time — with no snapshotting, vesting, or minimum holding period — an attacker can front-run a known/predictable emissions-deposit transaction with a large `lending_account_deposit`, capture a disproportionate share of the value increase, then immediately `lending_account_withdraw`, exactly mirroring the Sturdy `distributeYield` JIT-deposit MEV attack.

### Finding Description
The instruction is declared permissionless in both the patch notes and the on-chain comment: "Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault, increasing depositor value through asset share value" [2](#0-1) , and is exposed via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` in `lib.rs` with the doc comment "(permissionless) Deposit same-bank emissions directly into liquidity vault and increase depositors' value via `asset_share_value`" [3](#0-2) .

The core mechanics:
1. `total_asset_shares` is read at call time [4](#0-3) .
2. `amount` tokens are transferred into the liquidity vault [5](#0-4) .
3. `asset_share_value` is recomputed as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` [6](#0-5) .

Every existing shareholder's balance increases proportionally to the shares they hold *at that exact instant*, regardless of how long they have held those shares. There is no cooldown, minimum deposit duration, or time-weighted accounting mechanism anywhere in the deposit/withdraw path that would prevent a same-block or same-transaction deposit-withdraw sandwich around this call (confirmed by the absence of any such controls in the codebase, and by the test suite's own `assertSameBankDeposit` helper explicitly modeling the "before deposit → deposit → after" pattern) [7](#0-6) .

The Sturdy `distributeYield` finding describes exactly this pattern: watch the mempool (or predict) an admin/protocol yield-distribution call, JIT-deposit a large stake immediately before it, capture a share of the distributed value proportional to that stake, then withdraw immediately after — receiving yield the attacker did nothing to earn. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` reproduces this mechanism precisely, with the added aggravation that the instruction is *permissionless*, so the "victim" transaction (whoever funds emissions — protocol ops, an integrator sweeping harvested rewards, or another user) is a normal, predictable/observable public transaction with no special submission privacy.

### Impact Explanation
Any depositor who front-runs (or otherwise times their deposit immediately before) a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call, and withdraws immediately after, captures a share of the deposited emissions/rewards proportional to their JIT stake size — value that rightfully belongs to depositors who held their position through the accrual period. This is a durable value-redirection: legitimate long-term depositors receive a diluted share of intended rewards, while the JIT attacker extracts value with no capital lock-up or economic contribution (utilization/borrowing) to the bank. Depending on bank deposit-limit headroom and the size of the emissions deposit, the attacker can capture a very large fraction of the distributed amount by temporarily inflating `total_asset_shares` right before the call.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is permissionless and can be called by anyone funding the transfer, so exploitation does not require compromising an admin key — it only requires observing (or anticipating) when such a call will land (e.g. periodic reward sweeps such as Kamino/Juplend/Drift harvested rewards being reinjected via same-mint emissions deposits, or scheduled protocol top-ups). On Solana, front-running/ordering guarantees are weaker than Ethereum MEV, but leader/validator-level transaction ordering influence, or simply racing with a bot that submits both the JIT deposit and withdrawal around a known/predictable emissions-deposit schedule, is a realistic attack surface, especially since deposit/withdraw carry no cooldown.

### Recommendation
Snapshot depositor shares prior to the emissions deposit (e.g., require a minimum holding duration, or distribute emissions based on time-weighted average shares since the last emissions deposit) rather than instantaneous share balance. Alternatively, apply the value increase gradually over time (streaming it into `asset_share_value` similar to interest accrual) rather than as an instantaneous lump-sum bump, and/or impose a deposit cooldown before newly deposited shares become eligible for a pro-rata cut of same-block emissions deposits.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank X has `total_asset_shares` S held by long-term depositors, with `asset_share_value` V.
2. Attacker observes (or predicts) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` call for bank X (e.g., a routine reward sweep).
3. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large amount `D`, minting `D/V` new shares just before the emissions-deposit transaction lands, making `total_asset_shares' = S + D/V`.
4. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` executes: `asset_share_value` increases from V to `V' = (S*V + D + amount) / (S + D/V)` [6](#0-5) .
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) for `D/V * V'`, netting a share of `amount` proportional to `D/(S*V+D)`, despite never being at risk or contributing to utilization.
6. Long-term depositors receive a correspondingly smaller share of `amount` than if the attacker's transient deposit had not been present.

```rust
/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
```

function assertSameBankDeposit(

  const totalDeposited =

  );
  // Should be roughly equal. If at all interest accrual happens, time barely passes between
  // this and the last one.
  assert.approximately(
    wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(shareValueAfter).toNumber(),
    beforeVal * (1 + emissionsDepositAmount / totalDeposited),
    beforeVal * 10 ** -10,
  );
  assert.equal(
    liquidityVaultAfter - liquidityVaultBefore,
    emissionsDepositAmount,
  );
}
