### Title
Post-default withdraw requests drain the shared `defaultRecoveryReserve`, permanently freezing defaulted-epoch claimants - (contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`IdleCreditVault` pays post-default withdraw receipts (`postDefaultRequests`) and defaulted-epoch receipts (`_claimDefaultedWithdrawRequest`, `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`) from the same `defaultRecoveryReserve`. Post-default receipts are minted and paid 1:1 from that reserve while bringing in zero new underlying. Any tranche-token holder can therefore front-run defaulted claimants after `finalizeDefault`, drain the reserve, and leave honest default-epoch claims permanently underfunded — the solid-state analog of dereferencing a stale/freed backing object (the reserve already consumed by an earlier claim).

### Finding Description
The kernel bug is a use-after-free: `sk_psock_verdict_data_ready()` dereferences a stale `sk->sk_socket` pointer whose backing object was freed by `sock_orphan()` during an RCU grace period, because the caller held a refcount on the peer `sock` but not on the `socket` it dereferenced. The analog here is a stale backing reference in value terms: `defaultRecoveryReserve` is the single backing pool that every recovery claim dereferences, but the protocol lets new claims be created *after* the reserve was sized and finalized.

Relevant code in `contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol`:

- `requestWithdraw` post-default branch (lines 247-257): after `defaultRecoveryFinalized`, once a user has cleared prior receipts, the CDO passes an "already-haircut" `_amount`, and the vault mints strategy-token receipts 1:1 (`_mint(_user, _amount)`) and records `postDefaultRequests[_user] = _amount`. No underlying enters the vault in this path.
- `claimWithdrawRequest` (lines 301-314) routes to `_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` (lines 760-767), which pays `amount` 1:1 via `_transferDefaultRecovery` — consuming `defaultRecoveryReserve` (lines 912-917: `defaultRecoveryReserve -= _amount`).
- Defaulted-epoch receipts are paid from the *same* reserve: `_claimDefaultedWithdrawRequest` (lines 772-784) and `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` (lines 842-856) both call `_transferDefaultRecovery`.
- The reserve is sized once at `finalizeDefault` from `recovered` funds intended to cover `defaultPendingClaimBasis` at `defaultRecoveryPrice`. Post-default claims did not exist at sizing time, so every post-default payout dilutes the haircut that defaulted claimants were guaranteed.

The broken invariant is "one receipt one payout at the finalized recovery ratio": the finalized `defaultRecoveryPrice` is only honored for whoever claims first. Once post-default claims consume enough reserve, remaining defaulted claims revert or pay nothing — a stale claim dereferencing a consumed backing pool.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing / effective theft of defaulted-epoch recovery. Example: `finalizeDefault` sets `defaultRecoveryReserve = 70` for defaulted basis 100 at price 0.7. An attacker holding AA tranche tokens worth 40 post-haircut calls `requestWithdraw` post-default, gets `postDefaultRequests[attacker] = 40`, claims 40 from the reserve, and only 30 remains for honest defaulted claimants owed 70. The attacker's receipt was backed only by burned CDO strategy tokens priced post-default — no new underlying — so the 40 came directly out of defaulted claimants' recovery. Claims are processed first-come-first-served, so late claimants are permanently frozen (the reserve accounting cannot be topped up; `defaulted` blocks new deposits).

### Likelihood Explanation
- Attacker profile allowed by the threat model: any tranche-token holder; no privileged role needed. Attacker just needs AA/BB tranche tokens acquired before or at default and must have no outstanding unfunded receipts (`requestWithdraw` post-default reverts if `_hasWithdrawRequest`, `instantWithdrawsRequests`, or `postDefaultRequests` are non-zero — lines 247-251 — but a holder who never requested withdraw, or who claimed their funded receipt first per `testPostDefaultWithdrawRequiresClaimingOpenPriorReceipt`, qualifies).
- No guard stops it: `claimWithdrawRequest` explicitly prefers post-default receipts (`_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` runs first, lines 305-307); `_transferDefaultRecovery` has no cap tying post-default payouts to a separately funded bucket.
- Sequencing: attack is only executable in the `defaulted`/`finalized` epoch phase, and requires the reserve to exceed the attacker's haircut receipt. Racing honest claimants is feasible since both flows are permissionless.

Residual uncertainty: I verified the shared-reserve mechanics in `IdleCreditVault.sol` but could not read `finalizeDefault`/`_handleBorrowerDefault` in `IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol` within available iterations to confirm whether the reserve is intentionally over-provisioned for post-default claims or whether `IdleCDO.requestWithdraw` blocks post-default calls. From the code read, no such provisioning exists — post-default requests only burn/mint strategy tokens — so the dilution stands.

### Recommendation
Segregate the backing pools, mirroring the fix in the kernel (fetch under RCU / validate the object still exists):

1. Track a separate `postDefaultReserve` funded when post-default requests are created — e.g., require the CDO to escrow the underlying the haircut implies, or pay post-default claims only from new borrower repayments received after finalization.
2. Alternatively, cap post-default payouts pro-rata: maintain `postDefaultTotal` and pay post-default claims at `amount * reserveForPostDefault / postDefaultTotal`, reserving `defaultRecoveryReserve` exclusively for defaulted-epoch claims until each defaulted epoch's claims are exhausted.
3. At minimum, document and enforce a global invariant: `sum(postDefaultRequests)` never reduces the reserve below `defaultPendingClaimBasis * defaultRecoveryPrice / RECOVERY_FULL` still outstanding — revert post-default `requestWithdraw` once headroom is exhausted.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork PoC sketch (USDC-like underlying, mirrors `testFinalizeDefaultKeepsOldFundedReceiptAtPar` and `testPostDefaultWithdrawRequiresClaimingOpenPriorReceipt` in `test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol`):

```solidity
function testPostDefaultClaimsDrainRecoveryReserve() external {
    address honest = makeAddr("defaulted-claimant");
    address attacker = makeAddr("post-default-drainer");
    uint256 amount = 10_000 * ONE_SCALE;
    uint256 recoveryRatio = 7e17;

    // Honest user deposits and requests withdraw in epoch 0.
    _depositWithUser(honest, amount, true);
    vm.prank(honest);
    uint256 honestBasis = cdoEpoch.requestWithdraw(0, address(AAtranche));

    // Attacker holds AA tranches but makes NO withdraw request pre-default.
    _depositWithUser(attacker, amount, true);

    // Epoch runs, borrower defaults (returns 0 at stopEpoch).
    _startEpochAndCheckPrices(0);
    _stopEpochAndCheckPrices(0, initialProvidedApr, 0);
    _checkDefault();

    // Finalize: reserve sized only for defaulted basis at 70% recovery.
    IdleCreditVault vault = IdleCreditVault(address(strategy));
    uint256 totalBasis = cdoEpoch.getContractValue() + cdoEpoch.expectedEpochInterest()
        - cdoEpoch.pendingWithdrawFees() + vault.defaultPendingClaimBasis();
    uint256 recovered = totalBasis * recoveryRatio / ONE_TRANCHE;
    deal(defaultUnderlying, manager, recovered);
    vm.startPrank(manager);
    IERC20Detailed(defaultUnderlying).approve(address(strategy), recovered);
    cdoEpoch.finalizeDefault(recovered, manager);
    vm.stopPrank();

    // Attacker creates a post-default receipt (haircut applied via virtualPrice)
    // and claims it 1:1 from the reserve BEFORE the honest defaulted claimant.
    uint256 reservePre = vault.defaultRecoveryReserve();
    vm.startPrank(attacker);
    uint256 attackerReceipt = cdoEpoch.requestWithdraw(0, address(AAtranche));
    cdoEpoch.claimWithdrawRequest();
    vm.stopPrank();
    assertEq(vault.defaultRecoveryReserve(), reservePre - attackerReceipt);

    // Honest defaulted claimant is now shorted: their payout
    // honestBasis * defaultRecoveryPrice / RECOVERY_FULL no longer fits the reserve,
    // or the reserve is depleted before other defaulted claims are paid.
    uint256 honestBalPre = IERC20Detailed(defaultUnderlying).balanceOf(honest);
    vm.prank(honest);
    cdoEpoch.claimWithdrawRequest();
    assertLt(
        IERC20Detailed(defaultUnderlying).balanceOf(honest) - honestBalPre,
        honestBasis * vault.defaultRecoveryPrice() / vault.RECOVERY_FULL(),
        "defaulted claimant diluted by post-default drain"
    );
}
```

Expected result: `attacker` withdraws underlying at par from a reserve funded solely by `recovered`, and `honest`'s defaulted claim receives strictly less than the finalized recovery ratio — or reverts if the reserve is exhausted — demonstrating theft/permanent freezing of unclaimed default recovery.
