### Title
Instant-withdraw receipts from prior epochs are excluded from default recovery basis, permanently freezing or draining recovery funds — (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`defaultPendingClaimBasis()` only counts `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` — receipts recorded in the *current* epoch. Instant-withdraw receipts requested in an earlier epoch that were never funded (because `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` pulled less than `pendingInstantWithdraws`) carry over in `instantWithdrawsRequests`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` but are invisible to the recovery accounting. Analogous to the CVE's heap over-read (reading data outside the intended bounds), the strategy reads only the current-epoch bucket while the actual claim set spans multiple epochs. Recovery price is then computed over an understated basis, so early claimants are overpaid and the reserve is drained, while holders of pre-default-epoch receipts can never claim — their tokens are neither cleared by `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` (which only reads `defaultRecoveryEpoch`) nor payable via `_transferFundedClaim` (the reserve guard reverts).

### Finding Description
- `requestInstantWithdraw` records basis per request epoch: `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][currentEpoch]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[currentEpoch]` (lines 367–372). `epochNumber` increments on `deposit()` during a running epoch (lines 607–611).
- If `stopEpoch` collects less than `pendingInstantWithdraws` via `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, the remainder persists into the next epoch(s) (lines 398–403). Those old receipts stay in `instantWithdrawsRequests[user]`.
- On default, `finalizeDefaultRecovery` → `defaultPendingClaimBasis` adds only `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` when `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` (lines 644–649). `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve` likewise only treats the current-epoch basis minus pending as prefunded (lines 716–723). Old-epoch receipt basis is omitted from `totalBasis`, inflating `recoveryPrice` (line 688).
- `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` only clears `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]` (lines 843–844). An old-epoch receipt's remainder stays in `instantWithdrawsRequests`, and the subsequent par-value claim path hits the `defaultRecoveryReserve` guard in `_transferFundedClaim` (lines 899–905), reverting forever.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft + permanent freezing: each early default claimant is paid `claimBasis * recoveryPrice` where `recoveryPrice` is inflated by the omitted old-epoch basis, draining `defaultRecoveryReserve`; holders of receipts from epochs before `defaultRecoveryEpoch` can never withdraw — their claim either burns their receipt at the haircut price only for the current-epoch portion, and the remainder reverts in `_transferFundedClaim`. Loss equals the full unfunded pre-epoch instant basis, split between overpaid claimants and frozen users.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an unprivileged lender to have an instant-withdraw receipt left unfunded through at least one epoch boundary, then borrower default and `finalizeDefaultRecovery`. Underfunding is a normal outcome whenever the CDO's available liquidity is short at `stopEpoch` (no privileged misbehavior needed — the queue just isn't fully filled). Any honest manager can then trigger `finalizeDefault`/`finalizeDefaultRecovery`, crystallizing the mispricing.

### Recommendation
Track total outstanding instant receipt basis across all epochs (e.g., a global `instantWithdrawClaimsTotal` incremented in `requestInstantWithdraw` and decremented on claim/collect), and use it in `defaultPendingClaimBasis` and `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve` instead of `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]`. Also make `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` clear a user's full `instantWithdrawsRequests` remainder at the recovery price, not just the default-epoch bucket.
