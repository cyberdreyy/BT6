## Answer

No vulnerability found for this question.

### Reasoning

The premise requires an **unprivileged attacker** to call "an owner method" while `vesting_information` is `Terminating`. This is not reachable:

1. Every owner-only method (`select_staking_pool`, `unselect_staking_pool`, `refresh_staking_pool_balance`, `withdraw_all_from_staking_pool`, `unstake_all`, etc.) begins with `self.assert_owner()`, which checks `env::predecessor_account_id() == self.owner_account_id` and panics immediately if it doesn't match — before any promise is scheduled or gas-dependent state change occurs. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

2. The methods that actually drive the `Terminating` state machine (`terminate_vesting`, `termination_prepare_to_withdraw`, `termination_withdraw`) are **foundation-only** methods gated by `self.assert_called_by_foundation()`, which likewise checks predecessor identity synchronously before doing anything else. [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

3. All owner methods additionally call `self.assert_no_termination()`, which panics outright if `vesting_information` is `Terminating` — so even the legitimate owner cannot invoke these methods during termination, regardless of gas. [5](#0-4) 

4. The callback functions that actually read/write `termination_withdrawn_tokens` / `TerminationStatus` (e.g. `on_get_account_staked_balance_to_unstake`, `on_staking_pool_unstake_for_termination`, `on_withdraw_unvested_amount`) are all guarded by `assert_self()`, meaning they can only be invoked by the contract itself as the result of an already-scheduled promise — an outside caller (including the attacker) cannot invoke them directly no matter how much or how little gas they attach. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

Since an unprivileged attacker's transaction is rejected synchronously by `assert_owner` / `assert_called_by_foundation` / `assert_self()` before any promise chain begins, the "insufficient prepaid gas" scenario described in the question (attaching just enough gas to schedule the chain but not to complete it) is not attacker-reachable at all — that failure mode could only be self-inflicted by the legitimate owner or the NEAR Foundation, both of which are explicitly out of scope for this attacker model. The gas constants in `lockup/src/gas.rs` are irrelevant to an unprivileged caller because the call never gets past the identity assertions to reach a gas-dependent promise. [8](#0-7) 

No divergence in `termination_withdrawn_tokens`, `vesting_information`, or any balance accounting is reachable by an unprivileged attacker through this path.
