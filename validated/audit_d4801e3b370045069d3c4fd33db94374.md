The reported issue is specific to a DeFi AMM swap-based gas payment mechanism (UniswapV2 price quoting followed by execution without slippage protection) in the ZetaChain node's `x/crosschain/keeper/gas_payment.go`. This bug class does not have a meaningful analog in `agave`, which is a Solana validator client. Solana's transaction fee calculation is deterministic — fees are computed directly from `lamports_per_signature` (blockhash-queue based) and prioritization fees are user-specified compute-unit-price fields in the transaction itself, not derived from a market price quote susceptible to slippage or front-running between a "quote" step and an "execute" step, as seen in `svm/src/transaction_processor.rs` (`validate_transaction_fee_payer`) and `fee/src/lib.rs` (`calculate_fee_details`).
