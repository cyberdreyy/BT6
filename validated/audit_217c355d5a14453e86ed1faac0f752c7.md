### Title
Front-runnable `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets an attacker mint shares before a share-value-boosting emissions injection and skim value from existing depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that transfers tokens into a bank's liquidity vault and immediately recomputes `asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, raising the value of every existing share in that bank. Because the instruction (and its `amount`) is visible in the mempool before landing, any user can deposit into the bank right before it executes to mint shares at the pre-injection (lower) share value, then withdraw immediately after to redeem at the post-injection (higher) share value, capturing a pro-rata share of the injected emissions that should have accrued only to depositors who were in the bank before the injection.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share value from the pre-transfer `total_asset_shares` and the freshly added `amount`: [1](#0-0) 

This mirrors the root cause in the external report: a permissionless/administrative action that adds value to a shared pool and instantly re-prices outstanding shares (`_totalAssets += _lpAmtOut` in `AutoCompoundingPodLp::_processRewardsToPodLp`). In both cases:
- The value injection is atomic and externally observable before it lands (mempool visibility).
- Share value is computed as `(existing pool value + injected value) / existing shares`, so whoever holds shares at the moment of injection captures a proportional cut of the injected value, regardless of how briefly they have held those shares.
- There is no minimum holding period, cooldown, or deposit-lockup tied to the emissions-deposit instruction that would prevent same-block/adjacent-transaction deposit-then-withdraw around it.

Because `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly designed to be **permissionless** (`/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault, increasing depositor value through asset share value.`), any address — including the emissions funder itself acting maliciously, or an MEV searcher watching the mempool — can sandwich the call with `lending_account_deposit` immediately before and `lending_account_withdraw` immediately after, both of which are unprivileged user operations available to any `MarginfiAccount` authority.

### Impact Explanation
A front-runner can deposit right before an emissions injection and withdraw right after, extracting value that should belong to the depositors who supplied capital to the bank before the emissions arrived. This is a direct value-redirection/theft of profit among unprivileged depositors sharing the same bank, financially analogous to the M-15 finding: legitimate depositors are diluted out of yield that is rightfully theirs, and an opportunistic actor captures it without taking on any of the underlying risk or duration exposure that the honest depositors held.

### Likelihood Explanation
The likelihood depends on: (1) emissions-deposit transactions being large enough relative to `total_asset_shares` to produce a share-value bump worth front-running after paying transaction costs, and (2) the front-runner being able to see the pending transaction (mempool visibility) and land a deposit/withdraw pair around it — both of which are standard, low-cost, unprivileged capabilities (any signer can call `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw`, and there is no cooldown enforced between them). No special role or oracle manipulation is required, only ordinary transaction ordering, which is realistic on Solana especially via priority fees/leader-adjacent submission.

### Recommendation
Mitigate the sandwich by decoupling minted-share eligibility from instantaneous share-value jumps, e.g.:
- Introduce a minimum holding period or deposit/withdraw cooldown per bank/account so that a deposit cannot be withdrawn in the same or an adjacent slot/transaction.
- Stream/vest emissions deposits into `asset_share_value` gradually (e.g., over a time window) rather than crediting the full amount instantly, similar to how interest is accrued via `accrue_interest` over `time_delta` rather than all at once.
- Alternatively, require an admin-controlled or time-locked emissions schedule instead of an instantaneous, permissionless lump-sum injection, or add a pause on deposits for the bank immediately surrounding known/scheduled emissions deposits.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has `total_asset_shares = S` and `asset_share_value = v`, so `total_assets = S*v`.
2. Attacker observes a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(bank=B, amount=A)` transaction in the mempool.
3. Attacker submits `lending_account_deposit(bank=B, amount=D)` with higher priority fee so it lands first, minting `D/v` shares at the pre-injection price.
4. The emissions-deposit transaction lands: [2](#0-1)  recomputes `asset_share_value' = (S*v + D + A) / (S + D/v)`, which is greater than `v`.
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem their `D/v` shares at `asset_share_value'`, receiving `D/v * asset_share_value' > D`, netting a profit funded by the emissions amount `A` that should have accrued only to the pre-existing shareholders `S`.
