## Analysis

**Binding claimed:** For any two accounts A and B, `reward_credited(X) / total_reward == shares_held_across_epoch(X) / total_stake_shares_during_epoch`. In particular, an account that held `S` shares for the full epoch and an account that held the same `S` shares for a single block immediately before the reward was posted should receive materially different rewards proportional to their time-weighted exposure — but the contract makes them receive **identical** rewards.

**Code path:**

`stake()` / `stake_all()` / `deposit_and_stake()` all call `internal_ping()` first, then `StakingContract::internal_stake` [1](#0-0)  which mints `num_shares` at the *current* share price and immediately adds them to `self.total_stake_shares` and `self.total_staked_balance` [2](#0-1) .

`internal_ping` is a no-op while `env::epoch_height()` hasn't changed [3](#0-2) , so calling `stake` in the last block of an epoch lets the attacker's shares get minted with zero effect from `internal_ping` — no reward has been distributed yet, but the attacker's shares are already counted in `total_stake_shares`.

At the next call after the epoch rolls over (e.g. any delegator's `ping`), `internal_ping` computes `total_reward = total_balance - last_total_balance` and distributes it by inflating `total_staked_balance` — i.e. proportionally to whoever currently holds shares in `total_stake_shares`, with **no per-account, time-weighted accounting** [4](#0-3) . The attacker's shares, minted one block earlier, get the exact same pro-rata cut as shares that were staked for the entire epoch that generated the reward.

The attacker then calls `unstake_all()` immediately, which itself calls `internal_ping()` (no-op, same epoch) and then `inner_unstake` redeems shares at the now-inflated price [5](#0-4) .

**Why guards don't help:** There is no minimum holding period before shares participate in `internal_ping`'s reward split — `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK` only gates *withdrawal* of unstaked funds, not reward eligibility of staked shares [6](#0-5) . `internal_ping`'s only assert is that `total_balance >= last_total_balance` [7](#0-6) , which does not prevent this. No `assert_owner`/`assert_self` applies since `stake`/`unstake_all` are open to any account.

This is a real, unmitigated design gap: the pro-rata reward-splitting model based purely on instantaneous share count at `internal_ping` time (rather than time-weighted share-epochs) allows any unprivileged account to capture a disproportionate share of the pool's reward by timing their stake/unstake around the epoch boundary, diluting genuine long-term delegators' and the owner's rightful reward share. This matches the description in Q2126 precisely, and the impact (reward misattributed away from the parties who actually earned it, extracted by an unprivileged account) fits the "High — rewards … attributed to the wrong party" category, since it is an accounting divergence in an on-chain balance (`total_staked_balance`/share price) that other delegators subsequently settle on.

### Title
One-block reward capture at epoch boundary dilutes genuine delegators' rewards - (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs`)

### Summary
`internal_ping` distributes each epoch's reward pro-rata across whatever `total_stake_shares` exist at the time of the first post-epoch-switch call, with no time-weighting. An attacker who calls `stake`/`deposit_and_stake` in the final block of an epoch mints shares that participate fully in the very next reward distribution, then calls `unstake_all` immediately after, extracting a full pro-rata epoch reward for one block of exposure.

### Finding Description
The invariant that should hold is: `account.reward ∝ ∫(account.stake_shares(t) dt)` over the rewarded epoch, i.e. reward proportional to time-weighted stake. Instead the contract implements `account.reward ∝ account.stake_shares(t_ping)`, evaluated only at the instant `internal_ping` runs.

Path: attacker calls `stake(amount)` (or `deposit_and_stake`) in the last block of epoch N. `internal_ping()` [3](#0-2)  returns early (epoch unchanged), so no reward has been settled yet. `internal_stake` then synchronously mints `num_shares` and adds them to `self.total_stake_shares`/`self.total_staked_balance` [8](#0-7) . When epoch N+1 begins and any account (attacker or someone else) triggers `internal_ping` again, the whole epoch's `total_reward` (driven by the protocol's validator reward credited to `env::account_locked_balance()`, which itself reflects stake that was locked *before* the attacker's last-block addition) is split pro-rata over the now-larger `total_stake_shares`, which includes the attacker's freshly minted shares [9](#0-8) . The attacker immediately calls `unstake_all()` (which itself is a no-op ping call, then `inner_unstake`) to redeem the diluted, reward-inflated share price [5](#0-4) , capturing a share of a reward it did not economically earn.

No existing check (`internal_ping`'s balance monotonicity assert, `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK` withdrawal delay, U256 rounding) restricts share issuance/eligibility based on time held; `stake`/`unstake_all` require no privilege.

### Impact Explanation
Every stake/unstake cycle timed at an epoch boundary transfers a disproportionate slice of `total_staked_balance` reward accrual away from delegators who held shares for the whole epoch (and away from the owner's fee, since `owners_fee` is computed on `total_reward` but shares distributed to the pool overall are also diluted by the attacker's temporary presence) and into the attacker's redeemable balance. This is repeatable every epoch, against any staking pool deployed from this contract, at effectively zero cost beyond gas and one block of capital lock-up, matching the "rewards … attributed to the wrong party" High-severity category.

### Likelihood Explanation
No special role or privilege is required — `stake`, `deposit_and_stake`, and `unstake_all` are open methods callable by anyone with capital to deposit. The attacker only needs to time a transaction near the end of an epoch (observable via `env::epoch_height()`/blockchain state) and repeat every epoch for continuous extraction. This is highly feasible and requires only normal user capabilities.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted share accounting (e.g., only allow newly staked shares to start earning rewards from the next full epoch after they are staked, tracking a "pending" bucket that is merged into reward-earning shares only after one full epoch has elapsed since staking), so `internal_ping`'s reward split excludes shares minted after the last ping in the reward-generating epoch.

### Proof of Concept
`cargo test` plan using `near-sdk-sim`/`RuntimeStandalone` (as in `staking-pool/tests/general.rs`):
1. Init pool, delegator A deposits and stakes `X` at epoch 0, ping to baseline.
2. Advance to the last block of epoch 1 (before reward credit). Delegator B (attacker) calls `deposit_and_stake(X)` in that same block.
3. Advance one block so epoch increments; simulate the protocol adding reward `R` to `account_locked_balance` (as other tests do via `reward_pool`/transfer + `ping`).
4. Call `ping()`. Assert `get_account_staked_balance(A)` and `get_account_staked_balance(B)` both increase by the same `~R/2` amount despite B only being staked for one block.
5. Immediately call `unstake_all()` for B and assert B's redeemable balance includes the reward share, proving `B.reward / B.time_staked >> A.reward / A.time_staked`, violating the proportional-time invariant.

    pub(crate) fn internal_stake(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Staking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        // Calculate the number of "stake" shares that the account will receive for staking the
        // given amount.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "The calculated number of \"stake\" shares received for staking should be positive"
        );
        // The amount of tokens the account will be charged from the unstaked balance.
        // Rounded down to avoid overcharging the account to guarantee that the account can always
        // unstake at least the same amount as staked.
        let charge_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);
        assert!(
            charge_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= charge_amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to stake"
        );
        account.unstaked -= charge_amount;
        account.stake_shares += num_shares;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The staked amount that will be added to the total to guarantee the "stake" share price
        // never decreases. The difference between `stake_amount` and `charge_amount` is paid
        // from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let stake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance += stake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;

    pub(crate) fn inner_unstake(&mut self, amount: u128) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Unstaking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        assert!(
            self.total_staked_balance > 0,
            "The contract doesn't have staked balance"
        );
        // Calculate the number of shares required to unstake the given amount.
        // NOTE: The number of shares the account will pay is rounded up.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_up(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "Invariant violation. The calculated number of \"stake\" shares for unstaking should be positive"
        );
        assert!(
            account.stake_shares >= num_shares,
            "Not enough staked balance to unstake"
        );

        // Calculating the amount of tokens the account will receive by unstaking the corresponding
        // number of "stake" shares, rounding up.
        let receive_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);
        assert!(
            receive_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        account.stake_shares -= num_shares;
        account.unstaked += receive_amount;
        account.unstaked_available_epoch_height = env::epoch_height() + NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The amount tokens that will be unstaked from the total to guarantee the "stake" share
        // price never decreases. The difference between `receive_amount` and `unstake_amount` is
        // paid from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let unstake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance -= unstake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares -= num_shares;
