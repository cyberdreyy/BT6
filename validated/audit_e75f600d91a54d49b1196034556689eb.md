What is confirmed from the code:

- `withdraw_from_staking_pool` sets the staking-pool status to `Busy` *before* the cross-contract `withdraw` call, and only sets it back to `Idle` inside the `on_staking_pool_withdraw` callback, which is also where `deposit_amount` is decremented (`saturating_sub`) — i.e., the account's physical balance can already reflect the withdrawn NEAR before `deposit_amount` is corrected: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .
- `get_account_balance()` saturates `env::account_balance() - MIN_BALANCE_FOR_STORAGE` to zero, matching the "storage floor" premise in the question: [4](#0-3) .
- `get_owners_balance()` sums `env::account_balance()` with the (possibly stale) `get_known_deposited_balance()` and subtracts `get_locked_amount()`, while `get_liquid_owners_balance()` caps the transferable amount to `min(get_owners_balance(), get_account_balance())`: [5](#0-4) .

During the window between the staking pool's `withdraw` actually crediting the lockup account (raising `env::account_balance()`) and the `on_staking_pool_withdraw` callback decrementing `deposit_amount`, `get_owners_balance()` can transiently double-count the withdrawn amount (once via the raised balance, once via the not-yet-decremented `deposit_amount`). Since `get_liquid_owners_balance()` only caps to the *physical* `get_account_balance()`, this cap does not prevent transferring funds that are physically present but still supposed to be locked — it only prevents transferring funds not physically present.
