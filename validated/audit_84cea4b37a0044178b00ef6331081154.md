### Title
Stake accounts remain freely transferable/authorizable during the multi-block partitioned epoch-reward distribution window, letting a new stake authority capture rewards accrued to the previous owner - ([File: runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/distribution.rs])

### Summary
The external report describes a race in `LendingNFT` where interest accrual (`claim()`) is not forced to happen atomically with NFT transfer, letting a buyer receive interest that rightfully belongs to the seller if the two operations land in different blocks. The analogous bug class - "value computed at one point in time but settled/delivered later, with no barrier against an ownership/authority change in between" - reappears in Agave's partitioned epoch-rewards mechanism for stake accounts.

### Finding Description
Stake rewards are not paid out atomically at the epoch boundary. Instead:

1. At the first slot of a new epoch, rewards for the *previous* epoch are calculated once, based on the delegation and effective stake recorded in `StakesCache` at that moment [1](#0-0) .
2. The resulting `PartitionedStakeRewards` are then distributed to individual stake accounts (identified only by `stake_pubkey`, not by any authority snapshot) across many subsequent blocks, up to 10% of the slots in an epoch [2](#0-1) .
3. When a stake account's turn comes up in `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` / `build_updated_stake_reward`, the reward is looked up fresh from the *current* `StakesCache` state of that address and lamports/delegation are added directly to whatever the account's state is at that time [3](#0-2) .

Nothing in this path re-checks or freezes the stake account's authorized staker/withdrawer, nor prevents `Authorize`/`AuthorizeWithSeed`/other stake-program mutations from moving control of the stake account to a different party between the moment the reward is calculated (start of epoch) and the moment it is actually credited (up to ~10% of an epoch later). I searched `programs/stake*/src` for any interval/reward-status gate analogous to the vote program's `pending_delegator_rewards` withdrawal guard, and found none - there is no `reward_interval`/`epoch_reward_status` check inside the stake program instruction handlers that would block reassignment of stake authority while a reward for that address is still pending distribution.

This mirrors the reported bug class precisely: the "interest" (stake reward) is computed based on ownership/delegation as of a past point in time, but its delivery (crediting the account) is deferred, uncoordinated, and spread over a window during which the account's controlling authority can change hands, just as the NFT's interest accrues based on past ownership but `claim()` is not enforced to happen atomically with `transferFrom`.

### Impact Explanation
If a stake account changes authorized staker/withdrawer (e.g., as part of a stake-account sale, delegation marketplace, or custodial transfer) during the reward-distribution window that follows an epoch boundary, the *new* authority ends up as the sole beneficiary of the lamports and delegation increase that was earned by the *previous* authority's stake activity in the prior epoch. Conversely, a seller who transfers control expecting to have already "claimed" their epoch's rewards may unknowingly hand over unclaimed rewards to the buyer. Given `store_accounts` writes the updated stake account unconditionally to whoever currently owns/controls it [4](#0-3) , this is a concrete misattribution of value between two parties, analogous to the referenced Medium-severity finding.

### Likelihood Explanation
Every epoch that uses partitioned rewards (i.e., any epoch beyond the warm-up period) creates this exposure window automatically for every delegated stake account, without requiring any bug or attacker-crafted transaction - it is a structural feature of the partitioned reward-distribution design. Because the window can span a meaningful fraction of an epoch's slots, an ordinary user transferring/selling stake authority during that period (a routine and expected operation, unlike triggering a rare edge case) can trigger the misattribution. No privileged access, leaked keys, or consensus-breaking behavior is required - only routine `Authorize`/stake-transfer transactions timed within the known distribution window.

### Recommendation
Either (a) snapshot and pin the reward recipient's identity/authority at calculation time and validate it still matches at distribution time (crediting an escrow/claimable balance instead if it does not), or (b) prevent stake authority changes for accounts with rewards still pending in the current `EpochRewardStatus::Active(...)` distribution window, similar to how the vote program blocks withdrawal while `pending_delegator_rewards > 0` [5](#0-4) .

### Proof of Concept
1. At an epoch boundary, Alice owns Stake Account S delegated to Vote Account V. `recalculate_stake_rewards`/`calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions` computes a reward for S based on stake as of the prior epoch and places it in a distribution partition scheduled for a block several hundred slots later [6](#0-5) .
2. Before that partition's distribution block arrives, Alice executes `Authorize`/`AuthorizeWithSeed` (as part of selling/transferring the stake account) to hand control of S to Bob.
3. When the scheduled block arrives, `distribute_epoch_rewards_in_partition` → `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` → `build_updated_stake_reward` credits the reward lamports and increased delegation directly onto S [7](#0-6) , with no check on who currently controls S.
4. Bob, now the authorized staker/withdrawer of S, receives the full benefit of the reward that was earned during Alice's ownership in the prior epoch.

Note: I was unable to fully verify the stake-program instruction handler source (`programs/stake*/src/*.rs` did not return the expected function definitions via grep, likely due to indexing limits), so I could not confirm with certainty whether any reward-interval guard exists deeper in that code that I did not locate. If such a guard does exist and blocks stake-authority changes while epoch rewards are pending for that specific account, this finding would not hold; I recommend a Devin session with full repository access to confirm this definitively.

    /// Calculate the number of blocks required to distribute rewards to all stake accounts.
    pub(super) fn get_reward_distribution_num_blocks(
        &self,
        rewards: &PartitionedStakeRewards,
    ) -> u64 {
        let total_stake_accounts = rewards.num_rewards();
        if self.epoch_schedule.warmup && self.epoch < self.first_normal_epoch() {
            1
        } else {
            const MAX_FACTOR_OF_REWARD_BLOCKS_IN_EPOCH: u64 = 10;
            let num_chunks = total_stake_accounts
                .div_ceil(self.partitioned_rewards_stake_account_stores_per_block() as usize)
                as u64;

            // Limit the reward credit interval to 10% of the total number of slots in a epoch
            num_chunks.clamp(
                1,
                (self.epoch_schedule.slots_per_epoch / MAX_FACTOR_OF_REWARD_BLOCKS_IN_EPOCH).max(1),
            )
        }
    }

    fn build_updated_stake_reward(
        distribution_epoch: u64,
        stake_history: &StakeHistory,
        new_warmup_cooldown_rate_epoch: Option<Epoch>,
        stakes_cache_accounts: &imbl::HashMap<Pubkey, StakeAccount<Delegation>>,
        partitioned_stake_reward: &PartitionedStakeReward,
        rent: &Rent,
        adjust_delegations_for_rent: bool,
        use_fixed_point_stake_math: bool,
    ) -> Result<StakeReward, DistributionError> {
        let stake_account = stakes_cache_accounts
            .get(&partitioned_stake_reward.stake_pubkey)
            .ok_or(DistributionError::AccountNotFound)?
            .clone();

        let (mut account, stake_state): (AccountSharedData, StakeStateV2) = stake_account.into();
        let StakeStateV2::Stake(meta, stake, flags) = stake_state else {
            // StakesCache only stores accounts where StakeStateV2::delegation().is_some()
            unreachable!(
                "StakesCache entry {:?} failed StakeStateV2 deserialization",
                partitioned_stake_reward.stake_pubkey
            )
        };
        account
            .checked_add_lamports(partitioned_stake_reward.inflation.stake_reward)
            .map_err(|_| DistributionError::ArithmeticOverflow)?;
        account
            .checked_add_lamports(partitioned_stake_reward.block_reward)
            .map_err(|_| DistributionError::ArithmeticOverflow)?;

        let mut new_stake = partitioned_stake_reward.inflation.stake;
        if adjust_delegations_for_rent {
            let minimum_balance = rent.minimum_balance(account.data().len());
            // The rewarded epoch is right before the distribution epoch
            let rewarded_epoch = distribution_epoch.saturating_sub(1);
            // The entry in `partitioned_stake_reward` contains the rewards,
            // calculated during the calculation phase
            let delegation_with_rewards = new_stake.delegation.stake;
            adjust_delegation_for_rent(
                &mut new_stake.delegation,
                rewarded_epoch,
                delegation_with_rewards,
                account.lamports(),
                minimum_balance,
            );
        } else {
            let expected_delegation = stake
                .delegation
                .stake
                .saturating_add(partitioned_stake_reward.inflation.stake_reward);
            assert_eq!(
                expected_delegation, new_stake.delegation.stake,
                "stake reward delegation must be consistent with the updated stake account \
                 lamport balance"
            );
        }
        account
            .set_state(&StakeStateV2::Stake(meta, new_stake, flags))
            .map_err(|_| DistributionError::UnableToSetState)?;
