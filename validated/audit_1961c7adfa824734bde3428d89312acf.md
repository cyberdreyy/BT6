Strategy-token receipt donation inflates CDO NAV — `_skimDonatedAssets` only skims underlying, enabling share-price manipulation and depositor theft - (File: contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol)

### Summary
`IdleCDOEpochVariant._skimDonatedAssets` (contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:794-796) removes only unsolicited `token` (underlying) donations, but the vault's NAV (`getContractValue` / `_managedContractValue` in contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:125-137) counts the full `strategyToken` balance held by the CDO. Withdraw receipts minted to users by `IdleCreditVault.requestWithdraw` and `requestInstantWithdraw` are plain ERC20 `strategyToken`s (minted via `_mint(_user, _amount)` at contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol:275 and :363). A KYC'd lender can therefore donate receipt tokens to the CDO, creating phantom NAV that is double-counted: the vault still owes the corresponding `pendingWithdraws`/`instantWithdrawsRequests` claim while the donated tokens inflate `virtualPrice` for all tranche holders.

### Finding Description
- `requestWithdraw` → `IdleCreditVault.requestWithdraw` burns `_principal` strategy tokens from the CDO but mints `_amount` (principal + interest) receipt tokens **to the user** (`IdleCreditVault.sol:272-275`). `requestInstantWithdraw` does the same (`:360-374`). Transfers are unrestricted — `canTransfer` is a deprecated storage flag (`:66-67`) and no `_beforeTokenTransfer`/`_transfer` hook restricts recipients.
- `_managedContractValue()` returns `balanceOf(strategyToken) - unclaimedFees` with no donation exclusion (`IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:133-137`). `virtualPrice` → `_virtualPriceAux` treats any NAV increase over `lastNAV` as a gain split per `trancheAPRSplitRatio` (`IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:287-314`).
- Every user-facing entry point (`_deposit`, `requestWithdraw`, `depositDuringEpoch`, `startEpoch`, `stopEpoch`, `finalizeDefault`) calls `_skimDonatedAssets()`, which transfers only `_contractTokenBalance(token)` — the underlying — to `feeReceiver`. `strategyToken` sent directly to the CDO is never skimmed and permanently inflates NAV.
- Attack sequence (buffer phase, fixed-APR mode): attacker deposits AA (becoming dominant LP), calls `requestWithdraw` to obtain receipt tokens, then `strategyToken.transfer(cdoEpoch, R)`. NAV rises by R while `withdrawsRequests[attacker]` is unchanged and `pendingWithdraws += R` was already recorded — the claim obligation is now double-counted as both a liability and as backing. `_updateAccounting` (triggered on the next deposit/withdraw) crystallizes the phantom gain into tranche prices. A victim depositing afterward mints at the inflated `virtualPrice` (`_mintSharesAtCurrPrice` path), under-receiving shares; the attacker then withdraws their remaining tranche tokens at the inflated price, extracting value backed by the victim's deposit and by vault cash reserved for funded claims. The attacker's own donated claim later reverts in `_claimFundedWithdrawRequest` (`_burn(_user, ...)` on a zero balance, `IdleCreditVault.sol:343`), but the theft is already realized and the forfeited receipt is part of the attack cost.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft plus solvency break. The fair-mint invariant is broken: each donated wei of strategy tokens raises NAV one-for-one while the underlying liability is unchanged, so tranche prices exceed real backing. Attacker profit is bounded by `donated × (their tranche share of the uplift captured from victim mints/withdraws)`; as the dominant or sole LP during the buffer window they capture essentially the full donation back plus the victim's under-minted difference, and funded withdrawal claims can be drained below par, leaving later claimants/LPs short — an insolvency condition equivalent to the DoS-with-fund-impact class of the reference bug (unprivileged availability/crash → here unprivileged NAV poisoning → frozen/mispriced claims).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only a KYC-passing lender (unprivileged per rules), a standard `requestWithdraw` during the buffer phase, and an ERC20 transfer — no privileged role, no timing race beyond a victim interacting while the donation sits unskimmed. Cost is the donated receipt claim plus gas. The guard exists for underlying but was never extended to the strategy token, and no epoch flag, only-CDO check, or reserve check intercepts the transfer.

### Recommendation
Extend donation handling to the strategy token: either skim `strategyToken` donations at the top of `_skimDonatedAssets` (transfer excess balance above the accounted backing to `feeReceiver` or burn it via `burnStrategyTokens`), or better, track expected strategy-token backing explicitly (e.g., derive NAV from accounted NAV rather than raw `balanceOf`) so unsolicited receipt transfers cannot enter `getContractValue`/`_managedContractValue`.
