### Title
Post-default instant withdraw receipts bypass recovery reserve accounting and spend other claimants' funded balance - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The dm bug class is a stale read of shared state combined with a use-after-free: one flow publishes a resource (`md->zone_revalidate_map`) while another flow keeps consuming the old one. The vault analog lives in the two parallel receipt paths of `IdleCreditVault`. `requestWithdraw` was hardened for the post-default world (`defaultRecoveryFinalized` branch funnels new requests into `postDefaultRequests` paid from the isolated `defaultRecoveryReserve`), but `requestInstantWithdraw` has no such branch. After `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, an attacker can still open an instant-withdraw receipt that is paid by `_transferFundedClaim` — i.e., from the strategy's non-reserve balance — consuming underlyings that back other users' already-funded loss-adjusted/defaulted receipts.

### Finding Description
`requestWithdraw` checks `defaultRecoveryFinalized` and routes post-default requests into `postDefaultRequests[_user]`, which are later paid via `_transferDefaultRecovery` against `defaultRecoveryReserve` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:247-257`, `760-767`, `912-916`). In contrast, `requestInstantWithdraw` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:356-375`) never checks `defaultRecoveryFinalized`. It burns `_amount` of the CDO's post-haircut strategy tokens, mints a 1:1 receipt to the user, and increments `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` / `pendingInstantWithdraws`.

`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:380-393`) then clears the *aggregate* `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` and pays via `_transferFundedClaim` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:897-907`), whose only guard is `balance - defaultRecoveryReserve >= amount`. `finalizeDefaultRecovery` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:661-709`) sized `defaultRecoveryReserve` only for `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` = `pendingWithdraws` + current-epoch instant claims measured **at finalization time**. An instant receipt created *after* finalization is outside that sizing, yet it is paid from the funded pool — the strategy balance minus reserve — which belongs to earlier funded receipts (funded instant claims not yet collected, and loss-adjusted claims awaiting `_claimLossAdjustedWithdrawRequest` at `IdleCreditVault.sol:789-801`).

Additionally, because `defaultInstantWithdrawsFinalized` is fixed at finalization (`IdleCreditVault.sol:696`), a post-finalization instant receipt that lands in `epochNumber` can partially alias `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]`, letting `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:842-856`) pay it at `defaultRecoveryPrice` out of the reserve while also decrementing `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` and `pendingInstantWithdraws` below their finalized values — corrupting the exact-basis accounting the reserve was sized for.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft of other claimants' funded withdraw proceeds. Each post-default instant request of `X` underlying surrenders strategy tokens whose NAV was already haircut to `defaultRecoveryPrice`, yet withdraws `X` from the funded pool. The `(1 - defaultRecoveryPrice) * X` premium is taken from underlyings reserved for other users' funded receipts; once the funded pool is drained, those users' `claimWithdrawRequest`/`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` calls revert at the `_transferFundedClaim` solvency guard (`IdleCreditVault.sol:904`), permanently freezing their claims. With `defaultRecoveryPrice = 0.5e18` and `F` of funded balance, an attacker extracts `F` of other users' funds while paying only `0.5 * F` of NAV.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only the honest sequence: a real borrower default finalized via `finalizeDefaultRecovery` with `defaultRecoveryPrice < 1` and a non-empty funded pool (loss-adjusted or funded instant receipts pending claim — common, since claims are lazy). The attacker is any KYC'd lender / tranche holder who calls the CDO's instant-withdraw request path after finalization and then `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`. No privileged role is abused; owner/manager calls (stopEpoch, finalization) happen normally. The missing `defaultRecoveryFinalized` gate in `requestInstantWithdraw` is the exact analog of `dm_blk_report_zones` being callable by any process against the not-yet-activated `zone_revalidate_map`.

Uncertainty: I did not trace the exact CDO entry point (`requestInstantWithdraw` is `_onlyIdleCDO`); a PoC must confirm the CDO exposes an instant-withdraw request that remains callable while `defaulted`/`defaultRecoveryFinalized`. If the CDO blocks it, the secondary vector (epoch-aliased `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` corrupting `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` after finalization) still stands wherever instant requests are possible between `startEpoch` and finalization.

### Recommendation
- In `requestInstantWithdraw`, either revert when `defaultRecoveryFinalized` is true or route the request into `postDefaultRequests`-style accounting paid from `defaultRecoveryReserve`.
- In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, when `defaultRecoveryFinalized`, clear only epochs covered by finalization; do not pay post-finalization receipts from `_transferFundedClaim`'s funded pool.
- Snapshot and freeze `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` mutation for `defaultRecoveryEpoch` once `defaultInstantWithdrawsFinalized` is set, mirroring how `dm` now tracks the single process allowed to touch `zone_revalidate_map`.
