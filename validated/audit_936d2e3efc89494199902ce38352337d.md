## Title
Just-in-time deposit before `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows capturing pro-rata emissions with no time-weighted contribution, diluting genuine depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that immediately raises a bank's `asset_share_value` in proportion to `total_asset_shares` at the instant it is called, with no mechanism that accounts for how long a depositor's shares have actually been outstanding. This mirrors the reported bug class: a reward event (swap-fee accrual there, direct emissions funding here) is distributed pro-rata over *current* share ownership rather than *time-weighted* ownership, letting an attacker who deposits immediately before the event and withdraws immediately after capture a disproportionate slice of rewards that should accrue to long-term liquidity/lending providers.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets any signer transfer same-mint tokens into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and then recomputes `asset_share_value` as `updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares`, where `total_asset_shares` is simply whatever the current on-chain state happens to be at call time: [1](#0-0) 

Because the value bump is applied uniformly across `total_asset_shares` regardless of how long each unit of shares has existed, a depositor's shares minted moments before this call receive exactly the same pro-rata boost as shares that have been outstanding for the entire campaign duration. There is no cooldown, minimum holding period, or time-weighted accrual gate on regular deposit/withdraw flows: [2](#0-1) 

The user-facing `EMISSIONS.md` guide explicitly documents the intended design as "pro-rata basis in real time," which is the same real-time-only, non-time-weighted distribution model that the external report identifies as exploitable: [3](#0-2) 

Since `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless (any `Signer` can call it, gated only by `has_one` checks on `mint`/`liquidity_vault`, not by admin authority) and is expected to be invoked by third parties (protocol team, reward sponsors, or automated funding flows) to fund incentive campaigns, an attacker capable of observing or predicting such a call (e.g., via mempool visibility, Jito bundling, or transaction-ordering influence) can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank just before the emissions-deposit transaction lands, inflating their `asset_shares` at essentially zero holding time.
2. Let the emissions deposit execute, which raises `asset_share_value` proportionally across all current `total_asset_shares`, including the attacker's freshly minted shares.
3. Immediately withdraw, realizing a share of the emissions funding that is disproportionate to the time their capital was actually at risk in the pool, at the expense of depositors who held shares throughout the accrual period.

### Impact Explanation
Genuine long-term depositors receive a diluted share of emissions funding intended for them, since the reward math has no time-weighting component; an attacker's capital only needs to be present for a single instant (bounded by two transactions, or potentially bundled) to be treated identically to capital that has been earning/at-risk for the full campaign. This is a direct value-redirection issue with financial effect on legitimate LPs/lenders, exactly the impact class the reported analog describes.

### Likelihood Explanation
No special privileges are required — `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless, and regular deposit/withdraw instructions have no cooldown or time-lock. The main precondition is the ability to time a deposit shortly before, and a withdraw shortly after, an emissions-deposit call, which is plausible via mempool observation or bundle-based transaction ordering on Solana, and is a routine occurrence whenever emissions campaigns are actively funded.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighting into the emissions/rewards distribution so that a share's eligibility for a given `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` boost scales with how long it has been outstanding (e.g., snapshotting `total_asset_shares` prior to a minimum staking window, or accruing emissions continuously rather than via lump-sum share-value bumps). Alternatively, apply a minimum holding period or block deposits/withdrawals from being paired within a short window around emissions funding events for the same account.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual reproduction based on the exposed code path:
1. Deployer/legitimate depositor deposits into a bank and holds shares for an extended period, accruing entitlement to an upcoming emissions campaign.
2. Attacker observes (or anticipates) an incoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call funding the bank's emissions.
3. Attacker submits a deposit transaction immediately before the emissions-deposit transaction, acquiring a large fraction of `total_asset_shares` at that moment: [4](#0-3) 
4. The emissions deposit executes, raising `asset_share_value` uniformly, crediting the attacker's freshly-minted shares equally to long-held shares.
5. Attacker withdraws immediately after, realizing their share of the emissions boost with negligible holding time, reducing the effective reward available to genuine depositors.
