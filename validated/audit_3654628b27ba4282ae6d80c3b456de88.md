### Title
Donation-inflated `virtualPrice` lets borrowers overborrow against AA tranche collateral via `TranchesChainlinkOracle`, causing bad debt - ([File: contracts/strategies/morpho/TranchesChainlinkOracle.sol](contracts/strategies/morpho/TranchesChainlinkOracle.sol))

### Summary
`TranchesChainlinkOracle.latestRoundData` reports `cdo.virtualPrice(collateralToken)` as the collateral price for Morpho Blue markets that accept IdleCDO tranche tokens at ~98% LLTV. `virtualPrice` is derived from `getContractValue()`, which counts the contract's raw `token` balance (`_contractTokenBalance(token)`). Any EOA can inflate NAV by directly transferring underlying tokens to the IdleCDO, pushing the reported tranche price above its fair value, borrowing against it, and then withdrawing the donated funds back out through `withdrawAA`/`withdrawBB`. This mirrors H-1: collateral is valued above its real recoverable value, producing undercollateralized loans and lender bad debt.

### Finding Description
- `TranchesChainlinkOracle.latestRoundData` returns `int256(cdo.virtualPrice(collateralToken))` [1](#0-0) 
- `virtualPrice` → `_virtualPriceAux` computes `totalGain = nav - lastNAV` where `nav = getContractValue()` [2](#0-1) 
- `getContractValue()` includes the contract's idle `token` balance with no donation isolation: `(strategyTokens * price) + _contractTokenBalance(token) - lockedRewards - unclaimedFees` [3](#0-2) 
- A plain ERC20 `transfer` of `token` to the IdleCDO raises `nav`, raising the AA tranche's `virtualPrice` proportionally (donated gain is split per `trancheAPRSplitRatio`; an attacker holding most of AA supply recaptures nearly all of it).
- The attacker then calls `withdrawAA`: `_updateAccounting` crystallizes the inflated price into `priceAA`, and `_withdraw` redeems from the unlent balance — which includes the donated tokens [4](#0-3) 

Broken invariant: fair collateral valuation — the oracle reports a price backed by funds that are withdrawable by the manipulator on demand. Existing guards do not stop it: a raw `transfer` does not call `_updateCallerBlock` (no same-block restriction), `_checkDefault` only detects strategy price drops, and `unclaimedFees`/`_lockedRewards` subtractions don't exclude donations. I could not fully verify whether a `_skimDonatedAssets`-style guard exists in `IdleCDOCreditVault` variants; in base `IdleCDO` there is none.

### Impact Explanation
The Morpho market using this oracle (deployed at `MORPHO_LLTV * 1e16` = 98% LLTV per `DeployMetamorphoVault`) allows borrowing up to 98% of the *reported* collateral value. After the attacker withdraws the donation, the tranche price reverts to fair value while the debt remains — the position is underwater and liquidations cannot recover it, leaving bad debt to lenders. Loss magnitude approaches the donation size times (LLTV − fair recovery)/LLTV; e.g., a 10% NAV inflation lets ~9.8% of true collateral value be borrowed risk-free against future bad debt.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only unprivileged actions: an ERC20 transfer, holding/buying tranche tokens (KYC'd lender role suffices since tranche minting is permissionless for allowed wallets), a Morpho `supplyCollateral`+`borrow`, and a later `withdrawAA`. Cost is limited to the performance fee on the donated gain plus the share of donated gain accruing to other tranche holders — minimized when the attacker dominates AA supply or when BB supply is zero (all gain goes to the depositing class). Profit depends on available Morpho liquidity in the tranche-collateral market.

### Recommendation
Exclude unaccounted donations from `getContractValue` (track expected unlent balance internally rather than reading `balanceOf`, or skim donated `token` before it reaches NAV), and/or have `TranchesChainlinkOracle` report the last crystallized `tranchePrice` instead of the manipulable `virtualPrice`. Additionally bound the oracle price change per block to mitigate single-transaction inflation.

    // check if _strategyPrice decreased
    _checkDefault();

    _updateAccounting();

    uint256 _want = toRedeem;
