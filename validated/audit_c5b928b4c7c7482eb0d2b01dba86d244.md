## Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Pro-Rata Share Value Boost Is Exploitable By Flash-Deposited Capital - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
The `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction lets **any caller** permissionlessly push tokens into a bank's liquidity vault, which immediately and uniformly raises `bank.asset_share_value` for every holder of `total_asset_shares` at that instant [1](#0-0) . Because the reward is distributed strictly pro-rata to whoever holds shares at the moment the deposit lands, and there is no minimum holding period, per-block update cap, or anti-flashloan restriction on this instruction, a user who deposits a large amount of the bank's asset immediately before an emissions push (and withdraws immediately after) captures a disproportionate share of value donated by someone else — exactly the attack pattern described in the bEth `update_global_index()` report.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` recomputes `asset_share_value` as:
```
total_assets = get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)   // shares * old_share_value
updated_total_assets = total_assets + amount
asset_share_value = updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares
``` [2](#0-1) 

This is structurally identical to the vulnerable `update_global_index()` in the bEth report: a reward pool balance is increased, and the increase is divided across `total_balance`/`total_asset_shares` without regard to *when* those shares were acquired. Depositing into a marginfi bank via `lending_account_deposit` mints shares at the current `asset_share_value` instantly [3](#0-2) , and withdrawing is equally instantaneous with no cooldown [4](#0-3) . Since `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless (`(permissionless) Deposit same-bank emissions directly into liquidity vault...`) [5](#0-4) , it is intended to let third parties (reward programs, LST issuers, points programs, etc.) donate value to existing depositors by directly bumping the share price — this is the marginfi analog of the `anchor_beth_reward` mechanism where rewards are pushed into a pool and divided by current balance.

Attack sequence:
1. Attacker observes (or anticipates, e.g. a recurring/scheduled) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction for bank X.
2. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large amount (own capital, or via marginfi's own flashloan wrapper `lending_account_start_flashloan`/`end_flashloan`, or any other atomic-composable capital source) just before the emissions deposit lands, acquiring a large fraction of `total_asset_shares` at the pre-emissions `asset_share_value`.
3. The emissions deposit lands, uniformly raising `asset_share_value` for all current shares, including the attacker's newly-minted ones.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (`withdraw_all`), realizing the inflated share value and extracting a slice of value that was intended for genuine, long-term depositors.
5. If capital was borrowed via flashloan, the attacker repays it in the same transaction and walks away with the captured spread, having contributed nothing but momentary capital.

Unlike the bEth converter case, the tests confirm there is no time-based, block-based, or holding-period gating anywhere in this path — only operational-state, transfer-fee, and transfer-hook checks are enforced [6](#0-5) .

### Impact Explanation
Genuine long-term depositors of the bank have their share of a legitimate emissions/rewards donation diluted by an attacker who held shares only for the duration of a single (or adjacent) transaction. This is a direct value-redirection / misappropriation of donated funds — a financial-impact issue affecting the fairness of reward distribution for any bank receiving `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` funding, which per the changelog is a real, currently-used feature (v0.1.9) rather than a theoretical one [7](#0-6) .

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-high in practice: the instruction is permissionless and callable by any actor (protocol, DAO, LST issuer) at any time, deposits/withdraws are unrestricted and instantaneous, and marginfi already ships a native flashloan primitive (`lending_account_start_flashloan` / `lending_account_end_flashloan`) that gives attackers atomic access to large capital within a single transaction [8](#0-7) . The main constraint is that the attacker must land their deposit/withdraw around the emissions-deposit transaction (same tx if they control both, or adjacent slots if front-running/back-running a third-party emissions funder), which is achievable for scheduled/predictable emissions campaigns.

### Recommendation
Adopt the same mitigations proposed in the referenced report, adapted to marginfi's share-based accounting:
- Require a minimum holding period (based on `Balance.last_update` / bank `last_update`) before a depositor's shares are eligible to receive the benefit of an emissions deposit, or snapshot share balances prior to the emissions deposit and distribute pro-rata to that snapshot rather than to the live `total_asset_shares`.
- Alternatively, rate-limit or cap the amount of `asset_share_value` increase that can be applied within a single slot/transaction relative to bank activity, similar to the existing `BankRateLimiter` used elsewhere in the protocol [9](#0-8) .
- Consider disallowing `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw`/`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` on the same bank within the same transaction or same flashloan context.

### Proof of Concept
Using the existing test harness pattern from `emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value` [10](#0-9) :
1. Depositor A deposits 40 USDC, Depositor B deposits 60 USDC (baseline `asset_share_value` = 1).
2. Attacker deposits, say, 900 USDC into the same bank (via flashloan or own capital) immediately before an emissions push, making the attacker hold ~90% of `total_asset_shares`.
3. A 50-USDC `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lands, computed as `asset_share_value = (1000 + 50) / 1000 = 1.05` — the attacker's shares alone now represent ~45 of the 50 USDC donated (`900 * 0.05`), versus A and B combined only receiving ~5 USDC, despite contributing all of the pool's actual economic activity.
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw(withdraw_all=true)`, extracting 945 USDC (900 principal + ~45 captured reward) and, if using a flashloan, repays the 900 USDC borrowed within the same transaction, netting ~45 USDC of value that should have gone to A and B.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
```

pub fn lending_account_withdraw<'info>(
    mut ctx: Context<'info, LendingAccountWithdraw<'info>>,
    amount: u64,
    withdraw_all: Option<bool>,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    let LendingAccountWithdraw {
        marginfi_account: marginfi_account_loader,
        destination_token_account,
        liquidity_vault: bank_liquidity_vault,
        token_program,
        bank_liquidity_vault_authority,
        bank: bank_loader,
        group: marginfi_group_loader,
        ..
    } = ctx.accounts;
    let clock = Clock::get()?;

    let withdraw_all = withdraw_all.unwrap_or(false);
    let mut marginfi_account = marginfi_account_loader.load_mut()?;
    let group = marginfi_group_loader.load()?;

    {
        let maybe_bank_mint = {
            let bank = bank_loader.load()?;
            utils::maybe_take_bank_mint(&mut ctx.remaining_accounts, &bank, token_program.key)?
        };

        let in_receivership_or_order_execution =
            marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP | ACCOUNT_IN_ORDER_EXECUTION);
        let mut bank = bank_loader.load_mut()?;
        validate_bank_state(&bank, InstructionKind::FailsInPausedState)?;

        // Fetch oracle price for rate limiting and deleverage tracking
        // When group rate limiter is enabled, oracle is required
        let group_rate_limit_enabled = group.rate_limiter.is_enabled();
        let price = if in_receivership_or_order_execution || group_rate_limit_enabled {
            let price = fetch_asset_price_for_bank_low_bias(
                &bank_loader.key(),
                &bank,
                &clock,
                ctx.remaining_accounts,
            )?;

            // Validate price is non-zero during liquidation/deleverage to prevent exploits
            if in_receivership_or_order_execution {
                check!(price > I80F48::ZERO, MarginfiError::ZeroAssetPrice);
            }

            price
        } else {
            I80F48::ZERO
        };

        bank.accrue_interest(
            clock.unix_timestamp,
            &group,
            #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
            bank_loader.key(),
        )?;

        let liquidity_vault_authority_bump = bank.liquidity_vault_authority_bump;

        let in_receivership = marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP);
        let lending_account = &mut marginfi_account.lending_account;
        let mut bank_account =
            BankAccountWrapper::find(&bank_loader.key(), &mut bank, lending_account)?;

        let (amount_pre_fee, share_amount) = if withdraw_all {
            // Note: In liquidation, we still want this passed on the books
            bank_account.withdraw_all(in_receivership)?
        } else {
            let amount_pre_fee = maybe_bank_mint
                .as_ref()
                .map(|mint| {
                    utils::calculate_pre_fee_spl_deposit_amount(
                        mint.to_account_info(),
                        amount,
                        clock.epoch,
                    )
                })
                .transpose()?
                .unwrap_or(amount);

            let share_amount = bank_account.withdraw(I80F48::from_num(amount_pre_fee))?;

            (amount_pre_fee, share_amount)
        };

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);
