### Title
Fee-on-transfer tokens break credit vault solvency: strategy mints unbacked strategy tokens and drains the CDO buffer - (File: contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit` correctly mints tranche shares based on the *net* tokens received (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal`), but then forwards the *gross* `_amount` to `IdleCreditVault.deposit`, which mints strategy tokens 1:1 for `_amount` while only receiving `_amount - fee` after a second transfer fee. The result is unbacked strategy-token supply counted in `getContractValue`, and a net drain of the CDO's underlying buffer belonging to other depositors.

### Finding Description
In `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit` (contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:191-212):

1. `_transferUnderlyingsFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount)` pulls `_amount` from the user; with a fee-on-transfer token the CDO receives `_amount - fee1`.
2. `_mintSharesAtCurrPrice(_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal, ...)` mints tranche tokens for the net received amount — consistent with user-facing accounting.
3. `IIdleCDOStrategy(strategy).deposit(_amount)` is then called with the **gross** amount.

In `IdleCreditVault.deposit` (contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol:596-617):

```solidity
underlyingToken.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount);
_mint(msg.sender, _amount);
```

The strategy pulls `_amount` from the CDO but receives `_amount - fee2`, while minting a full `_amount` of strategy tokens to the CDO. Since `getContractValue` (IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:125-128) values strategy tokens 1:1 with underlyings, NAV is inflated by `fee2` per deposit, and the CDO's underlying balance decreased by `_amount` while only `_amount - fee1` was credited to the depositor — the `fee1` difference is silently taken from the CDO buffer that backs instant withdrawals and other LPs.

The same pattern exists in `collectWithdrawFunds`/`collectInstantWithdrawFunds` (IdleCreditVault.sol:398-430), where funded amounts are accounted at gross but received net, permanently shorting funded receipts.

### Impact Explanation
- **Insolvency / loss socialization**: each deposit creates `fee1 + fee2` of unbacked value. The CDO buffer (used for `getInstantWithdrawFunds` and epoch rollovers) is drained by `fee1` per deposit; the strategy holds `fee2` less underlying than its minted supply. Last withdrawers / claimants cannot be paid — withdrawals revert or recovery prices are understated.
- If the CDO buffer is empty, `strategy.deposit(_amount)` reverts outright, permanently DoSing deposits once the token enables fees.
- Quantified: with a 1% transfer fee, depositing 100 tokens mints the user 99 tranche tokens but pushes 100 out of the CDO and mints 100 strategy tokens backed by only 99 — a 2-token hole per deposit borne by other LPs and pending claimants.

### Likelihood Explanation
Credit vaults are deployed per-borrower with a pool currency (e.g. USDC/USDT). Tokens like USDT already support fee-on-transfer on some chains, and USDC-class tokens can gain fee mechanics on L2 deployments — exactly the scenario in the source report. No privileged action is required: any KYC'd lender calling `depositAA`/`depositBB` triggers the discrepancy once the token charges fees. There is no guard (`_skimDonatedAssets` only handles unsolicited transfers to the CDO, not strategy-side shortfalls).

### Recommendation
Use balance deltas end-to-end. In `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit`, pass the actually received amount (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal`) to `strategy.deposit`, not the gross `_amount`. In `IdleCreditVault.deposit`, mint strategy tokens based on `balanceOf` delta rather than `_amount`. Apply the same delta accounting in `collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, and `sendInterestAndDeposits`, or explicitly document/enforce that only non-fee tokens are supported.

The invariant broken is solvency/fair mint: `strategyToken` supply is minted 1:1 with gross `_amount` while backing only grows by the net received, and the CDO spends more underlying than the depositor was credited for.
