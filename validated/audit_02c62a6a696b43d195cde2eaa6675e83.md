### Title
Stale `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` basis survives funded claims and is paid again from the default recovery reserve - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` zeroes the aggregate `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` but never clears the per-epoch receipt basis `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epoch]`. After a default is finalized for that epoch, `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` reads the stale per-epoch entry and pays it again out of `defaultRecoveryReserve`, exactly like the Node.js bug where a reset statement's cached iterator keeps executing with new bindings.

### Finding Description
In `requestInstantWithdraw`, the vault records both an aggregate receipt (`instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]`) and a per-epoch receipt (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][currentEpoch]`) used only for default-recovery claims (IdleCreditVault.sol:356-375). The normal claim path burns the receipt tokens, sets `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user] = 0`, and pays the funded amount — but leaves the per-epoch mapping untouched (IdleCreditVault.sol:386-393). The "reset" (funded claim) does not invalidate the cached per-epoch record.

Once `defaultRecoveryFinalized && defaultInstantWithdrawsFinalized`, `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` first calls `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`, which unconditionally loads `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]`, decrements `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` by it, burns that many receipt tokens, and pays `claimBasis * defaultRecoveryPrice` from the recovery reserve (IdleCreditVault.sol:842-856).

Sequence:
1. Buffer/running epoch N: attacker calls `requestInstantWithdraw` for K; the queue/CDO funds it via `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`.
2. Attacker calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`: receives K, receipt tokens burned, `instantWithdrawsRequests = 0`, but `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[attacker][N]` still equals K.
3. Epoch N ends in borrower default; `finalizeDefault`/`finalizeDefaultRecovery` set `defaultRecoveryEpoch = N` and fund `defaultRecoveryReserve`.
4. Before claiming, attacker places a new instant request of M ≥ K in epoch N+1 (mints M fresh receipt tokens; `instantWithdrawsRequests = M`).
5. Attacker calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` again: `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` clears the stale basis K, burns K (backed by the new receipt tokens), and pays `K * defaultRecoveryPrice` from the reserve; then the funded path pays the remaining `M - K`. Attacker extracts `K * defaultRecoveryPrice` of reserve that belongs to real defaulted-epoch claimants.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft of `defaultRecoveryReserve`: every defaulted-epoch creditor's pro-rata recovery is diluted by `K * defaultRecoveryPrice`. With K at whale scale (receipt is only limited by liquidity/attacker capital) the reserve can be drained before honest claimants call `claim`, causing permanent loss to defaulted lenders — equivalent in magnitude to the fraction of the reserve the attacker can replay.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: (a) instant withdrawals enabled and funded in the epoch that later defaults, (b) the attacker claiming before default, and (c) the ability to place a fresh instant request with receipt tokens ≥ the stale basis at claim time. (c) is the uncertain part — I did not fully verify whether `IdleCDOEpochVariant.requestWithdraw`/instant-withdraw remains callable for the attacker between default and claim, or in a subsequent epoch before finalization. If post-default/pre-finalization instant requests are blocked, the stale basis alone cannot be burned (receipt tokens for K were already burned at step 2), reducing this to a revert rather than theft. The bookkeeping flaw itself is unambiguous: the per-epoch record is never cleared on a successful funded claim.

### Recommendation
In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, when paying a funded claim, also clear the corresponding `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epochNumber]` (or track per-epoch paid flags) and decrement `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` so already-paid receipts are invisible to `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`. Alternatively, have `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` cap `claimBasis` at `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]`'s unfunded portion.
