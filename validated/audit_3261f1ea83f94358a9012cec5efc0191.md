This confirms the mechanism precisely. The `Accepted` branch's guard (`!block.gathered_signatures.contains_key(&slot_id)`) is independent of `block.total_weight_rejected`/`responded_signers`'s rejection bookkeeping, so a signer who previously rejected and is present in `responded_signers` (having added their weight to `total_weight_rejected` at `stacks-node/src/nakamoto_node/stackerdb_listener.rs:515-518`) and later accepts (a legitimate re-evaluation per the `LocallyRejected --> LocallyAccepted` transition documented for `BlockInfo::check_state`) will have their weight added again into `total_weight_approved` at lines 443-446, without any corresponding decrement of `total_weight_rejected`. This is the exact analog of the `RoyaltiesOracle` pattern: the check/update does not account for the *existing* contribution (the earlier rejection) when applying the *new* one (the acceptance), so the signer's weight is double-booked across the two mutually-exclusive tallies.

### Title
Stale rejection weight not cleared on vote flip causes spurious block rejection (weight double-counted across accept/reject tallies) - (File: stacks-node/src/nakamoto_node/stackerdb_listener.rs)

### Summary
`StackerDBListener`'s per-block `BlockStatus` (`total_weight_approved`, `total_weight_rejected`) is maintained as two independently-incremented running totals keyed off `responded_signers`/`gathered_signatures`. When a signer sends a `Rejected` response and later sends an `Accepted` response for the same block (a legitimate re-evaluation, matching the signer-side `LocallyRejected --> LocallyAccepted` transition), their weight is added to `total_weight_approved` without ever being removed from `total_weight_rejected`. The two aggregates no longer reflect the signer's *current* verified vote.

### Finding Description
`total_weight_rejected` is incremented in the `Rejected` branch guarded only by `block.responded_signers.insert(slot_id)` [1](#0-0) . `total_weight_approved` is incremented in the `Accepted` branch guarded by a *different* set, `block.gathered_signatures.contains_key(&slot_id)` [2](#0-1) . Because these two guards track disjoint state, a signer that rejects then later accepts gets counted in both totals, and `total_weight_rejected` is never decremented back out — the earlier vote persists as if it still stood, even though it has been publicly superseded by a signature. This is analogous to the `RoyaltiesOracle._setRoyalty()` bug, where an update did not net out the collector's existing contribution before re-checking against the shared budget; here the coordinator does not net out a signer's prior rejection weight before folding in their new acceptance weight, corrupting the aggregated-weight-vs-verified-accepts/rejects equality that `signer_coordinator.rs`'s `get_block_status` relies on to decide the outcome [3](#0-2) .

### Impact Explanation
This is a liveness wedge on the mining/coordination path (node-side, in-scope): the miner can conclude `total_weight_rejected.saturating_add(weight_threshold) > total_weight` (`stacks-node/src/nakamoto_node/signer_coordinator.rs:509-513`) and abandon a block as `SignersRejected`, even though the actual, current-opinion rejecting weight (excluding signers who flipped to accept) is below the 30% blocking minority and the block may in fact be legitimately signable. It is triggerable by a single signer changing their mind after a re-proposal/re-evaluation — no majority is required, only that one signer's rejection weight, combined with genuinely rejecting weight from others, crosses the blocking-minority threshold due to the stale double-count.

### Likelihood Explanation
Vote flips (`Rejected` → `Accepted`) are an explicitly supported and documented flow (`LocallyRejected --> LocallyAccepted: re-evaluated`, per `docs/signer-flows.md` and `BlockInfo::check_state`), so this is not a contrived edge case — it happens whenever a signer reconsiders a rejection (e.g., after `should_reevaluate_reject_reason` permits re-evaluation) and subsequently signs. Any signer running the standard protocol can produce this sequence via ordinary StackerDB gossip; no attacker-controlled key or majority collusion is needed.

### Recommendation
When processing an `Accepted` (or `Rejected`) response, check whether the signer's slot previously contributed to the *opposite* tally and, if so, subtract their weight from that tally before adding it to the new one — i.e., make `total_weight_approved`/`total_weight_rejected` mutually exclusive per signer, mirroring the fix applied to `RoyaltiesOracle._setRoyalty()` (net out the existing contribution before applying the new one), rather than tracking them as independently-incrementing counters guarded by separate sets.

### Proof of Concept
1. Miner proposes block B; `BlockStatus` for B is inserted with all totals at 0 (`insert_block`, `stacks-node/src/nakamoto_node/stackerdb_listener.rs:693-704`).
2. Signer S (weight w) sends `BlockResponse::Rejected` for B → `responded_signers.insert(S)` succeeds → `total_weight_rejected += w`.
3. Enough other signers reject so that `total_weight_rejected` sits just under the blocking-minority threshold (`total_weight - weight_threshold`).
4. The miner reproposes (or S re-evaluates per `should_reevaluate_reject_reason`) and S now sends `BlockResponse::Accepted` for the same block hash. `gathered_signatures.contains_key(S)` is false (S never accepted before) → `total_weight_approved += w`, and `S` is (redundantly) inserted into `responded_signers`.
5. `total_weight_rejected` still includes S's weight w, unchanged. If one more genuinely rejecting signer's weight arrives, `total_weight_rejected.saturating_add(weight_threshold) > total_weight` can become true in `get_block_status`, causing the coordinator to return `NakamotoNodeError::SignersRejected` and abandon the block — even though S's current, verified vote is an acceptance and the real rejecting weight (excluding S) may not constitute a genuine blocking minority.
