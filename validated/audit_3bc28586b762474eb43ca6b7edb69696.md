### Title
Unbacked strategy-token mint via ERC4626 vault donation inflating `totalInterestDueNow` in minted-interest programmable-borrower epochs - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol)

### Summary
In programmable-borrower mode with `isInterestMinted`, `stopEpoch` prices the epoch from `IProgrammableBorrower(borrower).totalInterestDueNow()` (`IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:1004`), which includes `vaultInterest` — the positive delta of `convertToAssets(vaultShares)` vs the epoch baseline (`ProgrammableBorrower.sol:337-347`). A third-party ERC4626 depositor can donate underlying to the external vault to inflate that delta. Because minted mode pulls zero cash (`_amountToPullFromBorrower = 0`, `IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:376`) and mints `_grossInterest` strategy tokens unbacked by real assets (`line 430`), phantom yield is crystallized into tranche prices and withdrawal receipts, then drained by the attacker as a KYC'd LP — a "declared vs. enforced amount" analog of the TUS bug: the vault credits more "yield" than was ever deposited by the borrower.

### Finding Description
- `_vaultNetInterest` computes `earnedAssets = _currentVaultAssets() + epochWithdrawnFromVault` vs `principalAssets = epochStartVaultAssets + epochDepositedToVault`. `epochDepositedToVault` is only incremented for the borrower's own `_depositToVault` calls (`ProgrammableBorrower.sol:378-385`), so any underlying donated by a third party directly into the ERC4626 vault (or any mechanism raising `convertToAssets` for the borrower's existing shares) is counted entirely as epoch "interest".
- In `stopEpoch`, programmable mode resolves `_interest` to `totalInterestDueNow()` (`IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:1001-1005`). With `isInterestMinted`, `_grossInterest` is minted as strategy tokens via `mintStrategyTokens` (`line 430`) and `_updateAccounting` splits the phantom gain into AA/BB NAVs and prices. The only gross-interest cap (`lines 377-380`) is unreachable in programmable mode because `_interest > 1` reverts at `line 1003`.
- The attacker, being both a vault depositor and a KYC'd tranche holder, deposits AA before `startEpoch`, donates X underlying to the ERC4626 vault during the epoch, lets the manager `stopEpoch(0,0)`, then calls `requestWithdraw`/`claimWithdrawRequest` and is paid real cash at the inflated receipt value, while redeeming their external vault shares to recover the bulk of the donation. The minted strategy tokens remain unbacked, leaving other LPs with diluted NAV and an insolvent pool equal to roughly X net of vault-dilution and fees.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft and insolvency: phantom interest is converted into real claims against the pool's cash (pending withdraws are paid from borrower repayments). Loss ≈ donated amount X, bounded only by the attacker's capital and the vault's willingness to count donations in `totalAssets`. The broken invariant is fair mint/burn plus solvency: `mintStrategyTokens` assumes minted tokens correspond to borrower-payable interest, but here they correspond to a recoverable third-party donation.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a programmable-borrower deployment with `isInterestMinted` and an external ERC4626 whose `totalAssets` reflects raw donations (common for idle-balance vaults; not for vaults that track only deployed principal). Attacker needs KYC (`isWalletAllowed`) plus capital for the donation, most of which is recoverable through their own vault shares. No privileged role is needed.

### Recommendation
Track all vault asset inflows the same way as `epochDepositedToVault` — e.g., baseline net-of-share issuance by snapshotting `convertToAssets` price per share at `startEpochAccounting` and computing interest as `shares * (priceNow - priceStart)` only if price moves are attributable to yield, or better, cap recognized `vaultInterest` at an imputed max APR over the epoch (`vaultAssets * maxApr * elapsed`), mirroring the `_maxApr` guard already used for the non-programmable override path. Alternatively, require `totalInterestDueNow` interest to be settled in cash (`_amountToPullFromBorrower > 0`) rather than minted whenever it exceeds a time-bounded expected yield.

### Proof of Concept
```solidity
// Fork: ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol setup (Morpho-style ERC4626 vault)
// Attacker = KYC'd LP + ERC4626 vault depositor. Epoch running, isInterestMinted = true.

uint256 amount = 10_000 * oneScale;
idleCDO.depositAA(amount);                 // attacker LP deposit
_startEpochAndCheckPrices(0);

// Donation inflates convertToAssets of borrower's vault shares
uint256 donation = 500 * oneScale;
deal(USDC, attacker, donation);
underlying.transfer(address(externalVault), donation); // totalAssets += donation

uint256 phantom = programmableBorrower.vaultInterestAccrued();
assertApproxEqAbs(phantom, donation, 10);  // donation counted as epoch yield

vm.warp(cdoEpoch.epochEndDate() + 1);
vm.prank(manager);
cdoEpoch.stopEpoch(0, 0);                  // mints `phantom` unbacked strategy tokens

// Attacker redeems vault shares: recovers most of donation
// Attacker requests + claims withdrawal at inflated tranche price,
// paid from real pendingWithdraw cash -> other LPs diluted by ~donation.
assertGt(cdoEpoch.virtualPrice(address(aaTranche)), pricePreDonation);
```
