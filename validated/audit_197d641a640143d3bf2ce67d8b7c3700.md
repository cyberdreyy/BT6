1. **Rejection recounted as acceptance / double counting.** The signer stackerdb listener explicitly guards against a signer's response being counted twice: `responded_signers.insert(slot_id)` gates both the acceptance and rejection tally paths, and each signer's slot can only register once toward `total_weight_approved` or `total_weight_rejected` [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) . 2. **Signing a conflicting/non-canonical block (equivocation).** The pre-commit → signature path re-validates chain state at multiple points and explicitly re-derives whether any previously-signed conflicting block at the same or higher height can still land in the canonical chain before allowing a signature (`get_signed_conflicts`, `conflict_still_blocks`, `reorg_permit_stands`) [4](#0-3) . This machinery is designed precisely to prevent the double-sign/equivocation scenario your prompt's rubric calls out as "Critical."

3. **Cross-context-valid signature / aggregated-weight vs verified-accepts.** `verify_signer_signatures` in the chainstate recovers each signature's public key, matches it against the reward set for that specific block's `signer_signature_hash`, and enforces strict signature ordering to prevent replay/reuse across contexts [5](#0-4) .
