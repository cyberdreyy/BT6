### Title
Post-default withdraw requests are excluded from the recovery claim basis but are still paid 1:1 from the shared `defaultRecoveryReserve`, draining funds owed to defaulted-epoch receipt holders - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The JOJO "delisted collateral still liquidated" bug class — an asset excluded from one accounting/health path while remaining usable in a value-extracting path — maps onto `IdleCreditVault`'s post-default withdraw flow. After `finalizeDefaultRecovery` fixes `defaultRecoveryPrice` and sizes `defaultRecoveryReserve` only for the pre-default claim basis, `requestWithdraw` keeps minting new receipt claims (`postDefaultRequests`) that were never part of `totalBasis`, and `_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` pays them 1:1 out of that same reserve.

### Finding Description
`finalizeDefaultRecovery` computes `totalBasis = activeBasis + pendingBasis` and stores `defaultRecoveryReserve = reserveAmount` sized to cover only those pre-default claims at `recoveryPrice` (IdleCreditVault.sol:674-708). Once `defaultRecoveryFinalized` is true, `requestWithdraw` takes a special path: it burns the CDO's strategy tokens, mints `_amount` receipt tokens to the user and records `postDefaultRequests[_user] = _amount` — without adding any underlying to the reserve (lines 247-257). `_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` then calls `_transferDefaultRecovery`, which decrements `defaultRecoveryReserve` 1:1 and transfers underlying (lines 760-767, 912-917).

The analog to delisted collateral: post-default receipts are "delisted" from the recovery basis (`defaultPendingClaimBasis` / `totalBasis` ignore them) yet remain liquidatable against the reserve at par. Each such claim consumes reserve that was only provisioned for defaulted-epoch receipts, so late-claiming defaulted receipt holders find the reserve depleted; `defaultRecoveryReserve -= _amount` underflows and their claims revert, permanently freezing their recovery.

### Impact Explanation
Any tranche holder can post-default `requestWithdraw` and then `claimWithdrawRequest`, extracting underlying at a 1:1 rate from a reserve that was sized only to pay `recoveryPrice` on pre-default basis. The quantified loss equals the sum of post-default payouts, directly reducing payouts to defaulted-epoch claimants (theft of unclaimed recovery) or making their claims permanently revert (permanent freezing of the reserve remainder).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a finalized default with positive `defaultRecoveryReserve` (borrower partial repayment or prefunded funds). After that, only an unprivileged tranche holder calling `requestWithdraw`/`claimWithdrawRequest` through the CDO is needed; no privileged action is involved. Each post-default request must wait one epoch boundary (`epochNumber > lastWithdrawRequest` guard in `_claimFundedWithdrawRequest` does not even apply since `_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` runs first), so claims are immediate.

### Recommendation
Track post-default requests in a separate accounting bucket and fund them separately (e.g., require the CDO to transfer the underlying backing the minted receipts into the strategy at request time, or cap post-default payouts to freshly supplied funds). Alternatively, add a `postDefaultReserve` incremented when the receipt is created, and make `_claimPostDefaultWithdrawRequest` consume only that bucket, never `defaultRecoveryReserve`.
