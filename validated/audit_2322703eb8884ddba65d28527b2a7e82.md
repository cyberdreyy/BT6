### Title
Unfunded instant-withdraw receipts from pre-default epochs are dropped from `defaultPendingClaimBasis`, mispricing `defaultRecoveryPrice` and permanently freezing those claims - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol](contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol))

### Summary
The external bug is a use-after-free caused by dropping a reference while the object is still in use. The direct analog in `IdleCreditVault` is a lifetime/accounting mismatch: `pendingInstantWithdraws` is an aggregate across all epochs, but `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` and `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest()` only account for receipts recorded under the *current* `epochNumber` (`instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` / `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][defaultEpoch]`). Instant receipts left unfunded in an earlier epoch (e.g., a `stopEpochWithDuration` loss epoch where `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` funded only part of the queue) are silently "freed" from the recovery basis while their receipt tokens still exist, so they can never be claimed and they inflate the recovery price paid to everyone else.

### Finding Description
- `requestInstantWithdraw` records receipts per epoch (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][epochNumber]`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]`) but tracks the unfunded remainder in a single aggregate `pendingInstantWithdraws` (`contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol:356-375`).
- `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` permits partial funding (`pendingInstantWithdraws -= _amount`), so a loss/stressed epoch can roll forward with `pendingInstantWithdraws > 0` belonging to an *older* epoch bucket (`IdleCreditVault.sol:398-403`).
- At finalization, `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` adds only `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` — the *current* epoch — whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:644-649`). Old-epoch instant basis is excluded from `totalBasis`, so `recoveryPrice = reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / totalBasis` is set too high (`IdleCreditVault.sol:679-692`).
- Post-finalization, `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` clears only `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:842-856`). A holder of an old-epoch unfunded instant receipt falls through to the funded path in `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, which pays `instantWithdrawsRequests[user]` at par via `_transferFundedClaim` — but those funds were never collected, and the reserve guard `balance - reserve < _amount` reverts (`IdleCreditVault.sol:897-906`). The receipt is permanently unclaimable.
- Symmetrically, because old instant basis was excluded from `totalBasis`, default-epoch claimants are each paid `claimBasis * defaultRecoveryPrice` at an inflated price, draining `defaultRecoveryReserve` early so later claimants' `_transferDefaultRecovery` underflows/reverts (`IdleCreditVault.sol:912-916`).

### Impact Explanation
Two-sided loss: (1) holders of unfunded instant receipts from the pre-default epoch lose their entire recovery share — a permanent freeze of funds that should have diluted `defaultRecoveryPrice`; (2) the reserve is distributed at an overpriced rate, so early default-epoch claimants receive more than their fair pro-rata share and later claimants' claims revert — theft of recovery value plus permanent freezing of the remainder. The loss magnitude is roughly `reserve * oldInstantBasis / (totalBasis + oldInstantBasis)` misallocated, plus the full stranded old-receipt basis.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a specific but unprivileged-compatible sequence: an epoch ending with a partial instant-withdraw funding (realized loss or shortfall at `stopEpoch`, an honest-manager action), leaving `pendingInstantWithdraws > 0` attributed to epoch N-1, followed by a borrower default finalized in epoch N. No malicious privileged role is needed; the attacker is simply an early claimer or a holder of the orphaned receipt. Existing guards (`_onlyIdleCDO`, the `_transferFundedClaim` reserve check, per-epoch clearing) do not prevent it — the reserve check is exactly what converts the accounting gap into a permanent freeze rather than an overpay-out.

### Recommendation
In `defaultPendingClaimBasis()`, include the full unfunded instant basis (track unfunded instant basis across all epochs, or key `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` inclusion by the aggregate `pendingInstantWithdraws` rather than only `epochNumber`). In `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`, clear every epoch bucket contributing to a user's unfunded instant basis (iterate/settle `instantWithdrawsRequests` fully rather than only `defaultEpoch`), or conservatively route any still-unfunded residual `instantWithdrawsRequests` through `defaultRecoveryPrice` instead of the at-par funded path.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork PoC outline (mirroring `test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol` helpers):
1. Deposit AA for `userA`; manager `startEpoch` (epoch N-1).
2. `userA` calls `requestInstantWithdraw(X)`; borrower returns only part of funds → `collectInstantWithdrawFunds(X - shortfall)`; `pendingInstantWithdraws == shortfall` with `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[userA][N-1] == X`.
3. `stopEpoch` → `epochNumber` becomes N; `userB` makes a normal `requestWithdraw` in epoch N.
4. Borrower defaults; manager/owner finalize via `finalizeDefaultRecovery(recovered, source)`.
5. Assert: `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` excluded `userA`'s `X`; `defaultRecoveryPrice` equals `reserve/(basisWithout_X)` instead of `reserve/(basisWith_X)`.
6. `userA.claimInstantWithdrawRequest()` reverts in `_transferFundedClaim` (balance == reserve, reserve != 0) → permanent freeze.
7. `userB.claimWithdrawRequest()` succeeds at the inflated price; a subsequent claimant's `_transferDefaultRecovery` underflows → DoS freeze of remaining recovery.

Uncertainty: I could not fully verify the IdleCDO-side calls that sequence `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` and finalization within the remaining budget, but the strategy-side accounting asymmetry (aggregate `pendingInstantWithdraws` vs. per-epoch buckets in `defaultPendingClaimBasis` and `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`) is directly visible in the cited code.
