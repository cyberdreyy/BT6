### Title
Reward Sniping via Flash Deposit/Withdraw Around `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Share-Value Snapshot - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
The OpenQ H-3 finding shows a pattern where a payout is computed from an instantaneous balance/share snapshot, and an attacker can move capital in and out around that snapshot to capture value disproportionate to their real, time-weighted contribution. Marginfi's permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction has the same structural weakness: it injects tokens directly into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` for every share outstanding **at that exact instant**, with no minimum holding period, deposit cap check, or time-weighting to prevent a flash deposit right before the injection followed by an immediate withdrawal right after.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless (any signer can call it) and mutates `bank.asset_share_value` based on `total_asset_shares` read at call time: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

The new share value is `updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares`, where `total_asset_shares` is whatever shares exist the moment the instruction executes — including shares freshly minted seconds earlier by a `lending_account_deposit` in the same or a preceding transaction. There is no lockup, minimum deposit duration, or time-weighted accrual comparable to the `deposit_warmup_period` / `withdrawal_cooldown_period` seen in the Kamino Farms integration that marginfi wraps but does not itself replicate for its own share-based accounting: [3](#0-2) 

This mirrors the OpenQ root cause: a pro-rata payout keyed off a single-block snapshot of participation, with no restriction preventing participants from entering/exiting solely to game that snapshot. Tests confirm the mechanism works exactly as described — depositing capital and then having emissions injected re-prices `asset_share_value` proportionally to shares held at deposit time: [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
An attacker (or a third party who is not the actual campaign sponsor) can:
1. Observe or trigger an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call for a bank (this is permissionless, so anyone, including an unrelated party crediting emissions to genuine depositors, can be front-run).
2. Immediately before it lands, deposit a large amount `D` via `lending_account_deposit`, minting shares `d` at the pre-injection `asset_share_value`.
3. Let the emissions deposit of amount `E` execute, which raises `asset_share_value` for the whole pool including the attacker's freshly minted `d` shares.
4. Immediately withdraw all of `d`'s value via `lending_account_withdraw`.

Because the payout is proportional to instantaneous share ownership rather than time-weighted deposits, the attacker captures a portion of `E` roughly equal to `d/(S+d)` (their share of the enlarged pool) with zero holding-period risk, diluting the emissions that should have accrued to genuine long-term depositors. This is a durable, repeatable value-redirection vector against any incentive/emissions campaign funded through this instruction, and it applies to every bank since the instruction is globally permissionless with no per-bank restriction beyond basic mint/transfer-hook checks.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-high: `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` are ordinary permissionless user instructions with no cooldown, and `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is itself callable by anyone, including the attacker, making the "front-run a third party's emissions deposit" scenario opportunistic and low-cost (bounded mainly by whatever capital is needed to dominate `total_asset_shares` at the moment of injection, and gas/priority fees to land the sandwich). The attack requires no privileged role, no oracle manipulation, and no assumptions beyond normal deposit/withdraw availability.

### Recommendation
Introduce a minimum holding/lock period for shares minted immediately before an `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call, or switch to a time-weighted (accrual-based) distribution model for emissions injected this way, similar to the `deposit_warmup_period`/`withdrawal_cooldown_period` pattern used by the underlying Kamino Farms integration. Alternatively, restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to a trusted emissions admin role and require it to be batched with a snapshot taken at a fixed, unpredictable time (e.g., previous slot hash) rather than the current instruction's on-chain state, and/or add a same-block/same-transaction deposit-then-withdraw guard.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (built on the existing test helpers `try_bank_deposit`, `try_emissions_deposit`, `try_bank_withdraw` used in `emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value`): [5](#0-4) 
1. Attacker deposits a large amount `D` into `usdc_bank` via `try_bank_deposit`, minting shares `d` at pre-injection `asset_share_value`.
2. A (possibly unrelated/third-party) caller invokes `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` with amount `E`, which recomputes `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets + E) / total_asset_shares` using the now-inflated `total_asset_shares` that includes `d`: [6](#0-5) 
3. Attacker immediately calls `try_bank_withdraw` for the full `d` shares, redeeming `d * new_share_value`, capturing `≈ E * D/(A+D)` of the injected emissions while having held capital in the pool for only the span of two transactions, at the expense of the pool's genuine longer-term depositors who would otherwise have received a larger proportional share of `E`.

I was unable to fully verify (due to no further tool calls being available) whether a bank-level deposit cap (`deposit_limit` referenced in `type-crate/src/types/bank_config.rs` and `programs/marginfi/src/state/bank.rs`) is enforced inside `lending_account_deposit` in a way that would meaningfully cap the size of `D` an attacker could inject in a single transaction, which would bound (but not eliminate) the severity of this analog.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);

    let depositor_a_state = depositor_a.load().await;
    let depositor_b_state = depositor_b.load().await;

    let depositor_a_shares = depositor_a_state
        .lending_account
        .balances
        .iter()
        .find(|b| b.is_active() && b.bank_pk == usdc_bank.key)
        .unwrap()
        .asset_shares;
    let depositor_b_shares = depositor_b_state
        .lending_account
        .balances
        .iter()
        .find(|b| b.is_active() && b.bank_pk == usdc_bank.key)
        .unwrap()
        .asset_shares;

    let depositor_a_assets = bank_after
        .get_asset_amount(I80F48::from(depositor_a_shares))?
        .to_num::<u64>();
    let depositor_b_assets = bank_after
        .get_asset_amount(I80F48::from(depositor_b_shares))?
        .to_num::<u64>();

    assert_eq_with_tolerance!(
        depositor_a_assets as i64,
        native!(60, "USDC") as i64,
        native!(1, "USDC") as i64
    );
    assert_eq_with_tolerance!(
        depositor_b_assets as i64,
        native!(90, "USDC") as i64,
        native!(1, "USDC") as i64
    );

    Ok(())
}
