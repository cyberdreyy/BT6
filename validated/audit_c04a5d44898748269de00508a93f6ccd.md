### Title
Solend integration never claims Solend liquidity-mining/reward-token accruals on its wrapped cToken position, permanently forfeiting yield and diluting other Solend depositors - (File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/solend/deposit.rs, programs/marginfi/src/instructions/solend/withdraw.rs)

### Summary
The external report describes Compound V2's `Position` contract, which supplies/borrows into Compound markets and holds the resulting `cTokens`, but never calls `claimComp()` to harvest the COMP rewards those `cTokens` accrue — permanently locking yield and diluting other market participants' rewards. Marginfi's Solend integration reproduces the identical pattern: the bank's `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA owns a Solend obligation holding Solend cTokens, but unlike every other integration in this codebase (Kamino, Drift, Juplend), there is no harvest/claim instruction wired up for Solend-side incentive rewards.

### Finding Description
Marginfi's Solend adapter deposits the raw underlying token into a Solend reserve via CPI and has the `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA act as the Solend obligation owner, holding the resulting collateral (cTokens) on behalf of all wrapped-bank depositors: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

The Solend reserve/collateral-supply accounts are explicitly annotated in the codebase as "the reserve's mint for cTokens" and "the reserve's collateral supply account (where cTokens are stored)": [3](#0-2) 

Compare this to the two other integrations that hold reward-bearing external-protocol tokens on the bank's behalf, both of which have a dedicated permissionless harvest path that sweeps accrued incentive tokens to the protocol's global fee wallet for eventual distribution:
- Kamino: `kamino_harvest_reward` CPIs into Kamino Farms' `harvest_reward` and forwards the harvested tokens to the `global_fee_wallet`'s canonical ATA. [4](#0-3) 
- Drift: `drift_harvest_reward` is documented as "(permissionless) Harvest rewards from admin deposits in Drift spot markets." [5](#0-4) 
- Juplend: rewards are same-mint and captured automatically through `token_exchange_price` appreciation, so no separate claim is needed at all. [6](#0-5) 

No equivalent `solend_harvest_reward` (or any Solend claim/harvest instruction) exists anywhere in the program — a repository-wide search for `pub fn solend_` only turns up `solend_deposit`, `solend_withdraw`, and `solend_init_obligation`; there is no harvest counterpart. Solend reserves can and do run liquidity-mining reward campaigns (reward-mint emissions tied to a reserve's obligation deposits), analogous to Compound's COMP rewards on `cTokens`. Because marginfi's obligation (owned by the `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA) is a normal Solend obligation participating in the reserve, it is eligible for any such reward campaign the same way a Compound `cToken` balance is eligible for COMP — but marginfi has no code path to call Solend's claim/harvest instruction and sweep the proceeds anywhere. The rewards accrue to the obligation and are never harvested, exactly mirroring the reported bug class.

### Impact Explanation
Any Solend reserve wrapped by a marginfi bank that has (or later starts) a liquidity-mining rewards campaign results in permanent, unrecoverable loss of that reward stream for marginfi's depositors and dilutes the reward pool for other real Solend users, since marginfi's obligation share of the reserve's deposits still counts toward reward-eligibility denominators without ever withdrawing its portion. This is a durable, protocol-wide loss-of-yield issue with direct financial effect, matching the "certain and total loss of yield" rationale that justified High severity in the original report.

### Likelihood Explanation
This requires no privileged action or unusual conditions — it triggers automatically whenever a group admin lists a Solend reserve as a wrapped bank and that reserve carries (or begins carrying) a Solend rewards campaign. It is a structural gap in the integration rather than an edge case, and it is trivially reachable by ordinary use of `solend_deposit` documented and tested in the repo (e.g., `tests/specs/solend/sl05_solendMrgnBank.spec.ts`, `tests/specs/solend/sl06_solendMrgnDeposits.spec.ts`). The main uncertainty is whether Solend reserves configured for marginfi in practice currently run active reward campaigns; I could not confirm this from the indexed code/docs, since Solend's own reward-campaign metadata lives off marginfi's repo. Regardless, the program provides no mechanism at all to claim such rewards if/when they exist, unlike its sibling integrations.

### Recommendation
Add a permissionless `solend_harvest_reward` instruction, mirroring `kamino_harvest_reward`/`drift_harvest_reward`, that CPIs into Solend's reward-claim/harvest instruction (if Solend exposes one for the obligation/reserve) using the `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA as signer, and sweeps any claimed reward tokens to the `global_fee_wallet`'s canonical ATA for later fair distribution to depositors, consistent with how Kamino Farms rewards are already handled.

### Proof of Concept
1. Group admin adds a Solend-wrapped bank for a reserve mint using `lending_pool_add_bank_solend` / `solend_init_obligation`. [7](#0-6) 
2. Users deposit into the bank via `solend_deposit`, causing the `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA to become the owner of a growing Solend obligation holding cTokens. [8](#0-7) 
3. Assume the underlying Solend reserve has an active liquidity-mining reward campaign (Solend supports reward-mint emissions on reserves, analogous to Compound's COMP emissions on cTokens).
4. Rewards accrue to the obligation owned by `liquidity_vault_authority` over time.
5. No instruction in the marginfi program (`pub fn solend_*` in `programs/marginfi/src/lib.rs`) exists to claim/harvest these rewards — unlike `kamino_harvest_reward` and `drift_harvest_reward` which exist for their respective integrations.
6. The accrued Solend rewards remain permanently locked/unclaimed in the reward-program's accounting for the obligation, representing forfeited yield for marginfi depositors and diluted rewards for the wider Solend reserve's other depositors.

pub fn kamino_harvest_reward(

## Juplend Rewards

### Key Behavior

- Rewards are **same-mint rewards**, not a second incentive token. For a USDC lending pool, rewards
  are USDC-denominated and realized through a higher `token_exchange_price` (fToken -> underlying),
  similar to interest earnings, not through separate token transfers, a claim portal, etc.
- In mrgn-wrapped banks, users earn rewards implicitly by holding wrapped Juplend collateral. The
  user doesn't do anything: as `token_exchange_price` rises, their collateral value in mrgn rises.
