### Title
Missing bank-binding constraint on `user_state`/`farm_state` in `KaminoHarvestReward` allows cross-bank/cross-obligation substitution - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/kamino/harvest_reward.rs`)

### Summary
The `KaminoHarvestReward` account struct declares `user_state` and `farm_state` as plain `UncheckedAccount`s with no `has_one`/`constraint` tying them to the calling bank's registered obligation (`bank.integration_acc_2`) or registered farm state, unlike sibling Kamino instructions. This lets a permissionless caller invoke `kamino_harvest_reward` for `bank = B` while supplying an arbitrary `user_state`/`farm_state` pair that does not actually belong to bank B's Kamino integration.

### Finding Description
`KaminoHarvestReward` only validates: the bank's `asset_tag` [1](#0-0) , the `fee_state` PDA and `destination_token_account`'s ATA ownership by the global fee wallet [2](#0-1) , and the `liquidity_vault_authority` PDA derived from `bank` [3](#0-2) . Neither `user_state` nor `farm_state` is checked against `bank.integration_acc_2` (the bank's own obligation/user_state, as used consistently by the off-chain derivation helper in `p0-cli`) or against the bank's registered `reserve_farm_state` [4](#0-3) . Compare this to the off-chain tooling, which explicitly derives `user_state` from `bank.integration_acc_2` [5](#0-4) , confirming that binding is expected but is not enforced on-chain. The CPI is signed by `liquidity_vault_authority` derived purely from the `bank` key [6](#0-5) , and the subsequent `transfer_checked` from `user_reward_ata` to `destination_token_account` is also authorized by that same PDA [7](#0-6) .

### Impact Explanation
This is a real gap relative to the invariant the question describes (PDA_BINDING: obligation/`user_state` must bind to the exact bank), and it diverges from patterns elsewhere in the codebase where `integration_acc_2` is checked. However, I could not verify the actual severity of exploitation because the underlying Kamino Farms program logic (`kamino_mocks::kamino_farms`) is not present/indexed in this repo — I was unable to confirm whether Kamino's own `harvest_reward` instruction independently enforces that the `user_reward_token_account` destination must belong to the obligation owner recorded in `user_state`. If Kamino enforces that binding internally (which the test file's "wrong destination token account owner" negative test suggests happens at the SPL/ATA level for `destination_token_account`), a mismatched `user_state` from a different bank/farm would likely cause the CPI or the final `transfer_checked` (whose `authority` must match the actual token account owner) to fail, since `user_reward_ata` must be owned by the calling bank's `liquidity_vault_authority` for the marginfi-side transfer to succeed. I cannot confirm this with certainty since the mock/real Kamino Farms source was not accessible via the tools available.

### Likelihood Explanation
Uncertain — contingent on unverified downstream Kamino behavior. The missing on-chain constraint is confirmed as a fact in this file, but whether it is exploitable to redirect real economic value depends on Kamino Farms' own authorization logic, which I could not inspect.

### Recommendation
Regardless of downstream enforcement, add explicit constraints so marginfi does not rely solely on a third-party program for this invariant: e.g. `constraint = user_state.key() == bank.load()?.integration_acc_2 @ MarginfiError::...` and `constraint = farm_state.key() == <bank's registered farm state> @ MarginfiError::...`, mirroring the pattern used for `integration_acc_1`/`integration_acc_2` in `kamino_withdraw`/`kamino_deposit`.

### Proof of Concept
Not conclusively reproducible without access to the real/mocked Kamino Farms CPI validation logic. A test plan would need to: set up two Kamino banks A and B with distinct farm states/obligations, call `kamino_harvest_reward(bank=B, user_state=<A's obligation state>, farm_state=<A's farm state>)`, and assert whether the transaction succeeds (bug) or fails due to Kamino-side or SPL-token-side ownership mismatch (no bug). This requires source access to `kamino_mocks::kamino_farms` that was not available in this session.

**Caveat**: Due to index/tooling limits, the `kamino_mocks` crate source was not retrievable, so the ultimate exploitability (whether Kamino's own program blocks this) is unverified. I recommend a Devin session with full repo/filesystem access to inspect `kamino_mocks::kamino_farms::cpi` and write the described PoC test to confirm or refute actual fund redirection.
