### Title
`stopEpoch` validates and accounts withdraw fees against a stale `pendingWithdrawFees` snapshot taken before `prepareStopEpochWithApr0` mutates it - (`contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol`)

### Summary
CVE-2017-9047 is a bounds check performed with a stale length: `xmlSnprintfElementContent` appends `content->prefix` to a buffer but reuses the pre-append `len` when checking whether `content->name` fits, letting the second write overflow. The same bug class exists in `IdleCDOEpochVariant._stopEpoch`: `pendingWithdrawFees` is snapshotted into the local `_pendingWithdrawFees` at the top of the function, then `IdleCreditVault.prepareStopEpochWithApr0` increases the real obligation by `_apr0Fee` and `pendingWithdraws` by `_apr0NetInterest`, yet all subsequent checks and fee math continue to use the stale local. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
In `_stopEpoch`:

1. `uint256 _pendingWithdrawFees = pendingWithdrawFees;` is captured before any APR0 settlement.
2. The sanity check `(_interest > 1 && (_interest < _pendingWithdrawFees || _newApr != 0))` is documented as ensuring overridden interest is "greater than pending withdraw fees ... otherwise withdrawal requests may not be fullfilled." It passes on the pre-append value.
3. `prepareStopEpochWithApr0(_interest)` then appends: `pendingWithdraws += _apr0NetInterest`, `_adjPendingWithdrawFees += _apr0Fee`, and `apr0RateByEpoch[epochNumber]` is stored — the exact analog of appending `prefix` while `len` is stale.
4. Downstream, the stale `_pendingWithdrawFees` is still used for `_availableForFees` and `_totalFees`/`netInterest`: [3](#0-2) 

The suffix `_pendingWithdraws` is re-read after settlement (line 364), but `_pendingWithdrawFees` is not — the invariant "`_interest` covers all pending withdraw fees" is therefore validated against a value that no longer reflects the fees actually owed.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged KYC'd lender can create the drift: during an epoch where `unscaledApr == 0`, they call `requestWithdraw` (routed into `_requestWithdrawApr0`, growing `apr0TotalPrincipal`). At the next `stopEpoch` with an `_interest > 1` override, `prepareStopEpochWithApr0` adds `_apr0Fee` to the adjusted fees, so:

- The guard at line 348 is satisfied even though actual adjusted fees exceed `_interest`, defeating its stated purpose of guaranteeing receipt funding.
- `lastEpochInterest = _grossInterest - _fees - _pendingWithdrawFees` is overstated by the APR0 fee delta. On the next `startEpoch`, `_toSend = lastEpochInterest + _totEpochDeposits` over-demands underlying from the strategy balance; if the strategy lacks the excess, `sendInterestAndDeposits` reverts inside `startEpoch` with no try/catch, blocking epoch start and temporarily freezing all depositor funds until donations or deposits replenish the gap. Quantified loss is bounded by the cumulative `_apr0Fee` across APR0 requesters (`apr0TotalPrincipal * pro-rata share * fee / FULL_ALLOC`).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an APR0 epoch configuration (`unscaledApr == 0` while requests are open) plus a manager `stopEpoch` call using the `_interest > 1` override path. The attacker-side action (requesting a withdraw during an APR0 epoch) is permissionless for any allowed wallet; no privileged collusion is needed to create the stale-check gap, though the magnitude is fee-scaled rather than principal-sized.

### Recommendation
Re-read `pendingWithdrawFees` after `prepareStopEpochWithApr0` (or move the `_interest` sufficiency check after the call) and replace all uses of the stale local with the returned `_adjPendingWithdrawFees`, mirroring how `_pendingWithdraws` is already refreshed at line 364:

```solidity
(_expectedInterest, _pendingWithdrawFees) = _strategy.prepareStopEpochWithApr0(_interest);
uint256 _pendingWithdraws = _strategy.pendingWithdraws();
// perform the _interest > 1 sufficiency check against _pendingWithdrawFees here
```

### Proof of Concept
A Foundry fork test would: deploy `IdleCDOEpochVariant` + `IdleCreditVault` with `disableInstantWithdraw = true` and `unscaledApr == 0`; have two allowed wallets deposit AA/BB and then `requestWithdraw` during the buffer (populating `apr0TotalPrincipal`); call `stopEpoch(0, interestOverride)` where `interestOverride` equals the pre-settlement `pendingWithdrawFees` but is below `pendingWithdrawFees + apr0Fee`; assert the stale check passes while adjusted fees exceed the override, and that `lastEpochInterest` is overstated by `apr0Fee`, causing the subsequent `startEpoch` to revert on `sendInterestAndDeposits`. A residual uncertainty: whether `startEpoch` actually reverts depends on leftover underlying in the strategy partially absorbing the over-demand, which a full fork PoC would need to confirm.
