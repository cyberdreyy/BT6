### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` reward surge can be front-run and drained by flash/JIT depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a **permissionless** instruction that transfers `amount` of the bank's mint directly into the bank's liquidity vault and instantly re-prices `bank.asset_share_value` for every existing depositor, exactly the "pull-based reward surge" pattern described in the referenced BkdLocker finding. Because marginfi has **no withdrawal delay, lockup, or vesting** on lending deposits/withdrawals, an attacker can deposit immediately before this call lands and withdraw immediately after, capturing a pro-rata share of the newly injected rewards that was intended for pre-existing depositors — with none of the mitigating friction ("`_WITHDRAW_DELAY`") that the original report relied on to downgrade severity.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share value as `total_assets_after / total_asset_shares`, i.e. a single lump-sum reward that is instantly and proportionally distributed to *whoever holds shares at the moment the instruction executes*: [1](#0-0) 

The instruction is explicitly documented and implemented as callable by any signer (`depositor: Signer<'info>`, no `has_one = admin` or elevated authority constraint): [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

Tests confirm the exact "surge" mechanics: total shares stay constant while `asset_share_value` jumps by `1 + emissions_deposit / total_deposited`, and this multiplier applies uniformly and immediately to every existing share, including shares minted moments earlier: [4](#0-3) 

Standard deposits (`lending_account_deposit`) and withdrawals (`lending_account_withdraw`) have no cooldown, lockup, or vesting period — a user can deposit and then withdraw (even `withdraw_all`) in a subsequent instruction/transaction as soon as the health check passes: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

This is structurally identical to the BkdLocker bug: `depositFees()` (surge deposit, no vesting) + a configurable-to-zero `_WITHDRAW_DELAY` enabled the front-run. In marginfi, the "withdraw delay" is not merely configurable to zero — it **does not exist at all** for standard lending banks, so the analog is strictly more exploitable than the original finding (the only friction is the risk-engine health check on withdraw, which a fully-collateralized same-asset deposit/withdraw trivially satisfies, and the optional group rate limiter, which is off by default and only throttles large USD-denominated outflows rather than preventing this attack pattern).

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who front-runs (or simply co-locates with) a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank right before the emissions deposit lands.
2. Let the emissions deposit execute, instantly inflating `asset_share_value` pro-rata to their (now large) share of `total_asset_shares`.
3. Withdraw immediately afterward, extracting a share of the reward proportional to their capital, having taken on no real economic exposure to the bank beyond the brief window.

This directly dilutes the emissions/rewards that legitimate long-term depositors were meant to receive, redirecting protocol-funded incentives (or third-party reward top-ups) to opportunistic capital. Financial effect is a durable, quantifiable loss of yield for genuine depositors and value extraction by the attacker — the same impact class judged Medium in the original report, but here the mitigating control (withdrawal delay) is absent by default, making the attack systematically easier to execute (any depositor/withdraw pair, no special configuration needed).

### Likelihood Explanation
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless and used in production flows (protocol emissions funding, third-party reward campaigns) as documented in the changelog: [7](#0-6) 
Any observer watching the transaction mempool/leader schedule for this call (or for scheduled/expected emissions funding) can trivially construct a deposit→(wait for target tx)→withdraw sequence. No admin privilege, no special account state, and no protocol misconfiguration is required — only capital and the ability to sequence transactions around the emissions deposit, which is a standard MEV/JIT-liquidity pattern on Solana.

### Recommendation
Do not apply reward top-ups as an instantaneous, lump-sum increase to `asset_share_value`. Instead, vest/stream the `amount` injected by `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` linearly over some time window (a rate-based accrual similar to Synthetix's `StakingRewards`), so that JIT depositors cannot capture a disproportionate share of a single-block reward injection. At minimum, consider requiring a minimum holding period (time-weighted share accounting) before a deposit is eligible to receive a proportional share of a same-block/same-slot emissions top-up.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has `total_asset_shares = S`, `asset_share_value = V`, and `total_assets = S*V`.
2. Some party (protocol/team/anyone) submits `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=R)` to reward existing depositors of `B`.
3. Attacker observes this pending transaction and submits, ahead of it, `lending_account_deposit` into `B` with amount `D`, sized so the attacker's shares become a large fraction of `S' = S + D/V`.
4. The emissions deposit lands: `asset_share_value` becomes `V' = (S*V + D + R) / S'`, uniformly applied to all shares including the attacker's just-minted shares — see the exact multiplier computation validated in [8](#0-7) .
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` with `withdraw_all=true`, redeeming `attacker_shares * V'`, which includes a share of `R` proportional to `D/(S*V+D)` despite having held the position for effectively zero time.
6. Legitimate long-term depositors of `B` receive a correspondingly smaller share of `R` than intended.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

    bank.update_bank_cache(&group)?;

    msg!(
        "Deposited {} same-bank emissions into liquidity vault",
        amount
    );

    Ok(())
}

#[derive(Accounts)]
pub struct LendingAccountWithdraw<'info> {
    #[account(
        constraint = (
            !group.load()?.is_protocol_paused()
            || marginfi_account.load()?.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_DELEVERAGE)
        ) @ MarginfiError::ProtocolPaused
    )]
    pub group: AccountLoader<'info, MarginfiGroup>,

    #[account(
        mut,
        has_one = group @ MarginfiError::InvalidGroup,
        constraint = {
            let acc = marginfi_account.load()?;
            !acc.get_flag(ACCOUNT_DISABLED)
        } @MarginfiError::AccountDisabled,
        constraint = {
            let a = marginfi_account.load()?;
            account_not_frozen_for_authority(&a, authority.key())
        } @ MarginfiError::AccountFrozen,
        constraint = {
            let a = marginfi_account.load()?;
            let g = group.load()?;
            is_signer_authorized(&a, g.admin, authority.key(), true, true)
        } @ MarginfiError::Unauthorized
    )]
    pub marginfi_account: AccountLoader<'info, MarginfiAccount>,

    /// Must be marginfi_account's authority, unless in liquidation/deleverage receivership or order execution
    ///
    /// Note: during receivership and order execution, there are no signer checks whatsoever: any key can repay as
    /// long as the invariants checked at the end of execution are met.
    pub authority: Signer<'info>,

    #[account(
        mut,
        has_one = group @ MarginfiError::InvalidGroup,
        has_one = liquidity_vault @ MarginfiError::InvalidLiquidityVault,
        constraint = is_marginfi_asset_tag(bank.load()?.config.asset_tag)
            @ MarginfiError::WrongAssetTagForStandardInstructions,
        // We want to block withdraw of assets with no weight (e.g. isolated) otherwise the
        // liquidator can just take all of them and the user gets nothing back, which is unfair. For
        // assets with any nominal weight, e.g. 10%, caveat emptor
        constraint = {
            let a = marginfi_account.load()?;
            let b = bank.load()?;
            let weight: I80F48 = b.config.asset_weight_init.into();
            !(a.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP) && weight == I80F48::ZERO)
        } @MarginfiError::LiquidationPremiumTooHigh
    )]
    pub bank: AccountLoader<'info, Bank>,

    #[account(mut)]
    pub destination_token_account: InterfaceAccount<'info, TokenAccount>,

    /// CHECK: Seed constraint check
    #[account(
        seeds = [
            LIQUIDITY_VAULT_AUTHORITY_SEED.as_bytes(),
            bank.key().as_ref(),
        ],
        bump = bank.load()?.liquidity_vault_authority_bump,
    )]
    pub bank_liquidity_vault_authority: UncheckedAccount<'info>,

    #[account(mut)]
    pub liquidity_vault: InterfaceAccount<'info, TokenAccount>,

    pub token_program: Interface<'info, TokenInterface>,
}
