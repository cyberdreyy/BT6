### Title
Stale `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` inflates default claim basis and phantom prefunded reserve — (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The Redis CVE is an integer overflow in a size computation: a length is computed too small, then a larger amount of data is written through it, corrupting shared state. The analog in this codebase is `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`, the per-epoch aggregate of instant-withdraw claim basis. It is incremented in `requestInstantWithdraw` (line 372) and decremented only in `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` (line 853). The normal funded-claim path `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` (lines 387–392) clears `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` but never decrements `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` or `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`. The per-user receipt is settled while the aggregate "size" stays inflated — the same shape as an under-accounted allocation that later over-consumes shared recovery funds.

### Finding Description
At `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, two computations consume `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]`:

- `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` (lines 644–649) adds the *full* current-epoch bucket to `pendingBasis` whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0`, inflating `totalBasis`.
- `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve()` (lines 716–723) adds `instantBasis - pendingInstant` to `reserveAmount`, i.e. it counts already-paid-out-and-claimed instant funds as still-held reserve.

If a user requested an instant withdraw in the defaulted epoch, had it funded via `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, and already claimed it, their basis remains in `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`. When the same epoch later defaults with at least one other instant receipt still unfunded (`pendingInstantWithdraws != 0`), finalization computes:

```
recoveryPrice = (realReserve + phantomClaimed) / (realBasis + phantomClaimed)
```

The numerator is phantom — those underlyings already left the strategy via `_transferFundedClaim`. Since real `reserveAmount/totalBasis < 1`, adding the same phantom `X` to both pushes `recoveryPrice` toward 1: every receipt claims at a higher ratio than the real reserve backs. `defaultRecoveryReserve` is then an accounting figure larger than the strategy's actual balance.

### Impact Explanation
Early claimants (including the attacker, who can hold a defaulted-epoch pending withdraw receipt via `requestWithdraw` or an unfunded instant receipt) call `claimWithdrawRequest`/`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` and are paid `claimBasis * inflatedPrice`, extracting more underlying than their fair recovery share. `_transferDefaultRecovery` decrements the inflated reserve while transferring real tokens; once the actual balance is exhausted, all remaining honest claimants' claims revert on `safeTransfer` — permanent freezing of their recovery funds, or direct theft of them by earlier claimants. Quantification: the phantom amount equals all instant claims funded and claimed during the defaulted epoch, which can be a large fraction of epoch TVL.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: instant withdraws enabled (manager sets `instantWithdrawDelay`/`allowInstantWithdraw`), at least one funded+claimed instant receipt and at least one still-unfunded instant receipt in the defaulted epoch, and a borrower default finalized with `finalizeDefaultRecovery`. Attacker needs only a KYC'd tranche position and normal request/claim calls — all unprivileged. The stale-bucket divergence also silently poisons `defaultPendingClaimBasis` for any epoch where a funded instant claim occurred, so no exotic setup is needed beyond the standard instant flow.

Caveat: I could not fully verify in this iteration the exact CDO-side gating (`allowInstantWithdraw`, `instantWithdrawDeadline`) that permits a funded `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` within the same `epochNumber` that later defaults, nor whether the prefunded variant uses this same path. If funded instant claims are only possible after `epochNumber` has advanced past the request epoch, the stale bucket would not collide with the defaulted-epoch bucket and the finding would not hold — this is the key assumption to confirm in the PoC.

### Recommendation
In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, decrement `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epoch]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]` for funded claims (tracking each receipt's request epoch), symmetric to the decrement in `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`. Alternatively, stop deriving "prefunded" reserve from the aggregate bucket and track an explicit funded-instant counter that is decremented on every claim path.
