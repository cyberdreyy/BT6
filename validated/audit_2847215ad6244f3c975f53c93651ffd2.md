## Finding

### Title
Reward/donation dilution via front-run stake before `ping` epoch reward distribution - (File: `staking-pool/src/internal.rs`)

### Summary
`internal_ping` distributes any balance increase detected between epochs (validator rewards, gas-fee rebates, or an intentional/accidental NEAR transfer to the pool account) pro-rata across whatever `total_stake_shares` happen to exist at the moment `ping` is executed, with no time-weighting or snapshot of who actually held those shares while the reward accrued. This is the exact same class of bug as the Arcadia `donateToTranche` dilution finding: value earned by existing depositors over an epoch can be diluted by anyone who deposits/stakes immediately before the reward is recognized and unstakes immediately after.

### Finding Description
The pool tracks reward via a simple balance delta: [1](#0-0) 

`total_reward = total_balance - self.last_total_balance` is computed once per epoch transition (first call to any pool method after epoch change), and the resulting `remaining_reward` is added to `total_staked_balance`, which increases the value of every currently outstanding "stake" share, regardless of when those shares were minted relative to when the reward accrued.

The contract's own documentation confirms this covers not just validator rewards but arbitrary transfers into the account: [2](#0-1) 

`deposit_and_stake` mints new shares at the *current* price before the next `ping` fires: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because `internal_ping` only distributes when `env::epoch_height()` differs from `self.last_epoch_height`, an attacker can:
1. Observe (or trigger by directly transferring NEAR to the pool account) an impending reward/"donation".
2. Call `deposit_and_stake` in the *current* epoch (before anyone calls `ping` for the next epoch), minting stake shares at the pre-reward price — this call's own `internal_ping()` is a no-op since the epoch hasn't changed yet.
3. Once the epoch turns over, the next call to `ping`/`deposit`/`stake`/`unstake` by anyone distributes the accumulated `total_reward` across `total_stake_shares`, which now includes the attacker's freshly minted shares.
4. The attacker calls `unstake`/`unstake_all`, which immediately fixes their claim at the post-reward share price: [5](#0-4) 
then withdraws after the mandatory unlock period.

The binding broken is: reward entitlement should equal time-weighted contribution to the pool during the period the reward accrued, but the contract equates it to instantaneous share count at the single, non-atomic moment `ping` executes. An account holding shares for a few blocks captures the same pro-rata cut of an epoch's (or a donor's) reward as an account that held shares the entire period, diluting the return of genuine long-term delegators — the same mechanism validated as Medium in the referenced Arcadia `donateToTranche` report.

### Impact Explanation
This is a rewards mis-attribution: value that should accrue to existing delegators for the risk/time they held stake is instead captured by an opportunistic staker who contributes capital only momentarily around the reward-recognition event. This matches the "rewards or fees mis-attributed" High-impact category, since another party (the diluted delegators) settles on the resulting `total_staked_balance` figure as the source of truth for their entitlement.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger requires only: (a) an epoch boundary (which happens routinely, roughly every ~12h on NEAR) or a visible incoming transfer to the pool account, and (b) the attacker being first to call a pool method after the epoch changes. No permissioned role is required — `deposit_and_stake`, `unstake`, and `ping` are all open, unprivileged entry points, and mempool observation is sufficient to front-run a pending donation or to simply front-run the epoch's own automatically-accrued validator reward. The only cost to the attacker is temporarily locking capital and observing the standard unstake delay, not fundamentally different from the capital risk accepted in the original Sherlock finding.

### Recommendation
Distribute rewards based on a time-weighted or snapshot mechanism instead of instantaneous share balances captured at the single `internal_ping` call — e.g., checkpoint share balances at the start of each epoch and only allow rewards accrued during an epoch to be claimed by shares that existed for that entire epoch, or require a minimum staking duration before newly staked shares participate in an already-accrued-but-undistributed reward.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool has delegators A and B, each with `stake_shares` proportional to `total_staked_balance` at epoch `N`.
2. At epoch `N`, an attacker calls `deposit_and_stake` with a large amount `X`; since `internal_ping` is a no-op (epoch unchanged), attacker's shares are minted fairly at the current price, `total_stake_shares += num_shares`.
3. Epoch changes to `N+1`; the protocol (or a donor) increases `env::account_locked_balance()`/`env::account_balance()` by `R` (validator reward or direct transfer), per [2](#0-1) .
4. Attacker (or anyone) calls `ping`; `internal_ping` computes `total_reward = R` and adds `remaining_reward` to `total_staked_balance`, increasing the value of all shares including the attacker's, per [6](#0-5) .
5. Attacker calls `unstake_all`, fixing their unstaked balance at the new, reward-inflated share price via `staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down`, per [7](#0-6) , capturing a slice of `R` proportional to `X`, despite having been staked for effectively zero time when `R` accrued.
6. After `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK` epochs, attacker withdraws, having diluted A and B's share of `R`.

```rust
    pub(crate) fn internal_stake(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Staking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        // Calculate the number of "stake" shares that the account will receive for staking the
        // given amount.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "The calculated number of \"stake\" shares received for staking should be positive"
        );
        // The amount of tokens the account will be charged from the unstaked balance.
        // Rounded down to avoid overcharging the account to guarantee that the account can always
        // unstake at least the same amount as staked.
        let charge_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);
        assert!(
            charge_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= charge_amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to stake"
        );
        account.unstaked -= charge_amount;
        account.stake_shares += num_shares;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The staked amount that will be added to the total to guarantee the "stake" share price
        // never decreases. The difference between `stake_amount` and `charge_amount` is paid
        // from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let stake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance += stake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;

        env::log(
            format!(
                "@{} staking {}. Received {} new staking shares. Total {} unstaked balance and {} staking shares",
                account_id, charge_amount, num_shares, account.unstaked, account.stake_shares
            )
                .as_bytes(),
        );
        env::log(
            format!(
                "Contract total staked balance is {}. Total number of shares {}",
                self.total_staked_balance, self.total_stake_shares
            )
            .as_bytes(),
        );
    }
```

```rust
    /// Distributes rewards after the new epoch. It's automatically called before every action.
    /// Returns true if the current epoch height is different from the last epoch height.
    pub(crate) fn internal_ping(&mut self) -> bool {
        let epoch_height = env::epoch_height();
        if self.last_epoch_height == epoch_height {
            return false;
        }
        self.last_epoch_height = epoch_height;

        // New total amount (both locked and unlocked balances).
        // NOTE: We need to subtract `attached_deposit` in case `ping` called from `deposit` call
        // since the attached deposit gets included in the `account_balance`, and we have not
        // accounted it yet.
        let total_balance =
            env::account_locked_balance() + env::account_balance() - env::attached_deposit();

        assert!(
            total_balance >= self.last_total_balance,
            "The new total balance should not be less than the old total balance"
        );
        let total_reward = total_balance - self.last_total_balance;
        if total_reward > 0 {
            // The validation fee that the contract owner takes.
            let owners_fee = self.reward_fee_fraction.multiply(total_reward);

            // Distributing the remaining reward to the delegators first.
            let remaining_reward = total_reward - owners_fee;
            self.total_staked_balance += remaining_reward;

            // Now buying "stake" shares for the contract owner at the new share price.
            let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(owners_fee);
            if num_shares > 0 {
                // Updating owner's inner account
                let owner_id = self.owner_id.clone();
                let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&owner_id);
                account.stake_shares += num_shares;
                self.internal_save_account(&owner_id, &account);
                // Increasing the total amount of "stake" shares.
                self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;
            }
            // Increasing the total staked balance by the owners fee, no matter whether the owner
            // received any shares or not.
            self.total_staked_balance += owners_fee;
```

```rust
    /// Unstakes all staked balance from the inner account of the predecessor.
    /// The new total unstaked balance will be available for withdrawal in four epochs.
    pub fn unstake_all(&mut self) {
        // Unstake action always restakes
        self.internal_ping();

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);
        let amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(account.stake_shares);
        self.inner_unstake(amount);

        self.internal_restake();
    }

    /// Unstakes the given amount from the inner account of the predecessor.
    /// The inner account should have enough staked balance.
    /// The new total unstaked balance will be available for withdrawal in four epochs.
    pub fn unstake(&mut self, amount: U128) {
        // Unstake action always restakes
        self.internal_ping();

        let amount: Balance = amount.into();
        self.inner_unstake(amount);

        self.internal_restake();
```
