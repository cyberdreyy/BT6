Confirmed: `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that instantly increases `bank.asset_share_value` proportionally to `total_asset_shares` at the moment of the call, with no vesting, cooldown, or time-weighting for how long a depositor has held shares. Deposits and withdrawals into the bank are likewise instant with no lockup. This structurally matches the Sherlock M-1 bug class: a lump-sum reward distributed pro-rata to whoever holds shares at distribution time, rather than pro-rata to time-weighted exposure, allowing a short-term depositor to capture full pro-rata rewards.

### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets short-term/opportunistic depositors instantly capture pro-rata rewards meant for long-term depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` [1](#0-0)  is a permissionless instruction that transfers same-mint tokens into a bank's liquidity vault and immediately raises `bank.asset_share_value` for the *entire* pool of `total_asset_shares` outstanding at that instant [2](#0-1) . Because deposits and withdrawals are instantaneous with no lockup or vesting period, a depositor can deposit immediately before this call lands and withdraw immediately after, capturing a full pro-rata share of the reward with effectively zero holding duration — exactly the "new stake gets full reward" dilution pattern described in the referenced report.

### Finding Description
The lump-sum reward mechanic computes the new share value purely from the ratio of `updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares` at the time of the call: [3](#0-2) 
This is identical in structure to `MlumStaking`'s reward-distribution bug: rewards are split among *current* share-holders without regard to how long each holder's shares have been outstanding. There is no accrual queue, no minimum holding period, and no distinction between shares that were present before vs. shares minted moments before the reward lands. `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` mint/burn shares immediately based on the current `asset_share_value` with no time-lock [4](#0-3) , so a user can freely enter right before and exit right after an emissions deposit.

Since `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless (any `depositor` signer can call it, funding from any account they control) [5](#0-4) , this also means third-party reward funders (protocol partners running incentive campaigns via this instruction) create a window: their transaction can be seen (mempool/known schedule) and any user (including bots) can front-run it with a large deposit and immediately capture a pro-rata slice of the deposited reward, exactly like Bob's 1-second stake in the referenced Sherlock bug.

### Impact Explanation
Depositors who hold shares for a long time see their earned reward share diluted by transient, opportunistic large deposits made moments before an emissions/reward top-up. This causes a durable, exploitable value redirection: real economic value (the emissions funder's tokens, intended to reward existing depositors) is siphoned to short-term "flash" depositors, at the expense of genuine long-term lenders.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is permissionless and has no cooldown/time-weighting guard, and normal deposit/withdraw instructions have no lockup, so exploitation only requires visibility into (or foreknowledge of) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call — a realistic assumption for any advertised/scheduled incentive campaign or any observable pending transaction.

### Recommendation
Do not let newly-deposited shares participate at full value in an immediately-following emissions deposit. Either (a) time-weight the reward split by tracking a snapshot of `total_asset_shares` prior to the deposit window and excluding shares minted within that window, or (b) require emissions deposits to queue rewards that vest over a minimum period before affecting `asset_share_value`, similar to the streaming/time-weighted design already used for the airdrop-style `emissions_rate` mechanism described in the EMISSIONS.md guide [6](#0-5) .

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank has depositor A with a long-standing deposit of shares `S_A`, `total_asset_shares = S_A`.
2. Attacker B observes (or knows in advance) that `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` will be called shortly (e.g. a scheduled protocol incentive funding tx).
3. B calls `lending_account_deposit` for a large amount, minting shares `S_B` at the pre-reward `asset_share_value`; now `total_asset_shares = S_A + S_B`.
4. The reward funder calls `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)`; `asset_share_value` increases uniformly for all shares per the formula in `configure_bank.rs` lines 138-146.
5. B immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem `S_B` shares at the new, higher `asset_share_value`, capturing `amount * S_B / (S_A + S_B)` of the reward despite zero time-weighted exposure, diluting A's rightful share.
