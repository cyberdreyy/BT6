### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows last-second deposit/withdraw sandwiching to capture instant, un-earned reward distribution - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
The external report describes an am-AMM bug where an entire epoch's accumulated rent is credited to liquidity providers in a single instantaneous distribution rather than streamed over time, letting an attacker deposit moments before the distribution, capture a full epoch's rewards, and withdraw shortly after. marginfi-v2 has a structurally identical pattern in its permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction, which instantly re-prices `asset_share_value` for all current depositors in one lump-sum step, with no vesting/streaming and no deposit/withdraw cooldown to prevent instant in-and-out capture.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets anyone permissionlessly push same-mint "emissions" tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault, and the entire deposited amount is immediately reflected in `asset_share_value` for all existing shareholders at that instant: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

This is analogous to the am-AMM bug's "burn rent charged" / lump-sum distribution at epoch boundary — in both cases, the whole reward for a period is applied to the *current* share-holder set at a single point in time rather than being distributed pro-rata over the period during which it accrued. Just as an am-AMM depositor could enter seconds before the epoch-end distribution and receive a full epoch's rent, a marginfi user can:
1. Deposit into the target bank immediately before an emissions deposit lands (whether from the group admin, a partner, or any permissionless caller since the instruction has no authority gate beyond `has_one` PDA checks on the bank/mint/vault — see the `LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit` accounts struct).
2. Have `asset_share_value` bumped in their favor in the same or very next transaction, since `bank.total_asset_shares` used for the pro-rata split is read at the moment of the emissions deposit, with no minimum holding period. [3](#0-2) 
3. Withdraw immediately afterward via the standard `lending_account_deposit`/withdraw flow, which has no lockup or cooldown enforced (unlike the epoch-based am-AMM design, marginfi deposits/withdrawals are otherwise instantaneous with only interest-accrual and health-check gating).

The regular interest-accrual path in `bank.accrue_interest` mitigates a similar issue for interest income by continuously streaming `asset_share_value` changes over `time_delta` seconds via the interest-rate model: [4](#0-3) 
but `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` deliberately bypasses this streaming mechanism and applies the entire reward atomically: [5](#0-4) 

### Impact Explanation
Genuine long-term depositors are diluted of a fee/reward stream (emissions) intended to compensate liquidity provision, because opportunistic actors can capture a pro-rata share of any single emissions deposit event with only momentary capital exposure (one deposit + one withdraw, potentially in the same slot/transaction bundle). This mirrors the reported Medium-impact classification: value is redirected away from genuine liquidity providers to short-term capital, with financial effect but no protocol insolvency.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is fully permissionless and requires no special timing restriction to exploit — any actor who can observe (or is themselves) a pending emissions deposit transaction can front-run/sandwich it with a deposit-then-withdraw pair in the same or adjacent transactions since there is no minimum holding period or streaming vesting logic gating the reward.

### Recommendation
Rather than applying the entire emissions deposit atomically to `asset_share_value`, stream it over a fixed vesting window (analogous to fixing the am-AMM report's recommendation to "distribute the epoch's reward over time"), e.g., accrue it gradually similar to how `accrue_interest` streams interest over `time_delta`, or require a minimum bank-balance holding duration before a deposit is eligible to share in a given emissions deposit.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors for (or is the one funding) a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call for `amount` X on bank B.
2. Immediately before that transaction (or in the same block), attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large amount D into bank B, increasing `bank.total_asset_shares`.
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes, computing `updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares`, crediting attacker's newly-minted shares with a slice of X proportional to D/(total shares), despite having provided liquidity for less than one transaction's worth of time — see the share-value repricing at [6](#0-5) .
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem D plus their share of X, exiting with profit and no meaningful capital lock-up.

Note: I was unable to fully verify in this session whether marginfi enforces any same-block/same-slot deposit-withdraw restriction (e.g., via `last_update` checks) that might partially mitigate the same-transaction variant of this attack — the `withdraw.rs` instruction file could not be read in the final iteration due to a tool error. This should be double-checked before treating the PoC as fully unmitigated.
