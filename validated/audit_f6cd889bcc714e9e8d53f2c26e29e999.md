### Title
BB holder escapes junior-first loss waterfall by converting to a withdraw receipt before a loss-bearing `stopEpochWithDuration` - (File: contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol + contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary

A BB (junior) tranche holder who anticipates that the next `stopEpochWithDuration` will realize a loss (`_lossAmount > 0`) can front-run it during the buffer period by calling `requestWithdraw`. This converts their first-loss BB position into an address-bound withdraw receipt. Because `IdleCreditVault` does not store the tranche identity of pending receipts, the pending portion of the loss is socialized pro rata across *all* pending receipts (AA and BB mixed), while only the remaining active-position loss follows the BB-first waterfall. The attacker dilutes their junior loss with AA receipt holders and exits ahead of the loss realization — the same "strip the value before the sale settles" pattern as the Footium club/players report.

### Finding Description

During the buffer period `allowAAWithdrawRequest`/`allowBBWithdrawRequest` are enabled, so any KYC'd holder can call `requestWithdraw` (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:739). Inside `IdleCreditVault.requestWithdraw` the strategy tokens are burned and an equal receipt is minted to the user, while `_amount` (principal + interest − fees) is added to the tranche-agnostic `pendingWithdraws`/`withdrawsRequestsByEpoch` counters (IdleCreditVault.sol:273-294) — no AA/BB identity is stored.

When the manager later calls `stopEpochWithDuration(..., _lossAmount)`, `_stopEpoch` burns `_lossAmount` strategy tokens and applies the documented policy (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:510-514): the pending-receipt portion of the loss is shared **pro rata** via `collectWithdrawFunds` → `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch` (IdleCreditVault.sol:411-420), and only the remaining loss hits active positions through the ordinary `_virtualPriceAux` BB-first waterfall (IdleCDO.sol:409-425). A BB holder who moved into a receipt therefore (a) shares the pending loss pro rata with AA receipts instead of absorbing it fully, and (b) shrinks `lastNAVBB`, reducing the junior buffer protecting active AA holders.

The loss is predictable: `_stopEpoch` pulls repayment from the borrower via `transferFrom`, so the borrower's approve/repayment transactions (or public knowledge of a partial default/`previewLossAdjustedWithdrawFunds` output) reveal the shortfall during the buffer window, before `stopEpochWithDuration` executes.

### Impact Explanation

Broken invariant: loss waterfall (junior absorbs losses before senior). The attacker converts last-loss-priority exposure into pari passu pending claims, shifting loss onto AA receipt holders and reducing protection for remaining AA active positions. Loss is quantified as `attackerReceiptBasis * (1 - lossRecoveryPrice)` avoided vs. the full BB-first absorption they would have borne.

### Likelihood Explanation

Requires a pending loss event known before `stopEpochWithDuration` — realistic for publicly signaled partial defaults or observable borrower funding — and a buffer-period transaction by any tranche holder. No privileged role needed; the receipt is non-transferable (`_transfer` reverts unless called by the CDO, IdleCreditVault.sol:939-942) but that does not stop the legitimate `requestWithdraw` path.

### Recommendation

Store tranche identity per receipt (e.g., `withdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][epoch][tranche]`) and apply the pending loss to BB receipts before AA receipts, or cap/normalize the loss adjustment so pending receipts cannot receive better treatment than the equivalent active tranche position.
