**Reasoning:**

1. **No unprivileged entrypoint exists.** `AggregatorOverriddenStateView::get_state_value` in [1](#0-0)  is purely an internal execution-engine mechanism used only during sharded block execution to stub out the total-supply aggregator value while the real value is proven later; it is not reachable or influenced by any unprivileged transaction, view function, or proof input. It is validator/executor infrastructure, out of scope per the review's requirement that the path begin from unprivileged input.

2. **The premise about `staking_config::calculate_rewards_rate` is factually false.** The actual reward-rate computation, `calculate_and_save_latest_epoch_rewards_rate`, only reads `StakingRewardsConfig` (in-memory rates, decrease rate, period timestamps) and `timestamp::now_seconds()` — it performs `FixedPoint64` math on these fields alone, with no read of total supply or `TOTAL_SUPPLY_STATE_KEY` anywhere: [2](#0-1) . Similarly, `get_reward_rate` reads only `StakingConfig`/`StakingRewardsConfig` fields: [3](#0-2) . There is no dependency on `TOTAL_SUPPLY_AGGR_BASE_VAL` or the sharded executor's total-supply override in this reward-rate computation path, so the described chain (unprivileged delegator → fixed total supply → corrupted reward rate → over/under-minting across stake pools) does not exist in the current codebase.

Since neither the entrypoint (validator-internal, not unprivileged) nor the alleged dependency (total supply is not consumed by the reward-rate calculation) actually exists, this does not meet the decision standard of an unprivileged-input path changing withdrawal/earn/reactivate rights across stake pools.
