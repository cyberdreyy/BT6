### Title
Mid-epoch `isInterestMinted` toggle double-counts borrower interest and inflates tranche NAV - (File: contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol)

### Summary
`setIsInterestMinted` can flip the interest-accounting mode while an epoch is live, even though the sibling flag `setIsProgrammableBorrower` is explicitly blocked with `_checkNotAllowed(isEpochRunning)` for the same reason [1](#0-0) . Flipping the flag mid-epoch desynchronizes the cash-vs-minted interest assumption made at `startEpoch` from the one used at `_stopEpoch`, letting the borrower keep cash interest it already received while the CDO also mints unbacked strategy tokens for the same interest.

### Finding Description
The accounting mode is sampled twice per epoch, at different times:

- `startEpoch` computes `_toSend = isInterestMinted ? _totEpochDeposits : lastEpochInterest + _totEpochDeposits` — in cash mode the borrower receives the previous epoch's interest plus deposits [2](#0-1) .
- `_stopEpoch` re-reads the flag: `_mintInterest = isInterestMinted && !_isRequestingAllFunds`, and if true pulls `_amountToPullFromBorrower = 0` cash for interest and instead calls `_strategy.mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) .

If the epoch was started in cash mode (`isInterestMinted == false`, so `lastEpochInterest` cash was transferred to the borrower) and the flag is flipped to `true` before `stopEpoch`, the CDO mints strategy tokens for `_grossInterest` without pulling any cash, while the borrower retains the cash it received at epoch start. The same interest is credited twice: once as tokens minted into the NAV and once as cash kept by the borrower. The reverse flip (minted → cash) causes `stopEpoch` to demand `_expectedInterest` cash that the borrower was never told to provision, forcing a spurious `_handleBorrowerDefault` and freezing all withdrawals [5](#0-4) .

Only the owner can call `setIsInterestMinted` [6](#0-5) , but the missing `isEpochRunning` guard is the defect — exactly the guard the codebase already applies to `setIsProgrammableBorrower` and to `setEpochParams` [7](#0-6)  and `setInstantWithdrawParams` (implicitly via `paused()` during epochs) [8](#0-7) . An unprivileged tranche holder sequences around the honest owner's configuration call: depositing BB before the flip and redeeming after `stopEpoch` crystallizes the inflated `virtualPrice` harvests a share of the unbacked minted interest.

### Impact Explanation
Direct protocol insolvency equal to one epoch of interest. Strategy tokens are minted for `_grossInterest` with zero cash backing while the borrower keeps the equivalent cash, so `getContractValue()` overstates real assets by `_grossInterest`. Tranche holders who redeem at the inflated post-stop price are paid from other LPs' principal; last-out LPs absorb the shortfall. Severity is bounded to direct fund loss: broken fair mint/burn and solvency invariants.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an owner/manager configuration call mid-epoch (routine when switching borrower setups between epochs, and there is no on-chain signal preventing it during a running epoch). The attacker only needs to be a tranche holder who deposits or redeems around the toggle; no privileged action is needed from the attacker. Because the guard exists on the analogous setter, the omission here appears to be an oversight rather than intended behavior.

### Recommendation
Add `_checkNotAllowed(isEpochRunning)` to `setIsInterestMinted`, matching `setIsProgrammableBorrower`, so the interest-accounting mode is fixed for the whole epoch that was started under it.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork PoC (scaffold against existing harness in `test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol`):

```solidity
function testMidEpochInterestMintedFlipDoubleCounts() external {
    uint256 amount = 10000 * ONE_SCALE;
    idleCDO.depositAA(amount);
    idleCDO.depositBB(amount); // attacker holds BB

    // Epoch started in cash mode: borrower receives lastEpochInterest + deposits
    _startEpochAndCheckPrices(0); // isInterestMinted == false

    // Honest owner flips the flag mid-epoch (no guard prevents this)
    vm.prank(owner);
    cdoEpoch.setIsInterestMinted(true);

    vm.warp(cdoEpoch.epochEndDate() + 1);
    vm.prank(manager);
    cdoEpoch.stopEpoch(0, 0);

    // Invariant: strategy tokens minted for interest, borrower kept cash.
    // virtualPrice/AA/BB prices are inflated by _grossInterest with no backing.
    // Attacker redeems BB at inflated price; residual NAV < sum of remaining claims.
    uint256 nav = idleCDO.getContractValue();
    assertLt(nav, realCashBacking(), "NAV exceeds real assets by one epoch of interest");
}
```

Expected result: after `stopEpoch`, `_strategy.mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` executed while `getFundsFromBorrower` pulled only `_pendingWithdraws`, so token-denominated claims exceed underlying held/owed by `_grossInterest`; a BB redemption at the new `virtualPrice` drains more than its fair share, confirming the double-count.
