### Title
Unincentivized permissionless bad-debt settlement (`lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy`) can lead to depositor fund lock-up - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/handle_bankruptcy.rs`)

### Summary
The reported bug class — a permissionless settlement function with no economic incentive for whoever calls it, which the protocol nonetheless depends on for correctness — has a direct, reachable analog in marginfi's `lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy` instruction. When a bank has `PERMISSIONLESS_BAD_DEBT_SETTLEMENT_FLAG` set, any signer may call it, but the instruction pays the caller nothing: no fee, no reward, no gas rebate. If it isn't called for banks that are not flagged permissionless, only `admin`/`risk_admin` may run it at all, and the docs explicitly state depositor withdrawals will be blocked until it is executed.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy` performs bad-debt resolution: it computes `bad_debt`, draws down the insurance fund, and (if insufficient) socializes the loss across the bank's remaining depositors, potentially killing the bank [1](#0-0) . Authorization is gated by a bank-level flag: if `PERMISSIONLESS_BAD_DEBT_SETTLEMENT_FLAG` is set, any signer can call it; otherwise only `admin` or `risk_admin` can [2](#0-1) .

Reviewing the full instruction body, there is no fee transfer, reward mint, or rebate paid to `signer` anywhere in the logic — the only token movements are insurance-vault-to-liquidity-vault and the internal `repay`/`socialize_loss` accounting [3](#0-2) . This differs from other permissionless/incentivized flows in the same codebase, e.g. classic and receivership liquidation carry a `liquidation_flat_sol_fee` / liquidation premium that compensates the caller, as documented in `guides/ADMIN/COLLECTING_FEES.md` [4](#0-3) . Bankruptcy settlement has no equivalent.

The protocol's own documentation confirms the consequence of nobody bothering to call this unincentivized instruction: "If Bankruptcy isn't executed on a bankrupt user, then remaining depositors can never withdraw the whole balance in the bank. The last few depositors who try to withdraw will find there are not enough funds" [5](#0-4) .

This maps precisely onto the report's bug class: a settlement/finalization step that benefits the protocol/other users but costs the caller gas with zero reward, so it is rational for permissionless actors to skip it and leave it to a centralized admin — exactly the centralization + latent-freeze risk the external report describes for `settle()`.

### Impact Explanation
Failure to promptly run `lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy` on a bankrupt account leaves bad debt on the books. Per the protocol's own bankruptcy guide, this directly causes a durable funds freeze/inconsistency: later depositors attempting to withdraw discover the liquidity vault is short by the uncleared bad debt, i.e. legitimate depositor withdrawals fail or are partially blocked [5](#0-4) . Because non-flagged banks additionally restrict the call to `admin`/`risk_admin`, and even flagged banks provide zero economic motivation for third parties to step in, the system structurally relies on an altruistic/centralized actor to prevent this freeze, precisely mirroring the report's centralization and griefing concern (spamming bankrupt positions across many banks that the admin must clean up at pure cost).

### Likelihood Explanation
Bankruptcy is rare by design (only triggered by extreme illiquidity or price gaps that liquidators fail to clear in time) [6](#0-5) , and the maintainers themselves note it has never fired in the main pool as of the current docs [7](#0-6) . This lowers day-to-day likelihood, but the lack of incentive is a structural gap that persists across every bank with the permissionless flag enabled, and becomes acute precisely during stress events (illiquidity, oracle dislocation) — the same conditions that make bankruptcy occur in the first place, when third parties are least likely to volunteer gas-cost-only transactions.

### Recommendation
Introduce a bounded incentive for callers of `lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy`, analogous to the existing `liquidation_flat_sol_fee` mechanism used for liquidations — e.g., a small flat SOL rebate or a modest cut of the socialized/insurance-covered amount, funded from the bank's fee/insurance flows, paid to `signer`. This should be combined with rate-limiting or eligibility checks (already partially present via `validate_bank_state`) to avoid incentivizing spam or premature settlement of borderline-bankrupt accounts.

### Proof of Concept
1. Configure a bank with `permissionless_bad_debt_settlement = true` (`BankConfigOpt`) as shown in the test suite [8](#0-7) .
2. Drive a borrower into bankruptcy (deposit collateral, borrow, then zero the collateral value) as in `marginfi_group_handle_bankruptcy_perimssionless` [9](#0-8) .
3. Observe that `lending_pool_handle_bankruptcy` succeeds when called by an arbitrary non-admin signer, transferring insurance funds and/or socializing loss, with no compensation credited back to that signer anywhere in the instruction body [10](#0-9) .
4. Because no reward accrues to the caller, in practice this step is left to the admin; if delayed, subsequent depositor withdrawals from the affected bank fail once available liquidity is exhausted, per the documented FAQ [11](#0-10) .

    let covered_by_insurance_rounded_up: u64 = covered_by_insurance
        .checked_ceil()
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .checked_to_num()
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;
    debug!(
        "covered_by_insurance_rounded_up: {}; socialized loss {}",
        covered_by_insurance_rounded_up,
        socialized_loss.to_num::<f64>()
    );

    let insurance_coverage_deposit_pre_fee = maybe_bank_mint
        .as_ref()
        .map(|mint| {
            utils::calculate_pre_fee_spl_deposit_amount(
                mint.to_account_info(),
                covered_by_insurance_rounded_up,
                clock.epoch,
            )
        })
        .transpose()?
        .unwrap_or(covered_by_insurance_rounded_up);

    bank.withdraw_spl_transfer(
        insurance_coverage_deposit_pre_fee,
        ctx.accounts.insurance_vault.to_account_info(),
        ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
        ctx.accounts.insurance_vault_authority.to_account_info(),
        maybe_bank_mint.as_ref(),
        token_program.to_account_info(),
        bank_signer!(
            BankVaultType::Insurance,
            bank_loader.key(),
            bank.insurance_vault_authority_bump
        ),
        ctx.remaining_accounts,
    )?;

    // Socialize bad debt among depositors.
    let kill_bank = bank.socialize_loss(socialized_loss)?;

    // Settle bad debt.
    // The liabilities of this account and global total liabilities are reduced by `bad_debt`
    BankAccountWrapper::find(
        &bank_loader.key(),
        &mut bank,
        &mut marginfi_account.lending_account,
    )?
    .repay(bad_debt)?;

    // Update bank cache after all manipulations (interest accrual, loss socialization, repay)
    bank.update_bank_cache(group)?;
    bank.update_cache_price(cached_price)?;

    marginfi_account.set_flag(ACCOUNT_DISABLED, true);
    marginfi_account.indexer_flags.has_been_bankrupted = 1;
    marginfi_account.last_update = clock.unix_timestamp as u64;
    if kill_bank {
        msg!("bank had debt exceeding liabilities and has been killed");
        bank.config.operational_state = BankOperationalState::KilledByBankruptcy;
