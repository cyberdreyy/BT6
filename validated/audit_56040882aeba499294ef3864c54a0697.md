## Analysis Result

### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Enables MEV Reward-Sniping / Dilution of Long-Term Depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that instantly injects same-mint reward tokens into a bank's liquidity vault and raises `asset_share_value` proportionally to whatever `total_asset_shares` exist at the moment of the call. [1](#0-0)  Because the value bump is distributed pro-rata to shares outstanding at call time, with no snapshot, vesting, or minimum holding-period requirement, an actor who deposits into the bank immediately before this call and withdraws immediately after can capture a disproportionate share of a reward that a third party (e.g., a partner protocol or the marginfi team running an incentive campaign) intended for genuine, longer-term depositors. This mirrors the `VAIVault` bug class: a permissionless reward-accounting event that instantaneously changes payout accounting without preventing "flash" participation around that event.

### Finding Description
The instruction computes the updated `asset_share_value` from `total_asset_shares` and the freshly transferred `amount`: [2](#0-1) 

This is functionally identical to the `VAIVault` reward-injection pattern described in the report: rewards materialize atomically and are distributed pro-rata over whatever principal (shares) is present the instant the reward lands, with no delay or time-weighting to separate "genuine" depositors from opportunistic flash depositors. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented as permissionless and is intended to be triggered by any depositor (protocol, keeper, or partner) funding an incentive campaign directly into the vault. [3](#0-2)  Standard `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` flows for marginfi-native banks have no cooldown, lockup, or same-transaction restriction preventing a deposit followed immediately by a full withdrawal (unlike, e.g., Kamino's stale-reserve check that blocks double actions in one tx). [4](#0-3)  Combined with `withdraw_all`, which fully unwinds a position by burning all `asset_shares` at the current (now-inflated) `asset_share_value`, [5](#0-4)  a searcher can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank right before an emissions-deposit transaction lands (via same-slot bundling/ordering).
2. Let the third-party `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call raise `asset_share_value` pro-rata to `total_asset_shares` (which now includes the searcher's inflated position).
3. Immediately withdraw all shares, realizing a slice of the reward proportional to their newly minted shares, with zero real staking duration or risk.

This dilutes the reward actually accrued to depositors who held their position through the relevant period, exactly analogous to the `VAIVault` MEV bug where an attacker deposits, triggers/awaits reward materialization, and withdraws in the same window to capture rewards disproportionate to their staking time.

### Impact Explanation
Legitimate long-term depositors in a bank receiving `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` incentives have part of their expected reward redirected to a MEV searcher who never bears meaningful exposure to the bank. This is a direct value-redirection / misappropriation of intended incentive funds, financially harming genuine depositors and undermining the incentive campaign's purpose, similar in kind to the original `VAIVault` finding (marked "Fixed" by that client because of its real financial impact).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: the attack requires (a) an external, permissionless call to `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` funded by a third party (a normal expected operational flow for incentive campaigns), and (b) the searcher being able to control transaction ordering around that call (e.g., via Jito bundles / same-slot ordering), which is a well-established MEV technique on Solana. `SECURITY.md` explicitly states that flash-loan/ordering-based attacks are not excluded from scope. [6](#0-5)  No admin/privileged access is required by the attacker; only the (unprivileged) emissions funder needs to exist, which the instruction's own design (permissionless, third-party-fundable) anticipates.

### Recommendation
Prevent flash-deposit reward capture by decoupling reward materialization from instantaneous share-based pro-ration:
- Introduce a minimum holding-period or a reward-vesting/streaming mechanism (similar to a linear "emissions_remaining" drip already present in `Bank`) rather than an instantaneous lump-sum share-value bump.
- Alternatively, snapshot `total_asset_shares` prior to the same-slot/transaction in which the emissions deposit occurs, or require the depositor to also enforce a cooldown before newly deposited shares participate in a freshly injected reward.
- Consider disallowing deposit-then-withdraw-in-same-transaction (or same-slot) patterns around `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, akin to Kamino's stale-reserve safeguard against double-action-in-one-tx.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptually (bankrun/localnet):
1. Depositor A holds 60 USDC of shares in `usdc_bank` (long-standing position).
2. Attacker deposits a very large amount (e.g., 10,000 USDC) into `usdc_bank` in the same transaction bundle immediately before a third party calls `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=50 USDC)`.
3. `bank.asset_share_value` increases pro-rata to `total_asset_shares` (now dominated by the attacker's deposit), per the arithmetic in `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`. [7](#0-6) 
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` with `withdraw_all=true` in the same bundle, redeeming shares at the new inflated `asset_share_value` and capturing the majority of the 50 USDC reward meant for Depositor A, despite holding the position for effectively zero duration.
5. Existing tests already demonstrate the pro-rata mechanics of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` distributing the reward strictly by relative share size at call time, without any time-weighting. [8](#0-7)

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

    /// Withdraw existing asset in full - will error if there is no asset.
    /// When `in_receivership` is true, clears the bank's liquidation price cache lock
    /// so that banks whose balances are closed mid-liquidation don't stay permanently locked.
    /// Returns `(spl_withdraw_amount, asset_share_delta)`.
    pub fn withdraw_all(&mut self, in_receivership: bool) -> MarginfiResult<(u64, I80F48)> {
        let balance = &mut self.balance;
        let bank = &mut self.bank;

        let total_asset_shares: I80F48 = balance.asset_shares.into();
        let current_asset_amount = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
        let current_liability_amount =
            bank.get_liability_amount(balance.liability_shares.into())?;

        debug!("Withdrawing all: {}", current_asset_amount);

        check!(
            current_asset_amount.is_positive_with_tolerance(ZERO_AMOUNT_THRESHOLD),
            MarginfiError::NoAssetFound
        );

        check!(
            current_liability_amount.is_zero_with_tolerance(ZERO_AMOUNT_THRESHOLD),
            MarginfiError::NoAssetFound
        );

        balance.close()?;

        // Only clear the lock when this account is actually in receivership.
        // The lock is bank-level global state, so clearing it unconditionally
        // would affect unrelated accounts sharing the same bank.
        if in_receivership {
            bank.cache.clear_liquidation_price_cache_locked();
        }

        bank.decrement_lending_position_count();
        bank.change_asset_shares(-total_asset_shares, false)?;

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);

    let depositor_a_state = depositor_a.load().await;
    let depositor_b_state = depositor_b.load().await;

    let depositor_a_shares = depositor_a_state
        .lending_account
        .balances
        .iter()
        .find(|b| b.is_active() && b.bank_pk == usdc_bank.key)
        .unwrap()
        .asset_shares;
    let depositor_b_shares = depositor_b_state
        .lending_account
        .balances
        .iter()
        .find(|b| b.is_active() && b.bank_pk == usdc_bank.key)
        .unwrap()
        .asset_shares;

    let depositor_a_assets = bank_after
        .get_asset_amount(I80F48::from(depositor_a_shares))?
        .to_num::<u64>();
    let depositor_b_assets = bank_after
        .get_asset_amount(I80F48::from(depositor_b_shares))?
        .to_num::<u64>();

    assert_eq_with_tolerance!(
        depositor_a_assets as i64,
        native!(60, "USDC") as i64,
        native!(1, "USDC") as i64
    );
    assert_eq_with_tolerance!(
        depositor_b_assets as i64,
        native!(90, "USDC") as i64,
        native!(1, "USDC") as i64
    );
