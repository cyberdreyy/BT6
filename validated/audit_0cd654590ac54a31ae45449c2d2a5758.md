### Title
Stale per-epoch instant-withdraw indices corrupt default-recovery accounting — claimed receipts are re-counted in `defaultPendingClaimBasis`, inflating `defaultRecoveryPrice` and permanently freezing post-default claimants - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol])

### Summary
The binder analog is an index/offset corruption: objects are indexed by offsets that get overwritten before the unwind pass reads them, so the wrong objects are decremented. In `IdleCreditVault`, instant-withdraw receipts are tracked by two redundant indices — the aggregate `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` and the per-epoch `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epoch]` / `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]`. The funded claim path clears only the aggregate and never clears or decrements the per-epoch entries. When a later phase (default finalization and defaulted-instant claiming) unwinds accounting by reading those per-epoch indices, it operates on stale offsets — exactly the "unwinding with corrupted offsets" bug class.

### Finding Description
`requestInstantWithdraw` records the receipt twice: `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user] += _amount` and `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][currentEpoch] += _amount` plus `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[currentEpoch] += _amount` [1](#0-0) . The normal funded claim, `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, burns the receipt and zeroes only `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]`; neither `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` nor `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` is ever decremented on a successful funded claim [2](#0-1) . `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` likewise only reduces `pendingInstantWithdraws` [3](#0-2) .

If the vault defaults in the same epoch (`epochNumber` no longer advances, so `defaultRecoveryEpoch` equals the request epoch), two corruption paths open:

1. `finalizeDefaultRecovery` computes `pendingBasis = defaultPendingClaimBasis()`, which adds `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` [4](#0-3) . That epoch bucket still contains receipts that were already claimed and paid at par, so `totalBasis` is overstated. Simultaneously `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve()` returns `instantBasis - pendingInstant`, where `instantBasis` includes the already-paid claims, so `reserveAmount` — and therefore `defaultRecoveryPrice = reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / totalBasis` — is computed from a phantom reserve that was already transferred out [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) .

2. In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, after finalization, `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` recomputes `claimBasis = instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][defaultEpoch]` — which includes the already-claimed amount — then does `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user] -= claimBasis` on the (now zero or smaller) aggregate [7](#0-6) . Any user who claimed a funded instant withdrawal in the default epoch and later holds any new instant receipt (note `requestInstantWithdraw` has no `defaultRecoveryFinalized` guard, unlike `requestWithdraw` at line 247) permanently reverts on underflow; meanwhile `pendingInstantWithdraws` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` mutations at lines 852–853 corrupt the global unwind for everyone else.

### Impact Explanation
- **Overstated recovery price**: the prefunded-reserve component counts underlying that was already paid out, so `defaultRecoveryPrice` is set too high. Early defaulted-receipt claimants (including an attacker who shapes the epoch's instant-claim history) draw more than their fair share from `defaultRecoveryReserve`, leaving later claimants with unpayable claims — direct theft/insolvency of the recovery reserve.
- **Permanent freezing**: users whose stale per-epoch basis exceeds their remaining aggregate revert inside `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` forever; their post-default instant receipts are unclaimable.
- **Broken invariant**: "one receipt one payout" and correct loss-waterfall accounting — the unwind pass operates on offsets (per-epoch indices) that were never updated when the corresponding objects were released.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: instant withdrawals enabled, a user (attacker can be any KYC-passing lender) who requests and successfully claims an instant withdrawal in epoch N, partial funding of the instant queue so `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0`, then borrower default and `finalizeDefaultRecovery` in the same epoch. All steps use only unprivileged/honest-role calls in normal order; the missing cleanup in `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` is unconditional code, not a race. The existing guards (`_onlyIdleCDO`, `NotAllowed` checks, `defaultRecoveryFinalized` flag) do not clear the stale indices.

### Recommendation
- In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, decrement `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epoch]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]` for every receipt settled at par (track the request epoch at claim time, or iterate/zero the current-epoch entry).
- In `requestInstantWithdraw`, mirror `requestWithdraw`'s post-default guard and either revert or route through a post-default receipt bucket.
- In `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`, cap `claimBasis` at `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` and treat prefunded basis consistently with what was actually collected.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork PoC outline (no privileged-role misbehavior):

1. Deploy `IdleCreditVault` + `IdleCDOEpochVariant` with instant withdrawals; lender `A` deposits AA, epoch 0 starts.
2. `A` calls `requestInstantWithdraw(x)` → `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[A][1] = x`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[1] = x`.
3. Honest manager triggers funding (`collectInstantWithdrawFunds` partial, leaving `pendingInstantWithdraws > 0` from another requester `B`); `A` calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` and is paid `x` at par. Observe `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[A][1]` is still `x` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[1]` still includes `x` — the stale offset.
4. Borrower defaults; CDO calls `finalizeDefaultRecovery`. Assert `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` counts `x` again and `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve()` is inflated by the already-paid amount → `defaultRecoveryPrice` overstated.
5. `A` (or `B`) requests a post-default instant withdraw, then calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`: either it reverts on `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user] -= claimBasis` underflow (permanent freeze), or, where the inflated price is paid first, drains `defaultRecoveryReserve` such that subsequent `claimWithdrawRequest`/`DefaultDistributor.claim` calls revert for honest users.

Assert failure conditions: `defaultRecoveryPrice` higher than `actualHeldReserve * 1e18 / trueBasis`, and post-default claims reverting while `defaultRecoveryReserve` accounting still shows obligations.
