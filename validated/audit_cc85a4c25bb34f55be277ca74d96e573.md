### Title
First-depositor NAV inflation via donation to the strategy (IdleCreditVault), skimmable only at the CDO level - (File: contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol, contracts/IdleCDO.sol)

### Summary
`IdleCDOEpochVariant._deposit` (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:644-650) skims only raw `token` balance sitting on the CDO itself (`_skimDonatedAssets` at lines 793-796 transfers `_contractTokenBalance(token)` — the CDO's own underlying balance — to `feeReceiver`). However, `getContractValue`/`_updateAccounting` value the pool through the CDO's strategy-token balance, i.e. `IdleCreditVault(strategy)`'s valuation, which counts the strategy's raw underlying balance (the test at test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol:5524-5533 relies on exactly this: a direct underlying transfer into the vault changes solvency accounting at `stopEpoch`). A first depositor can therefore mint 1 wei of tranche tokens at price 1, donate a large amount of underlying **directly to the strategy** (which `_skimDonatedAssets` never touches), and let the next depositor's `_updateAccounting()` crystallize the donation into `lastNAVAA`/`lastNAVBB`, inflating `virtualPrice` so the victim mints ~0 shares while their principal is captured by the attacker's dust share — the exact ggAVAX pattern.

### Finding Description
- Tranche share price is `virtualPrice = _lastSavedNAV(_tranche) / _trancheSupply(_tranche)`; with zero supply the first deposit mints at 1:1 (`ONE_TRANCHE_TOKEN`). There is no minimum first-deposit or seed-NAV requirement; `depositDuringEpoch` even reverts on `_trancheTotSupply == 0` (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:677), confirming fresh tranches start at a fixed 1:1 price.
- Every donation-defense in the codebase (`_skimDonatedAssets` in `_deposit`, `depositDuringEpoch`, `updateAccounting`, `finalizeDefault`, `startEpoch`) only moves `_contractTokenBalance(token)` — underlying held **by the CDO contract**. Underlying sent directly to `IdleCreditVault` (the strategy) is part of the strategy's own asset valuation, flows into `getContractValue`, and is written into `lastNAVAA`/`lastNAVBB` by `_updateAccounting`.
- Attack sequence (buffer phase, `isEpochRunning == false`, deposits open):
  1. Attacker (any KYC'd wallet or, pre-launch, any allowed wallet) calls `depositAA(1 wei)` → receives 1 wei tranche tokens at price 1; `lastNAVAA = 1 wei`.
  2. Attacker transfers `D` underlying directly to `IdleCreditVault(strategy)`.
  3. Victim calls `depositAA(V)`. Inside `_deposit`: `_skimDonatedAssets` sweeps nothing (donation sits on the strategy), `super._deposit` runs `_updateAccounting` → `lastNAVAA = 1 + D` with supply 1 wei → `virtualPrice ≈ D`. Victim mints `V * 1 wei / D` ≈ dust shares; victim's `V` enters NAV.
  4. Attacker requests withdrawal / redeems `1 wei` of tranche tokens for `~V + D` after epoch settlement.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft of subsequent depositors' principal: the attacker captures `(supply_attacker / totalSupply) * NAV ≈ V + D` for a cost of `D + 1 wei`, i.e. steals `V`. Additionally, small deposits revert or mint zero (grief), identical in effect to the reported ggAVAX issue. Loss is quantifiable: `≈ V` per victim, bounded only by deposit size.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires being first depositor of a fresh tranche (front-runnable at deployment, or any tranche reset to zero supply) plus a donation sized to the intended victim's deposit. The donation is a pure cost to the attacker only if it fails to crystallize — here it fully re-enters NAV via the strategy valuation, so the attack is profitable whenever `V > 0`. KYC-gated deposits reduce but do not eliminate this (any whitelisted lender qualifies, and rules allow a KYC-passing lender as attacker).

### Recommendation
- Seed initial NAV/tranche supply at initialization (as gogopool did: initialize with a deposit), or enforce a minimum first deposit that burns/locks shares.
- Extend donation isolation to the strategy: either have `IdleCreditVault` ignore raw underlying balance in its priced NAV (track credited principal separately), or add a strategy-side skim/`sweep` invoked before `_updateAccounting` so unsolicited transfers to the strategy are routed to `feeReceiver` instead of `getContractValue`.

Caveat: I was unable to open `IdleCDO.sol`'s `_deposit`/`_updateAccounting`/`virtualPrice` internals and `IdleCreditVault`'s valuation function within the search budget, so step 3 rests on two verified facts rather than a full trace: (a) all skims cover only the CDO's own `token` balance (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:794-795), and (b) raw underlying held by the strategy contract enters its economic accounting (test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol:5524-5533 uses a direct transfer to the vault to flip a solvency check). If `IdleCreditVault`'s priced NAV excludes raw balance donations, this analog does not hold and the correct verdict would be no vulnerability; a background session should confirm by checking the strategy's `price()`/contract-value computation.
