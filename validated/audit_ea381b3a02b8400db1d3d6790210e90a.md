### Title
Atomic multi-recipient fee delivery in `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` allows a single frozen/blacklisted destination to permanently block delivery of *all* fee legs, including the insurance vault - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/collect_bank_fees.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` is a permissionless instruction that atomically moves three separate fee legs (insurance, group, program) out of a bank's `liquidity_vault` into three different destination token accounts within a single Solana instruction/transaction. Because all three SPL transfers are CPI calls inside one instruction, if any single destination account cannot receive tokens (e.g. it is frozen by the mint's freeze authority, blacklisted, or backed by a malicious/Token-2022 mint with a reverting transfer hook), the entire transaction reverts and none of the fee legs are delivered — mirroring the reported Clober `collectFees` bug where a single bad recipient blocks atomic delivery to all parties.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` computes and transfers three amounts in sequence within one instruction: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

The three destinations are:
- `insurance_vault` — a bank-specific PDA, immutable once the bank is created.
- `fee_vault` — a bank-specific PDA, immutable once the bank is created.
- `fee_ata` — the canonical ATA of `FeeState.global_fee_wallet`, a single program-wide wallet used across *every* bank sharing that mint. It is validated against the `fee_state` account but not otherwise restricted: [3](#0-2) 

Because the instruction is called permissionlessly ("Anyone can call this" per the fee guide) and combines all three transfers atomically, any condition that makes one leg's SPL transfer CPI fail — e.g. the mint issuer freezes/blacklists the `fee_ata` or one of the vaults (common with tokens like USDC, or with Token-2022 mints carrying a `PermanentDelegate`/`TransferHook`/`FreezeAuthority` extension) — causes the whole transaction, including the insurance and group fee transfers that have nothing to do with the bad recipient, to revert: [4](#0-3) 

Note this affects the insurance vault, which is described as offsetting socialized losses on bankruptcy, meaning it is fed exclusively through this same atomic instruction: [5](#0-4) 

SPL Token's `Transfer`/`TransferChecked` instruction enforces the frozen-account check regardless of the amount transferred, so even a zero-amount leg to a frozen `fee_ata` will cause the CPI (and therefore the whole instruction) to fail.

This is the same bug class as the referenced Clober `collectFees`/`_collectFees` issue: a function intended to atomically deliver fee legs to multiple independent recipients is bricked in its entirety by a single recipient becoming untransferable, even though the other legs are otherwise deliverable.

### Impact Explanation
If any one destination in `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` becomes permanently untransferable (frozen by mint freeze authority, blacklisted, or reverting Token-2022 transfer hook), fee collection for that bank's mint is durably bricked:
- The `insurance_vault` never receives further top-ups, weakening the protocol's ability to cover socialized losses from bad debt across every bank using that mint.
- The `fee_vault` (group fees) also stops accruing, even though the group admin/host destination itself may be perfectly fine.
- Because `global_fee_wallet` in `FeeState` is a single program-wide address, freezing/blacklisting it (or its canonical ATA for a given mint) blocks `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` for **every bank** that uses that mint, not just one bank — a broader blast radius than the original Clober report.

This is a durable freeze of protocol fee/insurance accounting with financial effect, matching the "Medium" severity class of the original finding.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger does not require any marginfi-privileged action: it only requires the underlying SPL token's freeze/blacklist authority (held by the token issuer, e.g. Circle for USDC, or a malicious/compromised Token-2022 mint's extension authority) to freeze or block one of the three fixed destination accounts. Since marginfi supports arbitrary SPL/Token-2022 mints as bank collateral, and `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` is permissionless and routinely invoked (per the fee guide, "the vast majority of the time, we run this ix just before a withdraw"), the failure condition is externally triggerable and persists until the frozen/blacklisted account is somehow remediated — which for PDA vaults (`insurance_vault`, `fee_vault`) is not possible since they cannot be swapped.

### Recommendation
Split `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` into per-destination instructions (or otherwise decouple the three transfers so a revert on one leg does not roll back the others), and track independent outstanding-fee counters per leg so a failure on one destination does not block accrual/collection to the remaining destinations. This mirrors the fix Clober applied: parametrizing fee collection to a single token/recipient pair per call rather than delivering to multiple recipients atomically.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy a bank with an underlying Token-2022 mint that includes a `TransferHook` extension (or use a mint with a freeze authority such as USDC).
2. Accrue insurance, group, and program fees normally via `lending_pool_accrue_bank_interest`.
3. Have the mint's freeze authority freeze the `fee_ata` (canonical ATA of `FeeState.global_fee_wallet` for that mint) — or configure the transfer hook to revert on any transfer to that specific address.
4. Call `lending_pool_collect_bank_fees` (permissionless, callable by anyone).
5. Observe the whole transaction reverts due to the CPI transfer to `fee_ata` failing, even though the `insurance_vault` and `fee_vault` legs (lines 93-125 in `collect_bank_fees.rs`) would have succeeded independently.
6. Because `fee_ata`/`global_fee_wallet` is shared program-wide for that mint, repeat step 4 for any other bank using the same mint — all such banks are now permanently unable to move fees into their `insurance_vault`/`fee_vault`.
