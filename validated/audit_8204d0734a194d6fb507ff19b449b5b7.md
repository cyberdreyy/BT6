### Title
Inflated `totalInterestDueNow` via ERC4626 donation mints unbacked strategy tokens and permanently inflates tranche NAV - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol)

### Summary
An unprivileged depositor of the ERC4626 vault configured in `ProgrammableBorrower` can inflate `convertToAssets` by donating underlying directly to the vault. The phantom gain flows through `_vaultNetInterest()` → `totalInterestDueNow()` → `IdleCDOEpochVariant._resolveStopEpochInterest()` and, in minted-interest mode, `stopEpoch` mints strategy tokens (`mintStrategyTokens`) backed only by that transient donation. When the attacker later unwinds the donation (redeeming vault shares or after PB exits), the resulting `vaultLoss` is only netted against future interest and floored at zero — the already-minted tokens are never clawed back, so NAV remains inflated while real backing is short.

### Finding Description
`ProgrammableBorrower._currentVaultAssets()` values the whole position as `vault.convertToAssets(shares)` (ProgrammableBorrower.sol:546-549). `_vaultNetInterest()` treats any increase over `epochStartVaultAssets + epochDepositedToVault` as vault interest (lines 337-347), and `totalInterestDueNow()` reports it to the CDO (lines 330-334). `IdleCDOEpochVariant.stopEpoch` resolves interest from `IProgrammableBorrower.totalInterestDueNow()` for programmable borrowers (IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:1001-1005) and, when `isInterestMinted` is set, calls `_strategy.mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` (line 430), minting virtual strategy tokens that raise every tranche's virtual price and mint fee shares to `feeReceiver`/`owner` (lines 445-447).

The only sanity check on resolved interest is gated by `_mintInterest && _interest > 1` (line 377), but `_resolveStopEpochInterest` requires `_interest <= 1` for programmable borrowers — so the `maxApr` cap never applies in this mode. An attacker who is a depositor in the ERC4626 vault (e.g., a MetaMorpho vault user, an explicitly in-scope role) donates `D` underlying to the vault just before `stopEpoch`. PB's `convertToAssets` rises by roughly `D * PB_share_fraction`; `totalInterestDueNow` reports it as vault interest; the CDO mints ~that amount in unbacked strategy tokens.

After the stop, the attacker redeems their vault shares or waits for `borrow`/`emergencyExitVault` flows that shrink PB's position; next epoch the shortfall registers as `vaultLoss`, but `_vaultNetInterest` only floors `totalInterestDueNow` at zero — `lastEpochInterest` minted tokens and fee shares remain in the price base. The attacker, simultaneously holding tranche tokens (KYC-passing lender), redeems at the inflated price and exits, leaving remaining holders with NAV that exceeds real backing.

### Impact Explanation
Broken invariant: fair mint/burn and solvency. `mintStrategyTokens` issues claims against interest that was never durably held — a donation round-trip. Quantified loss: `D * PB_share_fraction` of phantom NAV; attacker profit is bounded by their tranche share of minted interest minus the donation slice absorbed by PB, and each retained minted share permanently impairs remaining holders' redemption value (first-come-first-served withdrawal against an overpriced pool → insolvency for late withdrawers).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a programmable-borrower deployment with `isInterestMinted = true` (the mode exercised in `test/foundry/ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol`) and an ERC4626 vault where donation/price manipulation moves `convertToAssets` — standard for vaults without virtual shares or where the attacker can deposit-donate-redeem. No privileged role needed: the attacker is merely a user of the external vault plus a normal tranche holder. No existing guard stops it: the `maxApr` cap is unreachable in programmable mode, `_skimDonatedAssets` does not apply to the external vault sleeve, and there is no grace period or TWAP on `convertToAssets`.

### Recommendation
- Apply the `maxApr` sanity bound to the resolved programmable-borrower interest, not only to the `_interest > 1` path.
- Subtract freshly donated/atomic price spikes by measuring vault PnL against a smooth baseline (e.g., compare `convertToAssets` to a stored per-share price from `onStartEpoch`/`onStopEpoch`, or require interest realization via actual `withdraw`/`redeem` deltas only).
- Alternatively cap `totalInterestDueNow` at cash the borrower contract can actually pull and have the CDO price only realized, transferable interest.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork sketch (mainnet fork with a live ERC4626 vault, mirroring `ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol` setup):
1. `owner` sets `isInterestMinted(true)`; lender deposits AA; `manager` calls `startEpoch()` → PB deposits pool assets into vault.
2. Attacker (vault depositor + AA holder): `vault.deposit(small)` to get shares, then `underlying.transfer(address(vault), D)` donation.
3. `vm.warp(epochEndDate + 1)`; record `programmableBorrower.totalInterestDueNow()` (≈ `D * PB share fraction` above true interest) and `manager` calls `cdoEpoch.stopEpoch(0, 0)`.
4. Assert `strategyToken.balanceOf(cdo)` increased by phantom `mintStrategyTokens` amount and `virtualPrice(AA)` rose; attacker calls `claimWithdrawRequest`/`redeem` tranches at inflated price.
5. Attacker redeems vault shares (or warp so the donation slice is withdrawn); next `startEpoch`/`stopEpoch` shows `vaultLoss > 0` but `lastEpochInterest == 0` floor — minted tokens persist → `getContractValue()` exceeds real recoverable underlying by ≈ `D * PB_share_fraction`.

Uncertainty: I could not confirm the exact line text of the `maxApr` default in `IdleCreditVault` or whether `_skimDonatedAssets` is invoked anywhere on the stop path; if `maxApr` is nonzero and enforced before resolution the bound may partially cap the exploit — worth verifying before finalizing severity.
