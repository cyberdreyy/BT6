### Title
Phantom vault PnL in `ProgrammableBorrower._vaultNetInterest` mints unbacked strategy tokens and inflates tranche prices at `stopEpoch` - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol])

### Summary
In programmable-borrower mode with minted interest (`isInterestMinted`), `IdleCDOEpochVariant._stopEpoch` resolves epoch interest from `IProgrammableBorrower.totalInterestDueNow()` and mints that amount of strategy tokens via `IdleCreditVault.mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)`. `totalInterestDueNow()` treats any increase in the external ERC4626 vault's `convertToAssets(shares)` valuation as earned interest. A donation to the external vault (or any manipulation that raises `convertToAssets` without transferring real yield to the facility) is therefore crystallized as real NAV in the CDO: tranche prices rise even though no additional underlying backs them. A tranche holder who also holds shares of the external vault can donate underlying, wait for the manager's `stopEpoch` to mint the phantom interest, then redeem tranche tokens at the inflated price — extracting more real underlying than deposited and leaving remaining LPs with an insolvent pool. This is the same bug class as the reference exploit: a mismeasured reserve valuation is minted into share supply and redeemed for real assets.

### Finding Description
`ProgrammableBorrower._vaultNetInterest` computes epoch vault PnL purely from the share-price oracle of an external ERC4626 vault: `earnedAssets = _currentVaultAssets() + epochWithdrawnFromVault` where `_currentVaultAssets()` is `vault.convertToAssets(vault.balanceOf(address(this)))`. Any positive `netDelta` is returned as `interest` and surfaced through `totalInterestDueNow()` as `vaultInterest + borrowerInterestAccruedNow() + bufferInterest`.

`IdleCDOEpochVariant._stopEpoch` resolves `_interest` via `_resolveStopEpochInterest` (which reads `totalInterestDueNow()` for programmable borrowers), then on the success path executes `if (_grossInterest != 0) _strategy.mintStrategyTokens(_grossInterest)` and `_updateAccounting()`. Because `IdleCDOCreditVault.getContractValue()` counts the minted strategy tokens 1:1, the phantom donation is written into `lastNAVAA`/`lastNAVBB` and raises `priceAA`/`priceBB`.

Attack sequence (unprivileged roles only):
1. During buffer, attacker deposits underlying into a tranche (`depositAA`/`depositBB`, passing KYC/whitelist), acquiring fraction `t` of tranche supply.
2. Attacker deposits into the same ERC4626 vault used by `ProgrammableBorrower`, acquiring fraction `v` of vault shares.
3. Manager calls `startEpoch`; `onStartEpoch` snapshots `epochStartVaultAssets` after depositing idle funds.
4. During the running epoch, attacker donates `D` underlying directly to the external vault, raising `convertToAssets` by `D`. `_vaultNetInterest` now reports `D` as epoch interest.
5. Manager calls `stopEpoch(0, 0)` after `epochEndDate`. `_stopEpoch` mints `_grossInterest` (including `D`) strategy tokens and runs `_updateAccounting`, raising tranche prices by ~`D` in aggregate NAV terms.
6. Attacker calls `requestWithdraw`/`claimWithdrawRequest` (or `withdrawAA` in non-epoch variants) and redeems at the inflated price, gaining ≈ `t·D` of real underlying. Simultaneously the attacker's external-vault shares recover ≈ `v·D` of the donation.

Net attacker PnL ≈ `D·(t + v − 1)`. Since a single actor can hold a large tranche share and a large external-vault share simultaneously, this exceeds zero: e.g., `t = 0.5`, `v = 0.7` yields `+0.2·D`. Even in the degenerate case `v = 1` (attacker owns all vault shares), the donation is fully recovered through the vault *and* `t·D` is extracted from the CDO — pure profit. The pool is left insolvent: `lastNAV` exceeds real recoverable backing by the phantom amount, so the last claimers cannot be paid (one-receipt-one-payout and solvency invariants broken).

Guards that do not stop it:
- `_skimDonatedAssets()` only skims raw underlying sent to the CDO; the donation here goes to the external vault and arrives via `convertToAssets`.
- The `maxApr` cap check in `_stopEpoch` (`_grossInterest > _calcInterestWithApr(getContractValue(), _maxApr) + _pendingWithdrawFees`) only bounds the total; a donation sized below the remaining headroom under `maxApr` passes.
- `_checkNotAllowed` gating, `isWalletAllowed` KYC, and `onlyIdleCDO` hooks are all satisfied — the attacker never needs a privileged call; inflation is created by the honest manager's routine `stopEpoch`.
- `borrow`/`repay`/`settleBorrowerInterest` are unaffected; the vault-PnL component is never reconciled against actual cash pulled (in minted mode `_amountToPullFromBorrower = 0` for interest).

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft plus insolvency. The attacker withdraws more underlying than their fair share; the phantom minted strategy tokens leave aggregate claims (`lastNAVAA + lastNAVBB`) greater than recoverable backing, so remaining tranche holders and pending withdraw-request receipt holders are permanently under-collateralized. Quantified loss to honest LPs ≈ `D·t` (the portion of the fake PnL paid out to the attacker), up to the `maxApr` headroom per epoch, repeatable across epochs.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requirements: a pool configured with `isProgrammableBorrower` and `isInterestMinted` (the intended programmable configuration per `setIsInterestMinted`), an external ERC4626 vault whose `convertToAssets` can be raised by direct underlying transfer (true for balance-based vaults such as MetaMorpho-style `totalAssets = balanceOf` accounting), and attacker capital for tranche + vault deposits plus the donation. All attacker actions are unprivileged deposits/transfers; the trigger is the honest manager's scheduled `stopEpoch`. The only bound is that fake interest must stay under the `maxApr`-derived cap (when `_interest > 1` resolution applies), which is a per-epoch budget rather than a block.

### Recommendation
Do not convert raw `convertToAssets` deltas into minted NAV. In `ProgrammableBorrower`:
- Track vault profit using deposit/withdraw flow accounting with an explicit price floor, e.g., snapshot `convertToAssets(1 share)` at epoch start and cap recognized interest to the share-price appreciation (a donation still raises price, so instead require realized profit: only count assets actually withdrawn above principal, or require `interest ≤ maxWithdraw`-realized amounts).
- Alternatively, in `_stopEpoch`, fund minted interest from cash actually pulled (`_amountToPullFromBorrower`) rather than minting unbacked strategy tokens against an unreconciled external valuation; or cap `_grossInterest` by contractual `borrowerInterestAccruedNow() + bufferInterest` and treat vault PnL as realized only on redeem.
- At minimum, bound recognized vault interest by a monotonic max-share-price watermark (`lastVaultSharePrice` updated only upward on realized redeems) so unsolicited donations cannot be recrystallized as CDO NAV.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork sketch (adapt `test/foundry/ProgrammableBorrowerCreditVault.t.sol`, which already wires `IdleCDOEpochVariant` + `IdleCreditVault` + `ProgrammableBorrower` against a real MetaMorpho vault on a mainnet fork):

```solidity
// Setup (reuse existing fixture): owner enables minted interest.
vm.prank(owner);
cdoEpoch.setIsInterestMinted(true); // required for programmable mode

// 1. Attacker (KYC'd wallet) takes a large AA tranche position.
deal(USDC, attacker, ATTACKER_DEPOSIT);
vm.startPrank(attacker);
underlying.approve(address(idleCDO), ATTACKER_DEPOSIT);
idleCDO.depositAA(ATTACKER_DEPOSIT);
vm.stopPrank();

// 2. Honest LP deposits the rest, manager starts epoch.
//    onStartEpoch deposits idle funds into the ERC4626 vault and snapshots epochStartVaultAssets.
idleCDO.depositAA(HONEST_DEPOSIT);
vm.prank(manager);
cdoEpoch.startEpoch();

// 3. Attacker also deposits into the external vault (allowed: vault user).
//    Then donates D underlying directly to the vault -> convertToAssets rises by D.
vm.startPrank(attacker);
underlying.approve(address(morphoVault), VAULT_DEPOSIT);
morphoVault.deposit(VAULT_DEPOSIT, attacker);
underlying.transfer(address(morphoVault), DONATION); // fake "PnL"
vm.stopPrank();

// 4. Warp past epochEndDate; manager stops epoch normally.
vm.warp(cdoEpoch.epochEndDate() + 1);
vm.prank(manager);
cdoEpoch.stopEpoch(0, 0); // mintStrategyTokens(totalInterestDueNow incl. DONATION)

// 5. Tranche price inflated by phantom NAV; attacker redeems at inflated price.
uint256 priceAA = cdoEpoch.virtualPrice(address(aaTranche));
assertGt(priceAA, priceAABefore); // phantom interest crystallized

vm.prank(attacker);
cdoEpoch.requestWithdraw(0, address(aaTranche));
vm.prank(attacker);
cdoEpoch.claimWithdrawRequest();

// Attacker recovered DONATION via own vault shares AND gained ~t*DONATION
// from the CDO; assert net profit and that remaining LP claims exceed real backing.
assertGt(underlying.balanceOf(attacker) + morphoVault.convertToAssets(morphoVault.balanceOf(attacker)),
         attackerStartBalance);
assertLt(realBacking, cdoEpoch.lastNAVAA() + cdoEpoch.lastNAVBB());
```

Caveat: I did not fully verify `_resolveStopEpochInterest` internals or the exact `maxApr` cap application to the `totalInterestDueNow` path; the PoC assumes resolved interest flows into `mintStrategyTokens` unbounded except by the `_interest > 1` cap check, which the donation must be sized under.
