### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows depositors to time deposit/withdraw around reward injections and skim a pro-rata share of yield meant for existing lenders - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
The External Report describes a River/lsETH accounting model where a single share price is used for the whole pool, and where profits (harvested by an oracle call) are distributed instantly and pro-rata to whoever holds shares *at that moment*, allowing depositors to time their deposit just before the profit push and their withdrawal just before a loss push, extracting value that should have accrued to long-term holders. Marginfi's core interest-accrual model differs fundamentally — interest accrues continuously as a function of `time_delta` and utilization [1](#0-0) , so there is no discrete "profit push" to front-run there. However, marginfi does have a discrete, atomic share-value-bump mechanism analogous to River's oracle-driven profit distribution: `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented and implemented as **permissionless** — "any signer" can push extra same-mint tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault, which instantly re-prices `asset_share_value` for the *entire* outstanding share supply in a single atomic step, without minting new shares: [2](#0-1) 

The new `asset_share_value` is computed as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` — i.e., all shares outstanding at the instant the instruction executes share in the injected amount proportionally, regardless of how long they've been in the bank: [3](#0-2) 

Deposits and withdrawals into/out of a bank are ordinary permissionless user instructions with no minimum holding period, cooldown, or vesting: `lending_account_deposit` credits shares immediately [4](#0-3)  and `lending_account_withdraw` allows an immediate withdrawal in the very next instruction, subject only to bank liquidity and account health [5](#0-4) .

This combination reproduces the exact bug class in the report: since (1) the emissions-deposit trigger is a public, observable transaction (permissionless, so anyone can also front-run it by including their own deposit ahead of it, or observe a pending legitimate emissions payout and race it via priority fees/MEV bundling), and (2) share re-pricing happens atomically and is not time-weighted, an attacker can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank immediately before an emissions deposit lands.
2. Let the emissions deposit execute, which raises `asset_share_value` for all shares, including the attacker's freshly minted ones.
3. Immediately withdraw, realizing a slice of the injected reward proportional to the attacker's share of `total_asset_shares` at that instant — despite having contributed zero time-weighted capital to the pool.

This directly dilutes the return that legitimate, long-term depositors were meant to receive from the emissions/rewards injection, mirroring the River finding: "Investors might time their deposit just before profits are being distributed, thus harvesting profits made by others."

### Impact Explanation
The impact is a value-redirection/misappropriation of yield: honest long-term depositors receive a diluted share of legitimate reward/emissions distributions, while an opportunistic actor captures value with a flash-style deposit/withdraw that carries no real economic exposure to the bank. Because `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless and can inject significant amounts (e.g., recovered funds, ecosystem incentives, or rebates), the amount skimmable scales with the size of each emissions injection and the attacker's ability to temporarily dominate `total_asset_shares` in that bank at the moment of injection. This is a financial-effect issue affecting real economic value transferred between users, not merely a theoretical or no-impact quirk.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is bounded by two factors: emissions deposits must be sizeable relative to total bank assets to make the skim worthwhile, and the attacker needs to land a deposit immediately before (and a withdrawal immediately after) the emissions-deposit transaction, which on Solana requires either observing a pending/queued transaction (e.g., via a scheduled distributor bot, RPC visibility, or Jito bundle simulation) or colluding with/being the emissions depositor. Since `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless by design, both the trigger and the deposit/withdraw race are fully unprivileged actions requiring no special access — making this a realistic MEV-style opportunity for any sufficiently capitalized actor whenever a known/predictable emissions payout is scheduled.

### Recommendation
- Time-weight the emissions distribution instead of instantly re-pricing `asset_share_value` for the full outstanding share supply — e.g., stream the injected amount into `asset_share_value` gradually over a vesting window (similar to how `accrue_interest` smooths interest via `time_delta`), rather than applying it as an instantaneous jump.
- Alternatively, snapshot eligible share balances (e.g., based on a `last_update`/minimum holding duration) before applying an emissions deposit, so shares created in the same transaction/slot (or within some minimal window) as the emissions deposit do not qualify for the distribution.
- Consider adding a short minimum holding period or withdrawal delay specifically around emissions-deposit events, or requiring emissions deposits to only be triggerable by a trusted role (undermining the "permissionless" design would reduce composability but eliminate the race).
- At minimum, document/monitor for large emissions deposits and consider disallowing/penalizing same-slot deposit+emissions-deposit+withdraw sequences.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (using existing test scaffolding such as `try_emissions_deposit`, `try_bank_deposit`, `try_bank_withdraw`):
1. Attacker observes (or triggers) an impending call to `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` on bank `B` funding a reward of size `E`.
2. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` on bank `B` with a large amount `D`, immediately before the emissions-deposit transaction lands, becoming a share of `total_asset_shares`.
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes, recomputing `asset_share_value = (total_assets + E) / total_asset_shares` for the whole bank (see `configure_bank.rs` lines 138-146 cited above) — the attacker's shares are priced up along with everyone else's.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) on bank `B`, realizing `D * (asset_share_value_after / asset_share_value_before - 1)` in profit — a slice of `E` proportional to `D / total_assets`, extracted without ever being exposed to the bank's risk/utilization over time.

This mirrors the exact "deposit right before profit distribution, withdraw right after" pattern flagged in the External Report against `OracleManager.setBeaconData`/River's share-price model, replicated here against marginfi's permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` share-repricing mechanism.

```rust
/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
```
