### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows flash-deposit "reward sniping" via instantaneous share-ratio snapshot - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` distributes a lump-sum reward injection to bank depositors purely in proportion to `total_asset_shares` at the instant the instruction executes, with no time-weighting of how long each depositor has held their position. Because the instruction is explicitly permissionless and the underlying `lending_account_deposit` / `lending_account_withdraw` primitives have no cooldown or minimum holding period, an unprivileged user can flash-deposit immediately before an emissions injection and withdraw immediately after, capturing a share of the reward far in excess of their true time-weighted contribution — the same "reward sniping" bug class described in the BellumNursery report.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new `asset_share_value` as:
```
total_assets = total_asset_shares * old_asset_share_value  (via get_asset_amount)
updated_total_assets = total_assets + amount
new_asset_share_value = updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares
``` [1](#0-0) 

This re-values every existing share holder equally based only on the current share count at call time — there is no checkpointing of when each depositor's shares were minted, and no accrual-over-time mechanism (unlike the Juplend integration, which streams rewards continuously via `token_exchange_price`/`updateRate` rather than injecting a lump sum instantly) [2](#0-1) .

Critically, the instruction is documented as callable by anyone: [3](#0-2) 
and the `depositor`/`emissions_funding_account` are unchecked, ordinary signer/token accounts [4](#0-3) .

The team's own user-facing documentation confirms the pro-rata, real-time (non-duration-weighted) design, with a worked example that mirrors the exact dynamic in the BellumNursery report — a user who deposits shortly before a distribution captures a disproportionate share relative to a long-term holder: [5](#0-4) 

Because `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` are ordinary, uncooled, atomic user instructions, a user can sandwich a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call (their own, a protocol's, or one observed pending in the mempool) with a deposit immediately before and a withdraw immediately after, in the same or immediately adjacent transactions, capturing reward value that should accrue to genuine long-term depositors.

### Impact Explanation
This allows unauthorized value redirection: an attacker with capital and timing visibility can extract a disproportionate fraction of any incoming emissions injection from a bank, diluting the rightful entitlement of long-term depositors in that bank without ever bearing meaningful economic exposure or time-at-risk. Repeated exploitation across banks/campaigns constitutes a durable and material economic drain on depositor rewards, financially harming honest long-term lenders. This is a value-redirection/exploitable-misvaluation issue reachable by any unprivileged user, matching the required bar for a concrete finding.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: the attack requires (a) capital sufficient to meaningfully skew `total_asset_shares` relative to the pending emissions amount, and (b) either calling `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` themselves right after depositing, or observing/front-running a pending emissions-deposit transaction (permissionless calls from third parties/bots are visible in the mempool). No protocol-level cooldown, minimum holding period, or withdrawal fee was found in the reviewed deposit/withdraw paths that would prevent this atomic sandwich, making the attack mechanically straightforward to execute for any funded actor.

### Recommendation
Adopt a streaming/rate-based reward accrual model for `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (similar to the Juplend integration's `token_exchange_price` accrual over a configured duration) rather than an instantaneous lump-sum share revaluation. Alternatively, implement reward checkpoints that track a depositor's time-weighted share of the pool (as recommended in the source report) so that shares acquired immediately before a reward injection do not receive full pro-rata credit for rewards accrued over a longer prior period.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual PoC (Anchor/TS, adapting the pattern from `try_emissions_deposit` test helpers):
```
1. Depositor A deposits 100 USDC into bank B and holds for 30 days.
2. Attacker observes (or itself triggers) an imminent lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=100 USDC) call.
3. Attacker calls lending_account_deposit(bank=B, amount=900 USDC) immediately before the emissions deposit lands.
4. lending_pool_emissions_deposit executes, raising bank.asset_share_value proportionally to
   total_asset_shares at that instant (per configure_bank.rs lines 138-146), which now includes
   the attacker's freshly-minted 900 USDC of shares.
5. Attacker immediately calls lending_account_withdraw to redeem their inflated share value,
   capturing ~90% of the 100 USDC emissions despite holding funds for only seconds,
   while Depositor A (30-day holder) receives only ~10 tokens — mirroring the BellumNursery PoC
   result (test_staking) of a 50/90% skew toward the last-second depositor.
``` [6](#0-5)
