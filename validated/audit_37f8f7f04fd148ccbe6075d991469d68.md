### Title
Funded instant-withdraw claims never clear their per-epoch ledger entries, inflating `defaultPendingClaimBasis` and permanently diluting/stranding default-recovery funds - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol])

### Summary
`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` pays a funded instant receipt at par and clears only the aggregate `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]`, but never decrements `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][epoch]` or `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]`. When a default is later finalized for that epoch, `defaultPendingClaimBasis` still counts those already-paid receipts, so `defaultRecoveryPrice` is computed against an inflated basis — the same class of bug as the `maligned` advisory: memory/funds "allocated" under one accounting layout (the per-epoch claim ledger) but "deallocated" under a different one (the aggregate ledger), leaving a stale record that corrupts later accounting.

### Finding Description
In `requestInstantWithdraw` the strategy records three pieces of state: `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]`, `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][currentEpoch]`, and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[currentEpoch]` [1](#0-0) .

The funded claim path clears only the aggregate: [2](#0-1) 

Neither `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` nor `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` is decremented. This is inconsistent with the defaulted-claim path, which does clear them (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][defaultEpoch] = 0; instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[defaultEpoch] -= claimBasis`) [3](#0-2) .

At default finalization, `defaultPendingClaimBasis` adds `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` to `pendingWithdraws` whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` [4](#0-3) , and `finalizeDefaultRecovery` prices the reserve as `reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / totalBasis` [5](#0-4) . Receipts that were already fully funded and paid at par therefore remain inside `totalBasis`, pushing `defaultRecoveryPrice` below the correct pro-rata value. The portion of the reserve "owed" to ghost receipts can never be paid out: when the already-paid user later calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` reverts on `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user] -= claimBasis` / `_burn(_user, claimBasis)` because both were already cleared, so that share of `defaultRecoveryReserve` is permanently stranded.

### Impact Explanation
After a borrower default with any partially-unfunded instant bucket, every honest defaulted claimant (normal `withdrawsRequests`, APR0 receipts, other instant receipts) and every active tranche holder receives `recoveryPrice` diluted by the sum of instant receipts that were already paid at par during the epoch. The corresponding slice of `defaultRecoveryReserve` (plus the equivalent NAV burned/minted for the CDO in `finalizeDefaultRecovery`) is locked forever — a permanent freezing of recovery funds and a direct wealth transfer away from honest claimants, quantified as `alreadyPaidInstantClaims / totalBasis` of the reserve.

### Likelihood Explanation
The sequence requires only unprivileged actions plus honest privileged calls: an attacker requests an instant withdrawal and claims it at par once the CDO funds the instant bucket (normal `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` flow); the borrower then defaults in the same epoch with `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` (i.e., the instant queue was only partially covered — a normal condition, not attacker-controlled privilege). Any single funded-then-defaulted instant receipt triggers the mispricing; no KYC bypass or privileged misbehavior is needed.

### Recommendation
In `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, mirror the defaulted-claim cleanup: when paying a funded receipt, decrement `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][<its epoch>]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[<its epoch>]` by the claimed amount (tracking the request epoch per user, or iterating/capping against the current epoch entry), so `defaultPendingClaimBasis` only ever counts genuinely unpaid instant claims.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork/harness outline (against `test/foundry/IdleCreditVault.t.sol` helpers):

1. Deposit for `userA` (AA tranche) and `userB`; `startEpoch`; enable instant withdrawals via `cdoEpoch.setInstantWithdrawParams`.
2. `userA` calls `requestInstantWithdraw` (receipt recorded in `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[userA][epoch]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]`).
3. Fund part of the instant bucket: borrower repays so `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` pulls less than the full instant basis, leaving `pendingInstantWithdraws > 0`; `userA` calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` and is paid at par. Observe `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]` is unchanged (assert).
4. Drive the epoch into default (`deal(underlying, borrower, 0)`, `stopEpoch`), then `finalizeDefault(recovered, manager)`.
5. Assert `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` still includes `userA`'s already-paid receipt, so `defaultRecoveryPrice` is lower than `reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / trueBasis`.
6. `userB` claims via `claimWithdrawRequest` and receives less than `hisBasis * correctPrice / RECOVERY_FULL`; attempting `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` for `userA` reverts, leaving `defaultRecoveryReserve` permanently nonzero — stranded funds equal to the ghost-claim share.
