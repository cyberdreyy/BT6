### Title
Mid-epoch deposits mint tranche shares for unfunded expected interest, diluting NAV and all withdrawal claims — (`File: contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol`)

### Summary
`depositDuringEpoch` mints `(amount + trancheInterest)` shares to the depositor while crediting the tranche NAV with only `amount` and minting strategy tokens for only `amount`. The `trancheInterest` portion of the minted shares has no underlying or strategy-token backing until the borrower repays interest at `stopEpoch`. During the epoch the vault is therefore fractionally reserved: every outstanding tranche share — and every pending withdrawal receipt priced off `virtualPrice`/`lastNAV` — is diluted by unbacked shares. If the epoch ends with any shortfall (partial borrower default, `previewLossAdjustedWithdrawFunds` loss socialization), the phantom-interest shares still claim real assets, transferring losses to honest LPs — the direct analog of the Amnext "mass-minted Ticket claims drained the pool" bug.

### Finding Description
In `depositDuringEpoch` (contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:656-733):

- `_minted = (_amount + trancheInterest) * _trancheTotSupply / expectedFinal` (line 724) mints shares whose value includes the deposit's *future* interest.
- `_mintShares(_tranche, msg.sender, _minted, _amount)` (line 725) increases `lastNAVAA`/`lastNAVBB` by only `_amount` (IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:355-360).
- `IdleCreditVault(strategy).mintStrategyTokens(_amount)` (line 730) backs only `_amount`, and only `_amount` is sent to the borrower (line 732).

So immediately after the call, `trancheSupply` grew by `_amount + trancheInterest` worth of shares while NAV and strategy-token backing grew by `_amount`. The comment "we seed initial NAV" acknowledges the design, but the gap between minted share value and funded NAV is only healed if the borrower repays principal **and** interest in full at `stopEpoch` (lines 408-436). Any shortfall — borrower default via `onStopEpoch`/`_handleBorrowerDefault` (lines 395-403), or pro-rata loss via `previewLossAdjustedWithdrawFunds` (line 393) — is socialized across supply that includes never-funded interest shares.

Additionally, because `_updateAccounting` runs at request time (line 750) and receipt underlyings are derived from `_trancheToUnderlyings`/`lastNAV` per supply (line 756), the intra-epoch dilution directly reduces the `principal` credited to honest `requestWithdraw` receipts filed after the attacker's deposit.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged KYC-passed lender (`isWalletAllowed`) calls `depositDuringEpoch` during a running epoch (fixed-APR, non-AYS, non-programmable, non-prefunded deployment). For a deposit of `A` with credited `trancheInterest = I`, the attacker instantly holds shares nominally worth `A + I` against only `A` of funded NAV. Theft materializes in two ways:

1. **Default/shortfall socialization:** if `stopEpoch` ends with a loss (borrower repays `< principal + interest`), the unbacked `I` shares dilute the recovered pool pro-rata — the attacker extracts `≈ I * recoveredPool / (supply + minted)` from honest AA/BB holders and pending receipts.
2. **Receipt dilution:** honest users' `requestWithdraw` receipts priced after the deposit embed the depressed `lastNAV/supply` ratio; the loss is `≈ I` spread across all claims.

Loss scales with `I = amount * APR * (remaining + buffer) / (epochDuration + buffer)`, i.e., up to the full epoch interest rate on the deposited amount — for large deposits and high APR this is a material fraction of pool NAV, matching the Medium severity of the reference incident.

### Likelihood Explanation
- Attacker needs only a KYC-passing wallet (explicitly in-scope) and one call during `isEpochRunning` with `block.timestamp < epochEndDate`.
- No privileged role, oracle, or third-party dependency is involved; the over-mint is pure accounting.
- Guards do not stop it: `_trancheTotSupply == 0` is checked but only excludes first deposits; `_skimDonatedAssets` handles raw donations, not share inflation; `_guarded` caps TVL, not share count. Profit requires only an epoch that stops with any shortfall, or honest withdrawal requests filed intra-epoch.
- Caveat: I could not fully trace `_calcTrancheInterestShare`/`_netGainAfterFees` internals or the `IdleCreditVault` claim path within available iterations, so the exact per-mode magnitude (fixed-APR vs APR0) should be confirmed in the PoC; the NAV-vs-supply mismatch itself is directly visible in lines 724-732.

### Recommendation
Either:
- Credit NAV/backing for the interest portion at deposit time (`mintStrategyTokens(_amount + trancheInterest)` and account `trancheInterest` in the tranche NAV or in `expectedEpochInterest`-backed reserves), or
- Mint only `_amount`-backed shares and deliver `trancheInterest` as a separate vested claim settled at `stopEpoch` after the borrower's repayment is verified, so defaulted interest can never dilute funded NAV, or
- Restrict `depositDuringEpoch` further (e.g., cap `trancheInterest` contribution by requiring the borrower to pre-fund expected interest).
