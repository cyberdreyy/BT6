### Title
`unclaimedFees` is not decremented on strategy-token outflows, so `getContractValue` underflows and freezes the credit vault - (File: contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`IdleCDOCreditVault.getContractValue()` computes NAV as `strategyTokenBalance + tokenBalance - unclaimedFees` (contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:125-128). `unclaimedFees` accumulates performance fees in `_updateAccounting` and time-based management fees via `_accrueManagementFee`, but it is never reduced when strategy tokens leave the CDO. Withdraw requests burn CDO-held strategy tokens in `IdleCreditVault.requestWithdraw` / `requestInstantWithdraw` (`_burn(msg.sender, _principal)` / `_burn(msg.sender, _amount)`, contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol:273, 360) while the fee counter stays fixed. This is the same drift class as the report: a balance that accounting treats as reserved (fees) can be consumed by outflows, and once `unclaimedFees > strategyTokenBalance + tokenBalance`, every read of `getContractValue` reverts on underflow.

### Finding Description
The contract itself acknowledges the drift: `_managedContractValue()` clamps `strategyTokenBalance - unclaimedFees` to 0 (contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:133-137), but `getContractValue()` — used by `_updateAccounting`, `virtualPrice`, `startEpoch` (`_calcInterest(getContractValue())`, IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:260), `stopEpochWithDuration` (line 379), and `requestWithdraw` → `_updateAccounting` → `getContractValue` — performs the raw subtraction. During the buffer phase a KYC'd lender calls `requestWithdraw`, which burns principal strategy tokens and calls `_withdrawOps`, shrinking the CDO's strategy-token balance while `unclaimedFees` is untouched. Because management fees keep accruing on the remaining live NAV while that NAV is being burned away, the residual balance can fall below the accrued fee total. From that point `getContractValue()` reverts, permanently freezing `depositAA/depositBB`, `requestWithdraw`, `claimWithdrawRequest` (via subsequent epoch ops), `startEpoch`, and `stopEpochWithDuration`, since all of them reach the underflowing subtraction. Borrower funds already lent cannot be recalled through the epoch machinery, and remaining LPs cannot exit — permanent freezing of funds, triggered by ordinary unprivileged withdraw-request outflows.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent DoS on all value-bearing entry points of the vault and permanent freezing of LP funds and borrower recall flows, caused solely by sequencing of honest privileged calls and unprivileged withdraw requests. Loss magnitude: the entire residual TVL plus pending epoch interest becomes immovable until a manual rescue/upgrade.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires the residual strategy-token balance to fall below accrued `unclaimedFees`. This is reachable when a large fraction of LPs request withdrawal after management/performance fees accrued (management fee accrues per unit time on live NAV and is charged upfront on withdraw requests, so fee mass grows as NAV shrinks). It needs no attacker sophistication beyond a KYC-passing lender calling `requestWithdraw` at the right time; it can also arise purely from honest user outflows. Uncertainty: whether precise mainnet parameters allow unclaimedFees to strictly exceed the remaining balance depends on fee rate and exit fraction, but the code contains no guard preventing it, and the clamped `_managedContractValue` shows the scenario was anticipated for the view path only.

### Recommendation
Mirror the report's recommendation: clamp `getContractValue` the same way `_managedContractValue` does (`bal > fees ? bal - fees : 0`), or decrement `unclaimedFees` proportionally when strategy tokens are burned in `requestWithdraw`/`requestInstantWithdraw`/claim paths so fee accounting tracks actual holdings.
