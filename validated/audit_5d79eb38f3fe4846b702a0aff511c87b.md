**No vulnerability found for this question.**

The behavior described is intentional, documented, and tested design of `delegation_pool.move`, not a bug.

`coins_to_transfer_to_ensure_min_stake` deliberately rounds the unlocked amount up to the delegator's full active balance whenever the residual would fall below `MIN_COINS_ON_SHARES_POOL` (1 APT), specifically to avoid stranding dust shares that can no longer meaningfully participate in reward accounting: [1](#0-0) 

This same rounding rule is symmetric — `coins_to_redeem_to_ensure_min_stake` applies it on the source side and `coins_to_transfer_to_ensure_min_stake` applies it on the destination side — and it's invoked from every stake-moving function (`unlock_internal`, `reactivate_stake`, `withdraw_internal`), always operating strictly on the calling delegator's own balance: [2](#0-1) 

The framework's own test suite explicitly exercises and asserts this exact "round up to move the entire balance" behavior when unlocking near the threshold, confirming it is the intended, audited outcome rather than an exploit: [3](#0-2) 

The delegation pool spec also documents this as a required invariant ("Req #9": active or pending_inactive balances must always be ≥ `MIN_COINS_ON_SHARES_POOL`, enforced by `add_stake`, `unlock`, and `reactivate_stake`), and states it has been audited: [4](#0-3) 

Because the rounding only ever operates on the caller's own active/pending_inactive shares (it can never move more than the delegator's own balance, never touches another shareholder's funds, and never exceeds `active` stake available on the underlying `stake` pool per the `ENOT_ENOUGH_ACTIVE_STAKE_TO_UNLOCK` check), it does not change who can withdraw/unlock/reactivate stake, does not redirect value to another party, and does not permanently strand funds — it merely avoids creating unusable dust balances. This does not satisfy the required impact of altering ownership, withdrawal rights, or accounting correctness across roles, so it does not meet the review's decision standard.

        src_shares_pool: &pool_u64::Pool,
