### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows front-run/back-run sandwiching to steal a disproportionate share of injected yield - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` reproduces the exact share-price-surge pattern described in the BathToken finding: a permissionless caller transfers underlying tokens directly into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and the program instantly raises `asset_share_value` for *all* existing shares, without minting any new shares to the depositor of the emissions. Because the price-per-share jumps atomically and discretely (rather than accruing smoothly like interest), any account holding shares at the moment the deposit lands captures a proportional windfall — including an account that deposited moments before purely to front-run the emissions injection and withdrew moments after.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless: [1](#0-0) . Its accounts struct only requires a valid `mint`/`liquidity_vault` match to the bank and a signer supplying tokens — no group-admin or emissions-admin check is present: [2](#0-1) .

The core logic transfers `amount` tokens into the vault and then recomputes `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, with **no shares minted** for the amount deposited: [3](#0-2) 

This is confirmed by tests showing `total_asset_shares` stays constant while `asset_share_value` jumps by the multiplier `1 + emissions_deposit / total_deposited`, instantly redistributing the injected value pro-rata across whoever currently holds shares: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

This mirrors precisely the root cause in the Rubicon report: a discrete, atomic surge in price-per-share driven by an external deposit that is not proportionally accounted to the party who is about to receive it. Standard `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` mint/burn shares at the *current* `asset_share_value`, with no lockup, minimum holding period, or vesting on newly deposited shares: [6](#0-5) . An attacker who deposits immediately before an `emissions_deposit` transaction lands, and withdraws immediately after, can capture a share of the injected value with essentially zero holding-period risk to existing depositors' assets — the attacker mints shares at the pre-injection price and burns them at the post-injection (higher) price.

### Impact Explanation
Any party who funds `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (e.g., the protocol distributing incentive/reward tokens to existing lenders) has a portion of that injected value siphoned off by an opportunistic depositor who has no genuine, sustained economic exposure to the bank. This is a direct value-redirection from legitimate long-term depositors (and/or the emissions funder, whose intent is to reward *existing* long-term depositors) to a MEV actor who merely sandwiches the transaction. The larger the emissions deposit relative to the pool's total assets, and the larger the capital the attacker can transiently commit, the larger the stolen fraction — in the limiting case (attacker deposits an amount much larger than existing TVL) the attacker captures nearly all of the injected emissions.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is permissionless and callable by anyone with no restriction preventing observation of a pending emissions-deposit transaction, and Solana supports transaction-ordering manipulation (e.g., via Jito bundles or leader-adjacent submission) to achieve front-run/back-run sequencing. No cooldown, minimum holding duration, or share-vesting mechanism was found gating newly minted shares from immediately being eligible for the increased `asset_share_value` on withdrawal. The only friction is the bank's `deposit_limit` (if configured) capping how much capital an attacker can inject to maximize their captured share, and a group-level withdrawal rate limiter (`record_withdrawal_outflow`) that could throttle the follow-up withdrawal in some configurations — these reduce but do not eliminate exploitability, and many banks/groups may not have restrictive limits configured. This is comparable in likelihood to the original C4 finding, which was rated High severity.

### Recommendation
Do not apply the emissions injection uniformly and instantly to `asset_share_value` for shares that were minted immediately prior to the call. Options:
- Vest/stream the value increase over time (e.g., linearly release the injected amount into `asset_share_value` over a cooldown window) instead of an atomic jump, similar to how interest accrues continuously via `accrue_interest` rather than in lump-sum jumps.
- Snapshot share balances (or require a minimum holding duration) so only shares that existed for some minimum period prior to the emissions deposit are eligible for the full uplift.
- Alternatively, restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to be admin/emissions-admin gated and executed atomically alongside deposit-freeze/withdraw-freeze safeguards, or require it to be bundled such that deposit/withdraw cannot occur in the same slot/adjacent slots as the emissions deposit.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `A` currently has `total_assets = 100 USDC`, `total_asset_shares = 100`, `asset_share_value = 1.0`.
2. The emissions funder is about to call `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount = 100 USDC)` (this instruction is public and permissionless per [1](#0-0) ).
3. Attacker detects the pending transaction and front-runs with `lending_account_deposit(9900 USDC)`, receiving `9900` shares at `asset_share_value = 1.0` (total shares now `10000`, total assets `10000`).
4. Emissions-deposit transaction executes: `asset_share_value` becomes `(10000 + 100) / 10000 = 1.01` per [7](#0-6) .
5. Attacker immediately withdraws all `9900` shares via `lending_account_withdraw(withdraw_all = true)`, receiving `9900 * 1.01 ≈ 9999` USDC — a profit of ≈`99` USDC (99% of the 100 USDC emissions injection), leaving the original depositor with only ≈`1` USDC of the intended reward, despite holding their position the entire time.

async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {

    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

        .try_bank_deposit(

            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);

function assertSameBankDeposit(
  sharesBefore: { value: number[] },
  sharesAfter: { value: number[] },
  shareValueBefore: { value: number[] },
  shareValueAfter: { value: number[] },
  liquidityVaultBefore: number,
  liquidityVaultAfter: number,
  emissionsDepositAmount: number,
) {
  const beforeVal = wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(shareValueBefore).toNumber();
  const totalDeposited =
    wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(sharesBefore).toNumber() * beforeVal;
  assert.equal(
    wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(sharesAfter).toString(),
    wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(sharesBefore).toString(),
    "total asset shares should be unchanged",
  );
  // Should be roughly equal. If at all interest accrual happens, time barely passes between
  // this and the last one.
  assert.approximately(
    wrappedI80F48toBigNumber(shareValueAfter).toNumber(),
    beforeVal * (1 + emissionsDepositAmount / totalDeposited),
    beforeVal * 10 ** -10,
  );
  assert.equal(
    liquidityVaultAfter - liquidityVaultBefore,
    emissionsDepositAmount,
  );

/// 1. Accrue interest
/// 2. Find the user's existing bank account for the asset withdrawn
/// 3. Record asset decrease in the bank account
/// 4. Transfer funds from the bank's liquidity vault to the signer's token account
/// 5. Verify that the user account is in a healthy state
///
/// Will error if there is no existing asset <=> borrowing is not allowed.
pub fn lending_account_withdraw<'info>(
    mut ctx: Context<'info, LendingAccountWithdraw<'info>>,
    amount: u64,
    withdraw_all: Option<bool>,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    let LendingAccountWithdraw {
        marginfi_account: marginfi_account_loader,
        destination_token_account,
        liquidity_vault: bank_liquidity_vault,
        token_program,
        bank_liquidity_vault_authority,
        bank: bank_loader,
        group: marginfi_group_loader,
        ..
    } = ctx.accounts;
    let clock = Clock::get()?;

    let withdraw_all = withdraw_all.unwrap_or(false);
    let mut marginfi_account = marginfi_account_loader.load_mut()?;
    let group = marginfi_group_loader.load()?;

    {
        let maybe_bank_mint = {
            let bank = bank_loader.load()?;
            utils::maybe_take_bank_mint(&mut ctx.remaining_accounts, &bank, token_program.key)?
        };

        let in_receivership_or_order_execution =
            marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP | ACCOUNT_IN_ORDER_EXECUTION);
        let mut bank = bank_loader.load_mut()?;
        validate_bank_state(&bank, InstructionKind::FailsInPausedState)?;

        // Fetch oracle price for rate limiting and deleverage tracking
        // When group rate limiter is enabled, oracle is required
        let group_rate_limit_enabled = group.rate_limiter.is_enabled();
        let price = if in_receivership_or_order_execution || group_rate_limit_enabled {
            let price = fetch_asset_price_for_bank_low_bias(
                &bank_loader.key(),
                &bank,
                &clock,
                ctx.remaining_accounts,
            )?;

            // Validate price is non-zero during liquidation/deleverage to prevent exploits
            if in_receivership_or_order_execution {
                check!(price > I80F48::ZERO, MarginfiError::ZeroAssetPrice);
            }

            price
        } else {
            I80F48::ZERO
        };

        bank.accrue_interest(
            clock.unix_timestamp,
            &group,
            #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
            bank_loader.key(),
        )?;

        let liquidity_vault_authority_bump = bank.liquidity_vault_authority_bump;

        let in_receivership = marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP);
        let lending_account = &mut marginfi_account.lending_account;
        let mut bank_account =
            BankAccountWrapper::find(&bank_loader.key(), &mut bank, lending_account)?;

        let (amount_pre_fee, share_amount) = if withdraw_all {
            // Note: In liquidation, we still want this passed on the books
            bank_account.withdraw_all(in_receivership)?
        } else {
            let amount_pre_fee = maybe_bank_mint
                .as_ref()
                .map(|mint| {
                    utils::calculate_pre_fee_spl_deposit_amount(
                        mint.to_account_info(),
                        amount,
                        clock.epoch,
                    )
                })
                .transpose()?
                .unwrap_or(amount);

            let share_amount = bank_account.withdraw(I80F48::from_num(amount_pre_fee))?;

            (amount_pre_fee, share_amount)
        };
