### Title
Fee-on-transfer underlyings mint unbacked strategy tokens and inflate the default-recovery reserve — (`File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol`)

### Summary
`IdleCreditVault.deposit` mints strategy tokens 1:1 with the stated `_amount` rather than the amount actually received, and `collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` and `finalizeDefaultRecovery` credit/pull stated amounts while accounting assumes the full sum arrived. If the vault `token` charges a transfer fee (e.g. USDT with its fee enabled), the strategy's claim liabilities exceed its actual underlying balance by the fee, so the last claimants' transfers revert and recovery funds are permanently frozen.

### Finding Description
The CDO side is partially protected: `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit` measures the token balance delta and mints tranche shares only on the amount actually received. However it then forwards the *stated* `_amount` to `IIdleCDOStrategy(strategy).deposit(_amount)` [1](#0-0) .

In `IdleCreditVault.deposit`, the strategy pulls `_amount` from the CDO and mints exactly `_amount` strategy tokens back to it, without measuring the received delta [2](#0-1) . With a fee-on-transfer token the strategy receives `_amount - fee` but issues `_amount` claims. The same pattern repeats on the funding legs:

- `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` and `collectWithdrawFunds` decrement `pendingInstantWithdraws`/`pendingWithdraws` by the stated `_amount` while receiving less [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) .
- `finalizeDefaultRecovery` books `_recoveredAmount + prefundedReserve + defaultRecoveryReserve` into `defaultRecoveryReserve` and computes `recoveryPrice` on it, but pulls `_recoveredAmount` via `safeTransferFrom`, receiving less than booked [5](#0-4) .

The broken invariant is solvency / "one receipt one payout": minted strategy-token claims and the recovery reserve exceed the contract's real underlying balance by the cumulative transfer fees.

### Impact Explanation
The shortfall is socialized onto the last claimants. `_transferFundedClaim` and `_transferDefaultRecovery` send the recorded amounts [6](#0-5) ; once earlier claimants have withdrawn, the balance is insufficient and `safeTransfer` reverts — or the reserve guard at line 904 reverts first — permanently freezing the last ~fee% of funded claims and default-recovery payouts. Additionally, deposits routed through the strategy while an epoch runs create strategy tokens worth more than the collateral actually forwarded to the borrower, so at `stopEpoch` the borrower is owed interest on `_amount` while only `_amount - fee` ever entered the system, forcing an underfunding that lands on active LPs via `collectWithdrawFunds` loss pricing.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. It requires the vault `token` to be a fee-on-transfer asset; USDT — a realistic pool currency for these credit vaults — contains a (currently disabled) fee switch, and governance-accepted ERC20s with transfer fees exist. No privileged misbehavior is needed; an unprivileged depositor triggers the mismatch on every deposit, and every borrower/manager funding leg amplifies it. Existing guards (skim/donation isolation, `_onlyIdleCDO`, reserve check in `_transferFundedClaim`) do not correct the stated-vs-received accounting; they merely make the eventual revert explicit.

### Recommendation
Measure the balance delta on every inbound `safeTransferFrom` and use the received amount for minting and accounting:

- In `IdleCreditVault.deposit`: snapshot `underlyingToken.balanceOf(address(this))` before the pull, mint `received = after - before` strategy tokens, and return/track `received` (also for `totEpochDeposits`).
- In `collectWithdrawFunds`/`collectInstantWithdrawFunds`: reduce the pending buckets by the received delta.
- In `finalizeDefaultRecovery`: add only the actually received `_recoveredAmount` to `defaultRecoveryReserve`.
- Alternatively, document and enforce a allowlist of non-fee-on-transfer underlyings at deployment.

### Proof of Concept
A Foundry PoC deploys `IdleCreditVault` with a mock FoT USDC (e.g. 1% burn-on-transfer) wired to an `IdleCDOCreditVault`:

1. Buffer phase: lender calls `depositAA(100e6)`. CDO mints AA shares on the ~99e6 it received, but `IdleCreditVault.deposit` mints `100e6` strategy tokens to the CDO while holding only ~99e6 underlying.
2. Honest manager starts the epoch; borrower receives 99e6 but `pendingWithdraws` accounting and strategy-token supply assume 100e6 of debt.
3. After epoch stop and a `requestWithdraw` funded via `collectWithdrawFunds` (which itself receives fee-less than `_amount`), the first claimants withdraw successfully; the final claimant's `claimWithdrawRequest` reverts in `_transferFundedClaim` on `balance - reserve < _amount` or on `safeTransfer` insufficient balance — the ~1% residual is permanently locked.
4. For the recovery leg: borrower defaults, recovery source approves `finalizeDefaultRecovery(100e6)`; only ~99e6 arrives but `defaultRecoveryReserve` is set to 100e6, so `defaultRecoveryPrice` overstates recovery and the last `_transferDefaultRecovery` call reverts, freezing the residual.

Key assertion: `strategyToken.totalSupply() > underlyingToken.balanceOf(strategy)` immediately after deposit, and the final claim reverts, quantifying the frozen amount as the aggregate fee.

  function _transferFundedClaim(address _user, uint256 _amount) internal {
    if (_amount == 0) return;
    uint256 reserve = defaultRecoveryReserve;
    if (reserve != 0) {
      uint256 balance = underlyingToken.balanceOf(address(this));
      // This should be unreachable when accounting is consistent. Keep the guard so old funded
      // receipts can never spend underlyings reserved for default recovery claimants.
      if (balance < reserve || balance - reserve < _amount) revert NotAllowed();
    }
    underlyingToken.safeTransfer(_user, _amount);
  }

  /// @notice Transfer default recovery reserve to a user.
  /// @param _user claim receiver
  /// @param _amount amount to transfer
  function _transferDefaultRecovery(address _user, uint256 _amount) internal {
    if (_amount == 0) return;
    // Every defaulted or post-default claim consumes the isolated recovery reserve.
    defaultRecoveryReserve -= _amount;
    underlyingToken.safeTransfer(_user, _amount);
  }
