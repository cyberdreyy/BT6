### Title
Builder index reuse allows a finalized `BuilderPendingPayment` to be paid out of an unrelated new builder's balance — ([File: specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md])

### Summary
`specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md` explicitly documents that builder registry slots (`BuilderIndex`) are reused once a builder exits, is swept to zero balance, and passes its `withdrawable_epoch`. The bid-payment accounting for a builder, however, is tracked purely by `BuilderIndex` — never by `pubkey` — in `state.builder_pending_payments` and `state.builder_pending_withdrawals`. If a builder's slot is reassigned to a brand-new builder (new pubkey) while an older bid's payment for that same slot is still pending finalization/withdrawal, the balance debited and the balance-mutation authority end up applying to the *new* builder rather than the one that actually made the bid — the same "stale-state-tied-to-a-removed-entity-gets-reused" bug class as the reported NFTFloorOracle finding, where `feederPrice` outlived `assetFeederMap` deletion and was reused for a re-added, unrelated asset.

### Finding Description
Builder-index reuse is spec-sanctioned: [1](#0-0) 

A slot becomes reusable purely based on balance and epoch, with no reference to any in-flight payment obligations tied to that index: [2](#0-1) 

`add_builder_to_registry` fully overwrites the `Builder` container at that index for the new pubkey: [3](#0-2) 

Meanwhile, a builder's execution-payload bid records a `BuilderPendingPayment` keyed only by `builder_index` (not pubkey), inside the fixed-length `builder_pending_payments` vector: [4](#0-3) 

Each epoch, `process_builder_pending_payments` unconditionally finalizes any payment whose accumulated attestation weight reached quorum into the unbounded `builder_pending_withdrawals` queue — again referencing only `builder_index`, with no check that the builder at that index is still the one that made the bid: [5](#0-4) 

Finally, when `process_withdrawals` drains `builder_pending_withdrawals`, it looks up `state.builders[payment.builder_index]` and decreases *that* builder's balance by `min(amount, builder.balance)`, paying the amount to `fee_recipient`: [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

This is structurally identical to the NFTFloorOracle issue: `assetFeederMap[_asset]` was deleted (its `index` reset) but `feederPrice` — data logically owned by the *specific occupancy* of that mapping slot — persisted and was silently reused once the slot was repopulated with an unrelated occupant (the asset added back). Here, `builder_pending_payments`/`builder_pending_withdrawals` entries are logically owned by the specific builder that made the bid, but they are indexed purely by `BuilderIndex`, a slot that the spec explicitly allows to be reassigned to an unrelated new builder. Nothing in `process_builder_exit_request`, `get_index_for_new_builder`, or `process_builder_pending_payments` cross-checks that the builder occupying the index at settlement time is the same one that earned/owes the payment.

### Impact Explanation
If the slot-reuse window and the pending-payment window overlap, a subsequent settlement (`get_builder_withdrawals` inside `process_withdrawals`) debits Gwei from a completely unrelated builder's balance — a builder mutated without its authority, and Gwei paid to a party (the old bid's `fee_recipient`) that the new builder never committed to pay. This falls squarely under the Critical/High impact categories: "Gwei created, destroyed or paid to a non-owner" and "a validator or builder mutated without its authority."

### Likelihood Explanation
I was not able to fully verify, within the available tool budget, the exact definition of `get_pending_balance_to_withdraw_for_builder` (used as a gate in `process_builder_exit_request`) or the precise timing/ordering guarantees between builder exit/sweep and the 2-epoch (`2*SLOTS_PER_EPOCH`) lifetime of `builder_pending_payments`. It is possible that `get_pending_balance_to_withdraw_for_builder` or another unseen invariant prevents a builder from exiting (and thus becoming sweepable/reusable) while it still has an un-finalized pending payment, which would close this window. Because of this gap, I can confirm the root-cause code pattern (index-only bookkeping across a spec-documented reusable registry slot) but cannot fully confirm reachability of the overlap without further inspection of `get_pending_balance_to_withdraw_for_builder` and `initiate_builder_exit`, which the index did not surface in the searches performed.

### Recommendation
- Bind `BuilderPendingPayment`/`BuilderPendingWithdrawal` entries to the builder's `pubkey` in addition to (or instead of) `builder_index`, and validate the pubkey still matches at withdrawal-settlement time before debiting `state.builders[builder_index].balance`.
- Alternatively, prevent `get_index_for_new_builder` from returning a slot that still has any outstanding `builder_pending_payments` or `builder_pending_withdrawals` entries referencing it, mirroring the recommended mitigation in the original report of proactively clearing/consuming residual per-slot state before allowing reuse.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (pending full verification of exit-gating semantics, per Likelihood note above):
1. Builder A (index `i`) submits a winning bid; a `BuilderPendingPayment` is recorded at `state.builder_pending_payments[SLOTS_PER_EPOCH + slot % SLOTS_PER_EPOCH]` with `builder_index = i` [8](#0-7) .
2. Builder A initiates exit and is later swept to `balance == 0`, `withdrawable_epoch <= current_epoch`.
3. A new builder B (different pubkey) deposits; `get_index_for_new_builder` returns slot `i` and `add_builder_to_registry` overwrites it entirely for B [9](#0-8) .
4. `process_builder_pending_payments` later finds A's old payment at quorum and appends `BuilderPendingWithdrawal(builder_index=i, ...)` to `builder_pending_withdrawals` [10](#0-9) .
5. `process_withdrawals` settles it by decreasing `state.builders[i].balance` — now builder B's balance — and paying A's bid `fee_recipient`, transferring Gwei out of builder B without B's authorization.

```python
def process_builder_pending_payments(state: BeaconState) -> None:
    """
    Processes the builder pending payments from the previous epoch.
    """
    quorum = get_builder_payment_quorum_threshold(state)
    for payment in state.builder_pending_payments[:SLOTS_PER_EPOCH]:
        if payment.weight >= quorum:
            state.builder_pending_withdrawals.append(payment.withdrawal)

    old_payments = state.builder_pending_payments[SLOTS_PER_EPOCH:]
    state.builder_pending_payments[:SLOTS_PER_EPOCH] = old_payments
    new_payments = [BuilderPendingPayment.empty() for _ in range(SLOTS_PER_EPOCH)]
    state.builder_pending_payments[SLOTS_PER_EPOCH:] = new_payments

def get_index_for_new_builder(state: BeaconState) -> BuilderIndex:
    for index, builder in enumerate(state.builders):
        if builder.withdrawable_epoch <= get_current_epoch(state) and builder.balance == 0:
            return BuilderIndex(index)
    return BuilderIndex(len(state.builders))
