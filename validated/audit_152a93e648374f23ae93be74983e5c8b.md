Based on my review of the delegation_pool, staking_contract, and vesting modules, I did not find a local, unprivileged root cause that reproduces the "compounding slippage" bug-class as a stake/lockup accounting or role-boundary break.

Key findings that ruled out candidate paths:
- The `add_stake` fee mechanism in `delegation_pool.move` uses a `NULL_SHAREHOLDER` placeholder that is deterministically released once `pending_active` returns to zero on synchronization, and this is explicitly tested (`test_add_stake_single`, `test_add_stake_many`), so it does not permanently strand value. [1](#0-0) 
- `withdraw_internal` in `delegation_pool.move` correctly isolates "excess" pending_inactive stake via a reactivate/withdraw/unlock sequence to avoid advancing the observed lockup cycle indefinitely, and this exact accounting is covered by regression tests. [2](#0-1) 
- `staking_contract::request_commission` and `unlock_stake` are gated to staker/operator/beneficiary only, and commission is always settled before principal accounting changes, preserving role boundaries.
