### Title
Claimed instant-withdraw receipts are not removed from `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`, so default recovery price is diluted by already-paid claims - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
Analog of "`fundingTotals` not updated when deposits are refunded": `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` is a cached aggregate claim basis used by `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` to compute the default recovery price, but `claimInstantWithdrawRequest()` never decrements it (nor the per-user `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` entries). If a borrower defaults after the instant queue was only partially funded, users who already claimed their funded portion remain counted in the recovery basis, diluting `defaultRecoveryPrice` for all other claimants and stranding reserve dust.

### Finding Description
`requestInstantWithdraw` records the claim both per-user and in the aggregate `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[currentEpoch]`, and bumps `pendingInstantWithdraws` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:356-375`). `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` reduces `pendingInstantWithdraws` by the amount actually funded (`:398-403`), which can be partial when `pendingInstant > totUnderlyings` at `startEpoch` (`IdleCDOEpochVariant.sol:279-289`).

`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` burns the user's receipt and pays out funded underlying via `_transferFundedClaim`, but clears only `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` — it never touches `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` or `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch` (`:380-393`).

Later, `finalizeDefaultRecovery` computes the recovery price as `reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / totalBasis`, where `totalBasis` includes `defaultPendingClaimBasis()`, which adds the full `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` (`:644-649`, `:679-688`). The helper `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve` correctly models prefunded-but-unclaimed cash (`instantBasis - pendingInstant`), but it does not account for the portion already paid out to claimants — that portion was funded into the vault and then transferred out, so it is neither in the reserve nor removed from the basis.

Broken invariant: `totalBasis` must equal outstanding (unpaid) claims; instead it equals historical requested claims, so `recoveryPrice` is systematically understated.

### Impact Explanation
After a partial instant-queue funding followed by a borrower default, every token already paid to an instant claimant is double-counted in `totalBasis`. `defaultRecoveryPrice` is therefore lower than the true pro-rata recovery, so defaulted-epoch receipt holders (normal pending withdraws via `_claimDefaultedWithdrawRequest`, unfunded instant claims via `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`) and active tranche holders (via `defaultBBNav`/DefaultDistributor) recover less than they should. The excess `defaultRecoveryReserve` has no corresponding basis to claim against and is stranded as dust in the strategy — a direct, quantified loss to remaining claimants equal to `paidOutInstant * (1 - price_correct/price_computed)` plus stuck dust.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a specific sequence: instant withdraw requests exist, `startEpoch` partially funds the queue (borrower cash shortfall), some users claim, then the borrower defaults and `finalizeDefaultRecovery` runs. The trigger actions (requesting and claiming instant withdrawals) are ordinary unprivileged user operations; the default itself is caused by the honest borrower failing to repay. Not exploitable on demand by the attacker, but reachable in normal operation whenever the above sequence occurs — comparable in likelihood to the reported OpenQ issue.

### Recommendation
Decrement the cached basis when instant claims are paid: in `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` (and in `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`), reduce `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][reqEpoch]` and `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[reqEpoch]` by the claimed amount, mirroring how `pendingWithdraws` is decremented in `_claimDefaultedWithdrawRequest` (`:778`). Alternatively, track paid instant claims and subtract them in `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve`/`defaultPendingClaimBasis`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork sketch:

1. KYC'd users deposit into AA/BB; owner calls `startEpoch`. Borrower (honest) leaves the pool short of cash so only part of the instant queue is funded — or more simply: users request instant withdrawals during buffer, `startEpoch` runs with `pendingInstant > totUnderlyings`, so `pendingInstantWithdraws` stays non-zero.
2. After `instantWithdrawDeadline`, user A calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` and receives funded underlying; `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epoch]` still contains A's amount.
3. Borrower fails repayment at `stopEpoch` → `_handleBorrowerDefault`; owner calls `finalizeDefault`/`finalizeDefaultRecovery`.
4. Assert: `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` includes A's already-paid claim, so `defaultRecoveryPrice < reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / outstandingBasis`.
5. Assert: user B's `_claimDefaultedWithdrawRequest` payout is strictly less than `B_basis * reserveAmount / outstandingBasis`, and after all claims `defaultRecoveryReserve > 0` remains unclaimable.

Uncertainty note: I could not fully verify the body of `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` and `_transferDefaultRecovery` (index excerpt cut off before those functions); if they already reconcile `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` on the defaulted-epoch claim path, the dilution is limited to claims paid *before* finalization, which is the scenario described above.
