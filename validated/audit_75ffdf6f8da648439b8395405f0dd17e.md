### Title
Stale instant-withdraw receipts from pre-default epochs survive `finalizeDefaultRecovery` and are never haircut or cleared - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
`requestInstantWithdraw` records receipts under the *current* epoch key (`instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][epochNumber]`), but `defaultPendingClaimBasis` and `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` only ever look at `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` / `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]` — the single epoch active at finalization. Instant receipts opened in an earlier epoch that were never fully funded via `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` are therefore excluded from the recovery basis, never cleared, and remain payable at full par through `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` → `_transferFundedClaim` — exactly the Hats pattern where `linkedTreeRequests` survives `unlinkTopHatFromTree` and can be "approved" later at stale terms.

### Finding Description
- `requestInstantWithdraw` tags a receipt only with `currentEpoch = epochNumber` and bumps `instantWithdrawsRequests`, `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][currentEpoch]`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[currentEpoch]`, `pendingInstantWithdraws` (lines 356-375).
- When the epoch rolls over (`deposit()` during a running epoch increments `epochNumber`, lines 607-611), an unfunded/partially funded instant receipt keeps `pendingInstantWithdraws > 0` but its claim basis stays keyed to the *old* epoch.
- At default finalization, `defaultPendingClaimBasis` adds only `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` — the *new* current epoch (lines 644-649). The attacker's old-epoch receipt is absent from `totalBasis`, so `recoveryPrice = reserve / totalBasis` is computed as if that claim did not exist.
- `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` clears only `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[user][defaultRecoveryEpoch]` (lines 842-856). The stale old-epoch entry survives, and `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` then pays the *entire* `instantWithdrawsRequests[user]` balance (stale + funded) at par through `_transferFundedClaim` (lines 380-393).

There is no equivalent of "delete on unlink": nothing deletes or haircut-marks pre-default-epoch instant receipts when the default transition occurs.

### Impact Explanation
Two concrete failure modes, both reachable by an unprivileged tranche holder:

1. **Reserve dilution / insolvency**: because the stale receipt is excluded from `totalBasis`, `defaultRecoveryPrice` is set higher than the fair haircut. Legitimate default-epoch claimants are over-paid relative to a basis that should include the attacker's claim; the attacker's surviving par claim then either drains non-reserve underlying that should back other funded claims, or pushes the reserve-balance check so that later claimants revert — last-in claimants are permanently unpaid.
2. **Broken "one receipt, one haircut" invariant**: the attacker escapes the recovery haircut entirely. Any non-reserve balance in the strategy (leftover funded instant amounts collected via `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, or dust above `defaultRecoveryReserve`) is withdrawable by the attacker at 100 cents on the dollar while every other defaulted receipt is haircut — theft of unclaimed yield proportional to the stale receipt size.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-low but credible: it requires an instant-withdraw request to remain at least partially unfunded across an epoch boundary — normal whenever `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` is called late or partially — followed by a borrower default and `finalizeDefaultRecovery`. All privileged actors (manager, borrower, queue) remain honest; the sequencing is driven entirely by ordinary epoch timing. No guard stops it: `_transferFundedClaim`'s reserve check (lines 897-907) protects the reserve but not non-reserve funds, and nothing bounds `instantWithdrawsRequests[user]` to the funded amount.

### Recommendation
Mirror the Hats fix (`delete linkedTreeRequests` on unlink): when finalizing default recovery, sweep *all* pending instant receipt epochs, not just `epochNumber`. Concretely:
- Include the full outstanding instant basis (e.g., `instantWithdrawsRequests` aggregate or a running `totalInstantClaims` counter) in `defaultPendingClaimBasis`, not only `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]`.
- In `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest`, clear every epoch entry for the user (or track a per-user aggregate receipt basis) so no pre-default receipt survives at par after finalization.
- Alternatively, on `stopEpoch`/epoch rollover, migrate `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[oldEpoch]` forward so the claim basis always lives under the active epoch key.
