No vulnerability found for this question.

**Reasoning:**

`staking_contract::add_stake` requires the caller to be the `staker` signer who already owns the `Store`/`StakingContract` entry for that `operator` — `assert_staking_contract_exists(staker_address, operator)` is checked against `signer::address_of(staker)`, and the `StakingContract.owner_cap` was created for that exact staker when they called `create_staking_contract` [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) . So there is no cross-account path: a caller can only ever add stake to (and mutate `principal` for) a staking contract they themselves created and own. This fails the review's requirement that the attacker not already own the pool — here the "staker" is always the owner by construction.

Within that self-owned scope, neither edge case produces desync:
- `amount = 0`: `coin::withdraw` yields a zero coin; `stake::add_stake_with_cap` detects `amount == 0`, destroys the zero coin, and returns before touching `stake_pool.active`/`pending_active` [3](#0-2) . Back in `staking_contract::add_stake`, `staking_contract.principal += amount` also adds zero [4](#0-3) , so `principal` and the pool's actual active balance move together (both by 0).
- Overflow amount: Move's native `u64` addition aborts on overflow rather than wrapping, and the formal spec explicitly encodes this: `aborts_if staking_contract.principal + amount > MAX_U64` [5](#0-4) . Additionally, `coin::withdraw<AptosCoin>(staker, amount)` would itself abort if the staker doesn't actually hold `amount` coins, since Move coin balances can't exceed real minted supply — there's no way to conjure an overflow amount that also passes withdrawal. The transaction reverts atomically; no partial/desynchronized state is left behind.

Since `principal` and `stake::add_stake_with_cap`'s pool mutation happen as two steps of the same atomic transaction, and both use the exact same `amount` value, they cannot be pushed out of sync by this entrypoint — invalid amounts abort the whole transaction rather than partially applying.

        staking_contract.principal += amount;
        let pool_address = staking_contract.pool_address;

    }
