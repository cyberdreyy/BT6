### Title
Flash-loan-funded share dilution attack on permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows theft of depositor rewards - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
The Party crowdfund bug class is: a permissionless action that atomically alters the proportional claim on a shared pool of value can be exploited by a flash-loan-funded actor who enters just before the value-injection event, captures a share of the injected value, and exits in the same transaction/bundle — extracting value that rightfully belonged to long-term participants, with no time-based protection against it. Marginfi has a structurally identical primitive in `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, a permissionless instruction that injects tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault and instantly raises `asset_share_value` for every current depositor pro-rata to their shares [3](#0-2) .

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transfers `amount` of the bank's underlying mint into the liquidity vault and recomputes `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, without minting any new shares [4](#0-3) . This is confirmed by the test suite, which shows the share value multiplier is `1 + emissions_deposit / total_deposited` applied uniformly to every current shareholder [5](#0-4) .

Because `total_asset_shares` at the moment of the call determines who gets to split the injected reward, an attacker can:
1. Take out a flash loan of the bank's underlying token.
2. Call `lending_account_deposit` to acquire a large number of shares in the target bank immediately before a legitimate (admin- or third-party-funded) call to `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lands (via Jito bundle ordering or simple mempool racing, since the instruction is permissionless and requires no special authority — [6](#0-5) ).
3. Immediately call `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) in the same transaction/bundle to redeem shares at the new, inflated `asset_share_value`, then repay the flash loan.

Neither `lending_account_deposit` nor `lending_account_withdraw` impose any minimum holding period, vesting, or same-block restriction [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) , and the protocol's own flashloan feature explicitly supports atomic borrow/arbitrary-CPI/repay sequences in one transaction [7](#0-6) , demonstrating the pattern is fully supported by the protocol's transaction model.

Mathematically: if `D` is the attacker's flash-loaned deposit, `S` is the pre-existing total shares/assets (share value ≈ 1), and `F` is the externally-funded emissions deposit, the attacker's profit is `D·F / (S + D)`, extracted directly from the `F` that should have accrued entirely to pre-existing depositors. This grows toward the full value of `F` as `D → ∞`, meaning a sufficiently large flash loan can steal nearly all of a reward distribution.

### Impact Explanation
Any permissionless or externally-sponsored `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (a documented, intended reward-distribution mechanism per the emissions guide [8](#0-7) ) can be sandwiched by a capital-free attacker, diverting depositor rewards to the attacker and diluting genuine long-term depositors. This is a direct value-redirection/theft vulnerability with financial impact proportional to the size of reward deposits and available flash-loan liquidity for the bank's mint.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-high: the instruction is explicitly permissionless by design [6](#0-5) , requires no privileged access, and the attack requires only (a) flash-loan liquidity for the underlying mint and (b) the ability to bundle transactions atomically around the targeted emissions deposit (trivial on Solana via Jito bundles, or race conditions in the mempool). The economics scale favorably with attacker capital since profit is unbounded by the attacker's own funds — only by flash loan liquidity available for that mint.

### Recommendation
- Require that whoever funds `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` also be the recipient, or restrict the caller to the group admin/authorized distributor so the injection cannot be timed by an unrelated, self-interested party.
- Snapshot eligible shareholders and their share counts at a fixed point (e.g., a prior block/slot or via a Merkle snapshot) rather than crediting whoever holds shares at call time.
- Introduce a minimum holding period or a fee/cooldown on deposit-then-withdraw within the same or adjacent slots for a given bank, breaking the atomicity that makes the sandwich profitable.
- Alternatively, mint new shares for the deposited emissions amount and distribute them only to a snapshotted eligible set, instead of adjusting `asset_share_value` for the current live share pool.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `X` has existing depositors with `total_asset_shares = S` and `asset_share_value = 1`.
2. Attacker observes (or bundles with) a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount = F)` transaction, funded by a third party.
3. Attacker flash-loans `D` of the bank's mint and calls `lending_account_deposit` on bank `X`, acquiring `D` shares (per [9](#0-8) ).
4. The `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(F)` transaction executes, recomputing `asset_share_value = (S + D + F) / (S + D)` (per [4](#0-3) ).
5. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` with `withdraw_all = true` in the same bundle, redeeming `D · (S + D + F)/(S + D)` tokens (per [10](#0-9) ), repays the flash loan of `D`, and nets a profit of `D·F/(S+D)`, taken from the pre-existing depositors' expected share of `F`.

Note: this PoC is derived analytically from the confirmed share-value math and the confirmed absence of anti-sandwich guards; I did not execute it against a live/bankrun test harness, so exact numeric behavior (rounding, fee edge cases) should be validated with an actual bankrun/trident test before treating this as fully proven.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

## Summary

Some banks, including banks from other venues, might offer a token incentive to depositors, or even
to borrowers. We call a set of incentives over time a "Campaign".

For example, a Campaign might distribute 7 tokens of A to lenders per week (one per day). Each
lender's share is determined on a pro-rata basis in real time. If there are two lenders, each
depositing the same amount, then each will be 3.5 tokens per week.

Friday. On Saturday and beyond, User 1 gets $1/(1+6)= 0.143$ tokens, and User 2 gets $6/(1+6)=0.857$
tokens/day.

Emissions/incentives are delivered by airdrop to the Account's authority, typically on Wednesday, in
no particular order. In the above example, User 1 would get $0.5 + 0.5 * 0.143 * 5 = 1.715$ tokens
