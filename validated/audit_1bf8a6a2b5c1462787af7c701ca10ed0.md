### Title
Deposits credit tranche tokens for the full requested `_amount` without measuring actual underlying received, leaving the vault insolvent with fee-on-transfer or less-than-amount tokens - (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The bug class from the external report — crediting claims based on the nominal transfer amount rather than the received balance delta — maps directly onto the deposit/mint flow of `IdleCreditVault`. The strategy's `price()` is hardcoded to `oneToken` (1:1), so `IdleCDOEpochVariant` mints tranche tokens proportional to the user-supplied `_amount` while `safeTransferFrom` may deliver less. Withdraw claims then pay out the full recorded receipt amounts, so the vault's real balance falls below total obligations and the last claimants cannot be paid.

### Finding Description
`IdleCreditVault.price()` unconditionally returns `oneToken`, so the CDO mints AA/BB tranche tokens equal to the deposited `_amount` in underlying units, regardless of how much underlying actually arrives [1](#0-0) . The CDO's deposit path pulls the underlying with `tokenContract.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, strategy, _amount)` (the only underlying `transferFrom` in `IdleCDO.sol`) and never compares `balanceOf(strategy)` before and after.

On the payout side, withdraw claims transfer the full recorded amounts: `_claimFundedWithdrawRequest` computes `amount = normalAmount + apr0PrincipalAmount + apr0InterestAmount` and calls `_transferFundedClaim(_user, amount)` [2](#0-1) , and `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` pays `instantWithdrawsRequests[_user]` in full [3](#0-2) . If the pool currency is a fee-on-transfer token (e.g. USDT with fees enabled, STA) or a less-than-amount token (cUSDCv3-style), the strategy balance is strictly less than the sum of minted tranche claims. This mirrors the reported issue: the claim/withdraw side assumes the nominal amount was received.

There is no `balanceBefore`/`balanceAfter` check, no skim guard that would restore solvency for depositor-caused shortfalls, and nothing in `initialize` restricting `token` to non-FoT assets [4](#0-3) .

### Impact Explanation
Every deposit with a fee-taking token mints shares for value never received, silently diluting all tranche holders and APR0/receipt accounting. When the epoch ends and `stopEpoch` funds `pendingWithdraws`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` from actual vault balance, the aggregate obligations exceed available underlying; the last users calling `claimWithdrawRequest`/`claimInstantWithdrawRequest` either revert or are underpaid, causing permanent freezing/loss of their unclaimed principal and interest. Loss is bounded by cumulative token fees taken on all deposits, e.g. a 1% FoT token makes ~1% of TVL unclaimable.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium-low: it requires the deployed pool currency to be a fee-on-transfer or transfer-less-than-amount token. The factory accepts an arbitrary `_underlyingToken` at `initialize` and the honest owner may legitimately choose such a token (USDT fee mode, rebasing/less-than-amount stablecoins). Once deployed, any unprivileged KYC'd lender depositing triggers the dilution with a single `deposit` call — no attacker action beyond a normal deposit is needed. No existing guard (skim, epoch gating, KYC, `_onlyIdleCDO`) detects the shortfall.

### Recommendation
Measure the balance delta rather than trusting `_amount`: in the deposit pull path (`IdleCDO._deposit` / strategy funding), snapshot `underlyingToken.balanceOf(strategy)` before and after `safeTransferFrom` and mint/account for the received amount, or explicitly document/reject fee-on-transfer underlyings in `initialize`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork outline:
1. Deploy `IdleCDOEpochVariant` + `IdleCreditVault` with a mock FoT token that keeps 1% of each `transferFrom` (or fork USDT-like behavior).
2. Lender A approves and calls `depositAA(100e6)`; vault receives 99e6 but 100e6 tranche tokens are minted 1:1 via `price() == oneToken`.
3. Lender B deposits similarly; both request withdraws; borrower/keeper runs `stopEpoch` funding `pendingWithdraws` from actual balance.
4. `claimWithdrawRequest` for A succeeds; B's claim reverts on `safeTransfer` (insufficient balance) — B's receipt is permanently unclaimable, with `vault.balance ≈ totalClaims - fees`.

Caveat: I could not fully verify the exact `IdleCDO._deposit` lines in this iteration; the claim relies on the single underlying `transferFrom` in `IdleCDO.sol` plus the confirmed 1:1 `price()` and full-amount claim payouts shown above.
