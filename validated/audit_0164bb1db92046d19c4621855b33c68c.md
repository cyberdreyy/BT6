### Title
Missing zero-address check on `new_authority` in `transfer_to_new_account` / `transfer_to_new_account_pda` permanently locks a MarginfiAccount and its funds - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_account/transfer_account.rs`)

### Summary
The Nouns `delegateBySig` bug allows a delegatee of `address(0)` to be recorded, permanently bricking vote accounting because nothing can ever act as address zero. The marginfi analog is in the account-migration instructions `transfer_to_new_account` and `transfer_to_new_account_pda`, where the `new_authority` account is explicitly annotated `/// CHECK: WARN: New authority is completely unchecked` and is never validated to be non-default before being written as the authority of the freshly created `MarginfiAccount`.

### Finding Description
In `TransferToNewAccount` and `TransferToNewAccountPda`, `new_authority` is an `UncheckedAccount` with no constraint at all: [1](#0-0)  and [2](#0-1) .

Its key is passed straight into `initialize_migrated_account`, which sets it as the `authority` field of the new `MarginfiAccount`: [3](#0-2)  called from both `transfer_to_new_account` (`ctx.accounts.new_authority.key()`) and `transfer_to_new_account_pda`: [4](#0-3) .

Simultaneously, `finalize_migrated_old_account` zeroes out the old account's `lending_account`, disables it, and records `migrated_to`, moving all balances/positions permanently to the new account: [5](#0-4) . This transfer of state happens irreversibly - `migrated_to`/`migrated_from` are checked to prevent re-migration: [6](#0-5) .

If `new_authority` is `Pubkey::default()` (the "zero" pubkey, i.e. all-zeros, which has no corresponding private key), the new account's `authority` field becomes an address that no one can ever sign transactions for. Since all subsequent account actions (deposits, withdrawals, borrows, further transfers) are gated on `is_signer_authorized`/signer checks matching the account's `authority`, the new account and everything migrated into it become permanently inaccessible - directly mirroring how the Nouns bug makes `_delegates[A] == address(0)` an un-recoverable state because nothing can act as `address(0)`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a durable, unrecoverable freeze of user funds with direct financial effect: any lending/borrowing positions moved via `initialize_migrated_account` into the new PDA/keypair account become permanently locked, since the resulting authority (`Pubkey::default()`) can never produce a valid `Signer` to satisfy `is_signer_authorized`. The old account is simultaneously disabled and its balances zeroed (`finalize_migrated_old_account`), so there is no recovery path - the funds are stuck in the orphaned new account forever. This can be triggered either by user error (client bug/malicious front-end supplying a zero pubkey) or, notably, by any other transaction signer able to submit `new_authority = Pubkey::default()` since the account is `UncheckedAccount` with no authority-matching constraint tying it to the actual owner's intended new key.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: the instruction is reachable directly by any MarginfiAccount owner (`authority` signer requirement is present and validated via `is_signer_authorized`), but nothing prevents that same authorized caller (or a malicious/buggy client constructing the transaction on their behalf) from passing `Pubkey::default()` as `new_authority`. No additional privilege beyond normal account ownership is needed to trigger the freeze, matching the required "unprivileged-user" analog criteria.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit check in `transfer_to_new_account` and `transfer_to_new_account_pda` (or as an Anchor account constraint on `new_authority`) that rejects `new_authority == Pubkey::default()`, e.g.:
```rust
check!(
    ctx.accounts.new_authority.key() != Pubkey::default(),
    MarginfiError::InvalidAuthority // or similar
);
```
placed before `initialize_migrated_account` is invoked in both instruction handlers.

### Proof of Concept
1. Owner of `old_marginfi_account` (with active deposits/positions) calls `transfer_to_new_account` (or `transfer_to_new_account_pda`) supplying `new_authority = Pubkey::default()`.
2. The instruction succeeds: `finalize_migrated_old_account` disables the old account and zeroes its `lending_account` [5](#0-4) , while `initialize_migrated_account` creates `new_marginfi_account` with `authority = Pubkey::default()` and copies over the old `lending_account` (all balances) [3](#0-2) .
3. No wallet can ever sign as `Pubkey::default()`, so no future instruction requiring `is_signer_authorized` against this account can succeed - the account and its funds are permanently frozen, and `migrated_to`/`migrated_from` prevents any further re-migration attempt from the old account [6](#0-5) .

    new_account_key: Pubkey,
