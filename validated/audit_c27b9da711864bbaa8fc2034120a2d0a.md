Based on my investigation, I found a strong analog in the `agave` reward-distribution accounting, structurally similar to the "mint without matching burn" bug class from the Tapioca report: a place where lamports are credited into accounts during Alpenglow block-reward distribution without ever being funded/backed at creation time, breaking the lamport-supply/capitalization invariant.

### Title
Block-reward lamports credited to stake accounts during Alpenglow epoch-reward distribution are never funded into the `EpochRewards` sysvar, breaking the mint/burn conservation invariant - (File: `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/calculation.rs`, `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/distribution.rs`, `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/sysvar.rs`)

### Summary
Agave's SIMD-0123/SIMD-0232 ("block revenue sharing") reward pipeline follows the same two-step "mint-then-burn/settle" design as the Tapioca BigBang market: lamports that are logically "already backed" are pre-funded into the `EpochRewards` sysvar account at epoch-reward-calculation time (`create_epoch_rewards_sysvar`), and later, when they are paid out to stake accounts, they are debited back out of that sysvar account without touching `capitalization` (because the funds already existed). However, `begin_partitioned_rewards` — the single production call site of `create_epoch_rewards_sysvar` — hardcodes the `block_rewards` funding parameter to `0`, instead of the actual sum of per-stake block rewards computed moments earlier by `calculate_block_reward`/`calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions`.

### Finding Description
`calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions` computes a non-zero `block_reward` for every stake delegation when `block_revenue_sharing` is active, sourced from each vote account's `pending_delegator_rewards`: [1](#0-0) 

These per-account `block_reward` values are stored in `PartitionedStakeReward` and later, at distribution time, unconditionally credited to the stake account's lamport balance via `checked_add_lamports`: [2](#0-1) 

The design intends for these credited lamports to be "already existing" — pre-funded into the `EpochRewards` sysvar account when the epoch's reward cycle begins, then later debited back out of that same sysvar account (without touching `capitalization`, since the funds are simply moving from the sysvar to the stake account): [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

However, the only production call to `create_epoch_rewards_sysvar` — in `begin_partitioned_rewards`, which kicks off the reward cycle every epoch — hardcodes the `block_rewards` funding argument to `0` instead of passing the actual total of per-stake `block_reward` amounts computed by `calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions`: [5](#0-4) 

Correspondingly, at distribution time `distribute_epoch_rewards_in_partition` only increases `capitalization` for `stake_reward_lamports_minted` (the inflation portion) and never for `block_reward_lamports_distributed`, matching the assumption that block-reward lamports were already accounted for at sysvar-funding time: [6](#0-5) 

Because the sysvar was funded with `0` instead of the real total, this is the exact analog of the Tapioca finding: value ("USDO"/lamports) is minted into user-facing balances (stake accounts) during the "liquidation"/distribution step, but the corresponding "burn"/debit source (the sysvar's pre-funded balance) was never actually backed at creation time. The result is either (a) a lamport supply increase with no corresponding `capitalization` increase — i.e., unbacked/free lamports created out of thin air in stake accounts, exactly mirroring the "USDO minted without collateral" defect — or (b) a deterministic panic when `update_epoch_rewards_sysvar`'s `checked_sub_lamports(debit_block_reward_lamports)` fails because the sysvar account lacks the lamports it was never funded with: [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation
If block-reward lamports are actually credited to stake accounts without the matching sysvar pre-funding, every validator that processes the epoch-reward-distribution slot would either: (1) silently increase the real lamport supply held in stake accounts without incrementing `capitalization`, permanently desynchronizing the bank's tracked total-supply invariant from the true sum of account balances (the direct analog of USDO's peg being broken by unbacked minting), or (2) hit the `.expect()` panic in `update_epoch_rewards_sysvar`, causing a deterministic, cluster-wide validator crash/halt at every epoch boundary once `block_revenue_sharing`/Alpenglow-based block rewards are non-zero. Both outcomes are severe: the first breaks a fundamental Solana invariant (lamport conservation / total supply accounting) that many downstream systems and consensus assumptions depend on; the second is a network-halting bug.

### Likelihood Explanation
This code path is exercised automatically, without needing a malicious transaction, every epoch once the `block_revenue_sharing` feature (SIMD-0123/0232, Alpenglow reward path) is active and validators have non-zero `pending_delegator_rewards`. I could not fully confirm within the available context whether `block_revenue_sharing`/Alpenglow block rewards are currently feature-gated to always be `0` in the deployed configuration, nor whether some other code path funds the sysvar account with block rewards that I did not locate (e.g., during `update_epoch_stakes` "VAT burns" referenced in a nearby comment). This uncertainty is significant: it's possible the `0` argument is intentionally correct today because block rewards are computed and funded through a different mechanism I did not find, and the actual per-partition `block_reward` values are always `0` under currently-active feature flags. I recommend a Devin session with full repository access to trace `block_revenue_sharing`'s activation status, confirm whether `pending_delegator_rewards` can be non-zero at `begin_partitioned_rewards` time, and check whether the `0` literal is a leftover/placeholder versus deliberate.

### Recommendation
Compute the true total of per-partition `block_reward` amounts during `calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions` (mirroring `total_stake_rewards_lamports`), thread it through `PartitionedRewardsCalculation`, and pass that value (not the hardcoded `0`) into `create_epoch_rewards_sysvar`'s `block_rewards` parameter in `begin_partitioned_rewards`, so the sysvar account is always funded with exactly the lamports it will later be debited for during distribution — preserving the lamport-conservation invariant instead of allowing free minting or a forced panic.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual reproduction (pending confirmation of feature-gating, as noted above):
1. Activate the `block_revenue_sharing` feature and drive the bank into the Alpenglow reward path such that vote accounts accumulate non-zero `pending_delegator_rewards`.
2. Advance to an epoch boundary; `begin_partitioned_rewards` calls `create_epoch_rewards_sysvar(..., 0)`, funding the `EpochRewards` sysvar with `0` block-reward lamports: [5](#0-4) 
3. During distribution, `build_updated_stake_reward` credits stake accounts with non-zero `block_reward` values computed from `pending_delegator_rewards`: [2](#0-1) 
4. `distribute_epoch_rewards_in_partition` never adds `block_reward_lamports_distributed` to `capitalization`: [8](#0-7) 
5. `update_epoch_rewards_sysvar` attempts to debit the (unfunded) sysvar account by `block_reward_lamports_distributed`, which either panics (`.expect()` failure due to insufficient lamports) or, if this path is bypassed/reached differently, leaves stake accounts holding lamports with no matching increase in tracked capitalization — an unbacked mint, directly analogous to unburned USDO inflating supply beyond its backing collateral.
