Based on my review of the CPI privilege-propagation logic, PDA signer derivation, and nonce/stake authority checks in this codebase, I did not find a reachable analog to the Storm CVE-2017-9799 confused-deputy pattern (where an untrusted actor tricks a privileged component into acting under a different identity).

Key findings that rule out an analog:
- CPI privilege escalation checks in `prepare_next_cpi_instruction` explicitly reject any callee account that gains signer or writable privileges beyond what the caller granted, and duplicate-account handling correctly propagates the strictest privileges rather than allowing bypass. [1](#0-0) 
- `native_invoke_signed` derives PDA signers strictly against the calling builtin program's own `program_id`, making it structurally impossible to vouch for a non-derived address (e.g., impersonating a different user/account) as a signer.
