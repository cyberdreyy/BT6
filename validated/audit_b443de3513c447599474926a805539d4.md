#No vulnerability found for this question.

**Reasoning:**

The claimed binding — "NEAR debited from `staking_information.deposit_amount` == NEAR actually delivered to `env::account_balance()`" — is not actually enforced anywhere as a solvency guarantee, and its violation does not produce the claimed impact, for two independent reasons:

1. **`transfer()` is capped by the real account balance, not by `deposit_amount`.** `get_owners_balance()` computes `account_balance() + get_known_deposited_balance() - get_locked_amount()`, but the actual owner-callable `transfer()` uses `get_liquid_owners_balance()`, defined as `min(get_owners_balance(), get_account_balance())`. [1](#0-0)  Since `transfer()` asserts against this minimum, an inflated or stale `deposit_amount` can never let the owner withdraw more real NEAR than `env::account_balance()` actually holds. [2](#0-1)  So even if the fake pool never delivers funds, insolvency via `transfer()` is structurally prevented.

2. **The scenario requires an owner action, which is out of scope.** The exploit requires "the lockup owner to select a pool contract they control" via `select_staking_pool`, which is an owner-only method gated by `assert_owner()`. [3](#0-2)  The rules explicitly exclude "anything requiring... a contract owner" action, and the attacker profile here is not the lockup owner and cannot invoke `withdraw_from_staking_pool`/`select_staking_pool` themselves — the owner must voluntarily trust and select the malicious pool. This is self-inflicted risk by the account's own owner, not an unprivileged attacker draining a victim's funds without consent.

Additionally, tracing the actual effect of `on_staking_pool_withdraw` under the stated precondition (`deposit_amount.0 < amount.0`) shows `saturating_sub` zeroes/reduces `deposit_amount` without a corresponding real balance increase, which *decreases* `get_owners_balance()` relative to reality (understating, not overstating, the owner's balance) — the opposite of the claimed "overstate" effect needed to drain real funds. [4](#0-3)

        (env::account_balance() + self.get_known_deposited_balance().0).into()

        self.assert_no_staking_or_idle();
        self.assert_no_termination();
        assert!(
            self.get_liquid_owners_balance().0 >= amount.0,

            self.get_liquid_owners_balance().0,
            amount.0,
        );

        env::log(format!("Transferring {} to account @{}", amount.0, receiver_id).as_bytes());

        Promise::new(receiver_id).transfer(amount.0)
    }
