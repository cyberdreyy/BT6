### Title
Depositing and staking right before an epoch boundary lets a delegator capture a pro-rata share of validation rewards it did not help earn - ([File: staking-pool/src/internal.rs])

### Summary
`StakingContract::internal_ping` distributes the entire epoch's accumulated reward across the *current* `total_stake_shares` at the moment `ping` runs, rather than the shares that were actually staked with the validator while that reward was being earned. A delegator can call `deposit_and_stake` (or `stake`) in the last block of an epoch, be issued shares immediately at the pre-reward price, and then receive a cut of the very next reward credited by `internal_ping`, even though NEAR's protocol-level stake activation is delayed by roughly two epochs, meaning the newly staked funds had no role in earning that reward.

### Finding Description
`internal_ping` computes the total reward received since the last ping as the delta between the current on-chain balance and `last_total_balance`, then distributes it by inflating `total_staked_balance` while leaving `total_stake_shares` untouched — effectively raising the share price for whoever currently holds shares: [1](#0-0) 

Crucially, this distribution happens against `self.total_stake_shares` and the balances of accounts as they stand *at ping time*, not as they stood throughout the epoch that generated the reward. `deposit_and_stake`/`stake` call `internal_ping()` first (settling any pending reward at the old share count), and only afterward call `internal_stake`, which immediately mints new shares to the caller at the current price and bumps `total_stake_shares`/`total_staked_balance`: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

Because NEAR's protocol determines validator stake/seat allocation with a multi-epoch lead time, a `stake` call submitted at the tail end of epoch `N` does not actually contribute to the validator's locked balance until epoch `N+2` (or later). Yet the shares are credited synchronously in epoch `N`. When the reward for epoch `N+1` (or even `N` itself, via gas-rebate/leftover balance increases that are folded into the same `total_reward` calculation) is settled by the next `internal_ping` call, it is split across whatever `total_stake_shares` exist at that time — which now includes the attacker's freshly minted shares alongside genuine long-term delegators' shares.

This is the same class of bug as the referenced am-AMM report: a party can acquire a proportional claim on a reward pool right before the reward for a past accrual period is distributed, without having contributed capital for the period that generated it. The equality that should hold — `shares entitled to reward == shares that were actually staked with the validator while the reward accrued` — is broken because share issuance is instantaneous while stake activation (and thus real reward-earning) is delayed.

### Impact Explanation
The attacker's undeserved reward is paid out of the same `total_staked_balance` pool that legitimate, long-standing delegators are also claiming from via their shares, so every reward distributed to the attacker's newly-minted, non-earning shares is value diverted away from existing delegators. This is rewards mis-attributed to a party not entitled to them, falling under the High-impact category described in the rules ("rewards or fees mis-attributed... where another party settles on it").

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role is required — any delegator can call `deposit_and_stake`/`stake` at will, and epoch boundaries are observable/predictable from chain state, allowing an attacker to time the call to just before an epoch switch. The only cost to the attacker is temporarily locking capital (mitigated further since they can `unstake_all` immediately after collecting the inflated share value, subject only to the standard 4-epoch unstaking delay before withdrawal), which matches the "funds blocked for a short period but reward still received" pattern in the reference report.

### Recommendation
Do not let shares minted in an epoch participate in rewards settled for that same epoch (or for periods before the underlying stake was actually active with the validator). For example, track a "pending"/"activating" share balance for shares/stake added since the last ping, and only merge them into the reward-eligible `total_stake_shares` after the epoch(s) corresponding to the protocol's stake-activation delay have elapsed, so `internal_ping` only distributes reward pro-rata over shares that were genuinely staked while the reward accrued.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors `env::epoch_height()`/`last_epoch_height` and waits until just before an epoch boundary.
2. Attacker calls `deposit_and_stake(amount)`. `internal_ping()` settles any pending reward first (at the pre-attack share count), then `internal_stake` mints new shares to the attacker at the current price, increasing `total_stake_shares` and `total_staked_balance` immediately: [2](#0-1) .
3. The epoch turns over. Because of NEAR's stake-activation delay, the attacker's newly staked funds are not yet part of the validator's actual locked stake, so they did not contribute to the reward the validator earns for the new epoch — but the contract's on-chain balance still increases by that reward.
4. Any subsequent action (or an explicit `ping`) triggers `internal_ping`, which computes `total_reward` and distributes it by inflating `total_staked_balance` relative to `total_stake_shares` as they currently stand — now including the attacker's shares: [4](#0-3) .
5. Attacker calls `unstake_all` to lock in the inflated share value, then `withdraw_all` after the unstaking delay, having captured a share of a reward period during which their capital was not actually earning it — at the expense of the pool's genuine delegators.

    pub(crate) fn internal_stake(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Staking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        // Calculate the number of "stake" shares that the account will receive for staking the
        // given amount.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "The calculated number of \"stake\" shares received for staking should be positive"
        );
        // The amount of tokens the account will be charged from the unstaked balance.
        // Rounded down to avoid overcharging the account to guarantee that the account can always
        // unstake at least the same amount as staked.
        let charge_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);
        assert!(
            charge_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= charge_amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to stake"
        );
        account.unstaked -= charge_amount;
        account.stake_shares += num_shares;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The staked amount that will be added to the total to guarantee the "stake" share price
        // never decreases. The difference between `stake_amount` and `charge_amount` is paid
        // from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let stake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance += stake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;

        env::log(
            format!(
                "@{} staking {}. Received {} new staking shares. Total {} unstaked balance and {} staking shares",
                account_id, charge_amount, num_shares, account.unstaked, account.stake_shares
            )
                .as_bytes(),
        );
        env::log(
            format!(
                "Contract total staked balance is {}. Total number of shares {}",
                self.total_staked_balance, self.total_stake_shares
            )
            .as_bytes(),
        );
    }

    /// Distributes rewards after the new epoch. It's automatically called before every action.
    /// Returns true if the current epoch height is different from the last epoch height.
    pub(crate) fn internal_ping(&mut self) -> bool {
        let epoch_height = env::epoch_height();
        if self.last_epoch_height == epoch_height {
            return false;
        }
        self.last_epoch_height = epoch_height;

        // New total amount (both locked and unlocked balances).
        // NOTE: We need to subtract `attached_deposit` in case `ping` called from `deposit` call
        // since the attached deposit gets included in the `account_balance`, and we have not
        // accounted it yet.
        let total_balance =
            env::account_locked_balance() + env::account_balance() - env::attached_deposit();

        assert!(
            total_balance >= self.last_total_balance,
            "The new total balance should not be less than the old total balance"
        );
        let total_reward = total_balance - self.last_total_balance;
        if total_reward > 0 {
            // The validation fee that the contract owner takes.
            let owners_fee = self.reward_fee_fraction.multiply(total_reward);

            // Distributing the remaining reward to the delegators first.
            let remaining_reward = total_reward - owners_fee;
            self.total_staked_balance += remaining_reward;

            // Now buying "stake" shares for the contract owner at the new share price.
            let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(owners_fee);
            if num_shares > 0 {
                // Updating owner's inner account
                let owner_id = self.owner_id.clone();
                let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&owner_id);
                account.stake_shares += num_shares;
                self.internal_save_account(&owner_id, &account);
                // Increasing the total amount of "stake" shares.
                self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;
            }
            // Increasing the total staked balance by the owners fee, no matter whether the owner
            // received any shares or not.
            self.total_staked_balance += owners_fee;
