### Title
IdleCreditVault mints strategy tokens 1:1 with the nominal deposit amount, ignoring fee-on-transfer shortfalls — (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The external report describes a protocol that credits the user-supplied `_amount` instead of the actually received token amount, so fee-on-transfer tokens silently break accounting. In `idle-tranches`, the `IdleCDO`/`IdleCDOCreditVault` deposit path already measures the real received amount (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal` before minting, `IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:203-206`), but the strategy layer does not: `IdleCreditVault.deposit` pulls `_amount` underlying from the CDO and then mints exactly `_amount` strategy tokens to the CDO (`IdleCreditVault.sol:603-604`). The same nominal-amount assumption exists in `collectWithdrawFunds` / `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, which `safeTransferFrom` the CDO and credit the full `_amount` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:402,428`), and in `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, which reserves the full `_recoveredAmount` regardless of what arrived (`IdleCreditVault.sol:686-708`).

### Finding Description
`deposit` is called by the CDO after the CDO has already pulled underlyings from the lender. With a fee-on-transfer underlying configured at `initialize` (`token = _underlyingToken`, `IdleCreditVault.sol:137`), two haircuts apply but only one is measured:

1. Lender → CDO: CDO measures the delta correctly, so it mints shares for `_amount - fee1`.
2. CDO → Strategy (`deposit`): the strategy receives `_amount - fee1 - fee2` (or just `_amount - fee` in `IdleCDOCreditVault` mode where the CDO pulls then pushes), but executes `_mint(msg.sender, _amount)`.

The strategy token supply — which is the borrower's debt basis and every receipt's claim basis (`requestWithdraw` mints receipts against this supply at `IdleCreditVault.sol:273-275`) — therefore exceeds actual strategy backing by the fee each hop. Analogous drift occurs whenever `collectWithdrawFunds`/`collectInstantWithdrawFunds` are funded with a fee-bearing token: `pendingWithdraws`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` are decremented by the nominal `_amount` while only `_amount - fee` reaches the reserve (`IdleCreditVault.sol:401-402, 420-428`).

### Impact Explanation
Strategy-token solvency invariant is broken: `totalSupply` of vault shares is backed by less underlying than accounted. Since `price()` is hard-coded to `oneToken` (`IdleCreditVault.sol:174-176`), every claim pays 1:1 until the balance runs dry. The shortfall (one transfer fee per deposit and per epoch funding, plus one per recovery pull in `finalizeDefaultRecovery`) is socialized onto the last withdraw-request claimants and defaulted-epoch recovery claimants, whose `_transferFundedClaim`/`_transferDefaultRecovery` transfers revert or underpay. Quantified loss per deposit ≈ `fee%` of the deposit (e.g., 1% on a 1M USDT-class deposit → ~10k units permanently missing from the redemption pool).

### Likelihood Explanation
The underlying token is a free parameter of `initialize`, chosen per-vault; nothing validates that it transfers without fees. If a vault is ever deployed on a fee-on-transfer or elastic-balance currency, every deposit and every `stopEpoch` funding in a running/buffer epoch silently leaks the fee. Triggerable by an unprivileged KYC'd lender simply calling `depositAA`/`depositBB`; no privileged misbehavior required. Likelihood is gated solely by the deployment-time token choice, which matches the Medium severity of the source report.

### Recommendation
In `IdleCreditVault.deposit`, `collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, and `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, measure `underlyingToken.balanceOf(address(this))` before and after `safeTransferFrom` and mint/credit/reserve only the delta. Alternatively, enforce at `initialize` that the underlying performs a fee-less transfer (test-transfer of a fixed amount and require received == sent), and document that only non-fee-on-transfer tokens are supported.
