### Title
Newly-deposited stake immediately shares in the full epoch's validator rewards, letting a staker join right before `ping()` and dilute long-term stakers - (File: staking-pool/src/internal.rs)

### Summary
The staking pool's reward-distribution routine `internal_ping` (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs`) attributes the entire reward accrued since the last epoch boundary to whichever accounts hold "stake" shares at the moment `ping` executes, with no minimum holding period or time-weighting. Any account can call `deposit_and_stake` to acquire shares immediately before the next state-changing call triggers `internal_ping`, capture a pro-rata slice of rewards that were earned by capital staked for the whole epoch by other delegators, and then call `unstake` to lock in that gain instantly. This is the direct on-chain analog of the reported GMXKey issue: an unprivileged actor buys into the reward-bearing position just before a reward-compounding event and exits immediately after, extracting value that belonged to long-term participants.

### Finding Description
`internal_ping` is invoked at the start of every state-changing method (`deposit`, `deposit_and_stake`, `stake`, `stake_all`, `unstake`, `unstake_all`, `ping`) and is fully permissionless — anyone can trigger it, and it only guards against double-processing within the same epoch: [1](#0-0) 

The reward is computed purely from the delta between the current total balance and `last_total_balance`, with no accounting for how long any given share has existed: [2](#0-1) 

Because `internal_ping` only fires once per epoch transition (not on every call), any reward that accumulated over the *entire* prior epoch is realized on the *first* call made after the epoch boundary. An attacker can `deposit_and_stake` at any point during epoch E — including in the final block before the epoch height increments — and receive shares at the pre-reward price via `internal_stake` (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs`, `num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down`): [3](#0-2) 

When the epoch subsequently rolls over and *any* account (even the attacker themselves) triggers the next `ping`, the full epoch's reward is split pro-rata across `total_stake_shares` as it stands at that instant — which now includes the attacker's freshly minted shares — inflating the stake-share price for everyone, including the attacker. There is no warm-up period, staking fee, or time-weighted accrual gating this.

The attacker can then immediately call `unstake`, which redeems shares at the now-inflated price with no minimum holding period check: [4](#0-3)  The only friction is the standard 4-epoch withdrawal delay (`NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK`) before the unstaked balance can be transferred out via `withdraw`, but the profit (the increased "stake" share price captured into the unstaked balance) is locked in at the moment of `unstake`, not at withdrawal.

This breaks the implicit binding that reward attribution should correspond to capital actually at risk/staked during the epoch that generated the reward: instead, `total_reward` is attributed strictly to `total_stake_shares` at ping-time, letting late joiners free-ride on rewards earned entirely by other, longer-term delegators — mis-attributing rewards exactly as described in the GMXKey report.

### Impact Explanation
This is a High-impact issue: rewards are mis-attributed away from the delegators who actually earned them (staked capital for the full epoch) toward an opportunistic staker who joins immediately before the distribution event. Every such sandwich dilutes the stake-share value of existing delegators proportionally to the attacker's injected capital, i.e., an accounting value (share price / reward entitlement) diverges from the reality of who contributed the staked capital that earned the reward.

### Likelihood Explanation
`ping`, `deposit_and_stake`, and `unstake` are all unprivileged, permissionless public methods with no cooldown or fee. An attacker only needs to observe the current epoch height (a public, queryable value) and time their `deposit_and_stake` call to just before the epoch turns over (or simply before whoever next calls a state-changing method), which is straightforward to script and repeat every epoch. No special privileges, redeploys, or trusted roles are required.

### Recommendation
Introduce a mechanism, as later adopted upstream for the referenced GMXKey issue, that prevents newly-staked shares from immediately participating in rewards accrued before they were staked — e.g., a warm-up period before new shares become reward-eligible, or accrual of rewards proportional to time-weighted stake within the epoch rather than a flat "shares at ping-time" snapshot, or a staking/unstaking fee that removes the profitability of single-epoch sandwiching.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes `get_total_staked_balance`, `get_number_of_accounts`, and the current `epoch_height` on the staking pool contract (`my_validator`), noting a large pending reward is about to be realized on the next epoch boundary (the pool's `last_total_balance` is stale relative to `env::account_locked_balance() + env::account_balance()`).
2. Just before (or right after) the epoch height increments, attacker calls `deposit_and_stake({ amount })`. Because `internal_ping` runs first and the epoch hasn't changed relative to `last_epoch_height` yet (or the reward hasn't been realized yet), attacker's shares are minted at the pre-reward price: `staking-pool/src/lib.rs` `deposit_and_stake` → `internal_stake` (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs:70-106`).
3. Attacker (or anyone) calls `ping()` after the epoch height changes. `internal_ping` (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs:192-250`) computes `total_reward` since `last_total_balance` and distributes it across `total_stake_shares`, now including attacker's shares, inflating the share price for the whole pool.
4. Attacker immediately calls `unstake_all` (or `unstake`), converting shares to unstaked balance at the new inflated price (`inner_unstake`, `staking-pool/src/internal.rs:124-165`), locking in a reward the attacker did not economically earn.
5. After the standard 4-epoch delay, attacker calls `withdraw_all` to extract the NEAR, having captured rewards diluted from long-term delegators for the cost of only a few epochs of exposure and no fee.

    pub(crate) fn internal_stake(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Staking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        // Calculate the number of "stake" shares that the account will receive for staking the
        // given amount.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "The calculated number of \"stake\" shares received for staking should be positive"
        );
        // The amount of tokens the account will be charged from the unstaked balance.
        // Rounded down to avoid overcharging the account to guarantee that the account can always
        // unstake at least the same amount as staked.
        let charge_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);
        assert!(
            charge_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= charge_amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to stake"
        );
        account.unstaked -= charge_amount;
        account.stake_shares += num_shares;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The staked amount that will be added to the total to guarantee the "stake" share price
        // never decreases. The difference between `stake_amount` and `charge_amount` is paid
        // from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let stake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance += stake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;

            account.stake_shares >= num_shares,

        );

    /// Distributes rewards after the new epoch. It's automatically called before every action.
    /// Returns true if the current epoch height is different from the last epoch height.
    pub(crate) fn internal_ping(&mut self) -> bool {
        let epoch_height = env::epoch_height();
        if self.last_epoch_height == epoch_height {
            return false;
        }
        self.last_epoch_height = epoch_height;

        // New total amount (both locked and unlocked balances).
        // NOTE: We need to subtract `attached_deposit` in case `ping` called from `deposit` call
        // since the attached deposit gets included in the `account_balance`, and we have not
        // accounted it yet.
        let total_balance =
            env::account_locked_balance() + env::account_balance() - env::attached_deposit();

        assert!(
            total_balance >= self.last_total_balance,
            "The new total balance should not be less than the old total balance"
        );
        let total_reward = total_balance - self.last_total_balance;
        if total_reward > 0 {
            // The validation fee that the contract owner takes.
            let owners_fee = self.reward_fee_fraction.multiply(total_reward);

            // Distributing the remaining reward to the delegators first.
            let remaining_reward = total_reward - owners_fee;
            self.total_staked_balance += remaining_reward;

            // Now buying "stake" shares for the contract owner at the new share price.
            let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(owners_fee);
            if num_shares > 0 {
                // Updating owner's inner account
                let owner_id = self.owner_id.clone();
                let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&owner_id);
                account.stake_shares += num_shares;
                self.internal_save_account(&owner_id, &account);
                // Increasing the total amount of "stake" shares.
                self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;
            }
            // Increasing the total staked balance by the owners fee, no matter whether the owner
            // received any shares or not.
            self.total_staked_balance += owners_fee;
