### Title
Fee-on-transfer underlying is credited at nominal amount in `IdleCreditVault`, minting unbacked strategy tokens and overstating funded/recovery reserves - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol](contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol))

### Summary
`IdleCreditVault` is the credit-vault strategy behind `IdleCDOEpochVariant`. `IdleCDO._deposit` already defends against fee-on-transfer tokens by minting tranche shares on the *balance delta* (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal`). [1](#0-0)  The strategy layer undoes that protection: it pulls `_amount` via `safeTransferFrom` but mints/credits the full nominal `_amount`, exactly the same bug class as Gravity's `sendToCosmos` emitting the nominal amount instead of the received amount. Three sites are affected:

- `deposit`: `underlyingToken.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount); _mint(msg.sender, _amount)` — strategy tokens minted 1:1 with the nominal amount, not the received amount. [2](#0-1) 
- `collectWithdrawFunds` / `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`: reduces `pendingWithdraws`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` and computes `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch` from the nominal `_amount` rather than the received delta. [3](#0-2) 
- `finalizeDefaultRecovery`: computes `reserveAmount` and `recoveryPrice` from the nominal `_recoveredAmount` before pulling the tokens. [4](#0-3) 

### Finding Description
When the vault underlying is a transfer-on-fee/deflationary token (the codebase's own `IdleCDO._deposit` explicitly accounts for this case via balance-delta accounting, so such tokens are within the supported design envelope), every pull of tokens into `IdleCreditVault` credits the gross amount while only the net amount arrives:

1. `deposit(_amount)` — called by the CDO on `directDeposit` flows (buffer-phase deposits routed to the strategy) — pulls `_amount` from the CDO and mints `_amount` strategy tokens to the CDO. If the CDO holds any other underlying (unclaimed fees, `sendInterestAndDeposits` returns, donations held before `_skimDonatedAssets` runs), the `transferFrom` of the nominal `_amount` succeeds even though the user only delivered `amount - fee`. The strategy-token supply is then backed by less underlying than recorded; the depositor's tranche shares, minted on the delta, are fine, but the strategy minted `_amount` tokens against `delta`-worth of fresh backing plus `fee`-worth of other users'/fee funds. Every such deposit silently transfers value from existing LPs/fee recipients to the new strategy-token basis.
2. `collectWithdrawFunds(_amount)` at `stopEpoch`/`stopEpochWithDuration`: `pendingWithdraws` is cleared and `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch[epochNumber]` is set assuming `_amount` arrived. Receipt holders later claim `(claimBasis * lossRecoveryPrice) / RECOVERY_FULL` against an actual balance that is `fee` lower than assumed, so the last claimants' `_transferFundedClaim`/`_transferDefaultRecovery` reverts (permanent freezing absent a top-up) and earlier claimants are overpaid at others' expense.
3. `finalizeDefaultRecovery(_recoveredAmount, _recoverySource)`: `defaultRecoveryPrice = reserveAmount * RECOVERY_FULL / totalBasis` uses nominal `_recoveredAmount`; the reserve is under-funded by the fee, so the recovery ratio is overstated — earlier default claimants drain the reserve and later claimants can never claim (insolvency), and the mint/burn of CDO strategy tokens (`activeFinalNAV`) is computed on the inflated price, mispricing tranche NAV.

Broken invariant: solvency — recorded claims (strategy-token supply, `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch`, `defaultRecoveryPrice`) exceed actual underlying backing by the cumulative transfer fees.

### Impact Explanation
- With `directDeposit` active, an unprivileged lender depositing a deflationary underlying mints strategy-token basis backed partly by funds belonging to other LPs / pending withdraw claimants / `unclaimedFees`. Redeeming those claims steals the difference; repeated deposits compound the theft.
- On any `stopEpochWithDuration` loss or default recovery, the overstated `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch`/`defaultRecoveryPrice` overpays early claimants and leaves the reserve short: the last claimants' payouts revert permanently (reserve never topped up), i.e. permanent freezing of unclaimed yield plus direct theft by early claimants. Quantified loss = `amount * feeRate` per deposit/funding/finalization event.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is conditional but real: the vault only needs to be deployed with a fee-on-transfer underlying (or receive one via a borrower/recovery path), and the CDO's delta-based deposit accounting shows fee-on-transfer tokens were contemplated. No privileged misbehavior is required — the attacker is an ordinary KYC-passed lender depositing during the buffer phase, or sequencing a normal `stopEpoch`/default finalization; all privileged callers act honestly. Existing guards (`_skimDonatedAssets`, reserve checks in `_transferFundedClaim`, `NotAllowed` reverts) do not detect the nominal-vs-received gap; the `_transferFundedClaim` reserve guard only converts the shortfall into a revert for the last claimant.

### Recommendation
Measure the received amount via `balanceOf` before/after every `safeTransferFrom` into the strategy and use the delta everywhere:

- `deposit`: `uint256 pre = underlyingToken.balanceOf(address(this)); safeTransferFrom(...); _mint(msg.sender, balanceOf(address(this)) - pre);` and use the delta for `totEpochDeposits`.
- `collectWithdrawFunds` / `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`: compute the loss-recovery price and `pendingWithdraws` decrement from the received delta, not `_amount`.
- `finalizeDefaultRecovery`: pull `_recoveredAmount` first, then compute `reserveAmount` from the balance delta.
- Alternatively, explicitly reject fee-on-transfer underlyings at initialization (e.g., a probe transfer) and remove the delta accounting in `IdleCDO._deposit` to make the assumption consistent.
