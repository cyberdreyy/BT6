### Title
Claimed instant-withdraw receipts stay counted in `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch`, inflating the default-recovery claim basis and diluting/insolventing honest claimants - ([File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol](contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol))

### Summary
In `IdleCreditVault`, `requestInstantWithdraw` records a per-epoch aggregate receipt basis in `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` (line 372). Neither `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` (lines 380-393) nor `collectInstantWithdrawFunds` (lines 398-403) ever decrements that per-epoch map or the per-user `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch`. The only place they are cleared is the post-default path `_claimDefaultedInstantWithdrawRequest` (lines 847-853). At default finalization, `defaultPendingClaimBasis()` adds `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[epochNumber]` to the haircut basis whenever `pendingInstantWithdraws != 0` (lines 644-649), and `_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve()` credits `instantBasis - pendingInstant` as already-held reserve (lines 716-723). An unprivileged user can therefore leave a stale, already-paid claim in the counter, then keep a dust instant request unfunded so the stale basis enters `finalizeDefaultRecovery` — mirroring the Venus bug where mint/burn manipulates `pendingScoreUpdates` to dodge unfavorable accounting.

### Finding Description
Sequence (running epoch, epochNumber = E):

1. Attacker calls `requestInstantWithdraw(X)` via the CDO. This sets `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[attacker][E] = X`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[E] = X`, `pendingInstantWithdraws += X`.
2. The CDO collects funds (`collectInstantWithdrawFunds(X)` → `pendingInstantWithdraws = 0`, underlying moved to the strategy) and the attacker calls `claimInstantWithdrawRequest` receiving `X` at par via `_transferFundedClaim`. Critically, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[E]` and `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[attacker][E]` remain `X` — the claim path only zeroes `instantWithdrawsRequests[attacker]`.
3. Attacker submits a second dust instant request `Y` (1 wei is enough). `pendingInstantWithdraws = Y`, `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[E] = X + Y`.
4. The borrower defaults within epoch E. Honest `finalizeDefaultRecovery` computes:
   - `pendingBasis = pendingWithdraws + instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[E]` — inflated by the attacker's already-paid `X`.
   - `prefundedReserve = (X + Y) - Y = X` — phantom underlying credited to `defaultRecoveryReserve` although those tokens were already paid out to the attacker.
   - `recoveryPrice = (recovered + prefunded + reserve) / totalBasis` is computed over a basis that double-counts `X`, while the reserve is credited funds that do not exist.

### Impact Explanation
Two-sided damage. (a) Every honest defaulted receipt claimant and active LP has their `defaultRecoveryPrice` diluted because `totalBasis` includes `X` of already-satisfied claims — direct loss of recovery proportional to `X / totalBasis`. (b) `defaultRecoveryReserve` is inflated by the phantom `prefundedReserve = X` without receiving tokens, so once honest users claim, the last claimants' `_transferDefaultRecovery` transfers revert on insufficient balance — permanent freezing/insolvency of unclaimed recovery. The attacker keeps the full `X` paid at par in step 2. Existing guards don't stop it: `_transferFundedClaim` only protects `defaultRecoveryReserve` post-finalization, and no check ties `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch` to actually-outstanding receipts.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an unprivileged lender (any KYC-passing user) to make an instant-withdraw request that gets funded, claim it, then leave a dust instant request pending when a default occurs in the same epoch. Attacker cost is gas plus temporarily locked principal; no privileged collusion needed. Triggering conditions (liquidity available for instant funding, then borrower default before epoch end) are consistent with normal vault operation.

### Recommendation
Decrement the per-epoch receipt accounting when instant claims are paid: in `claimInstantWithdrawRequest`, clear `instantWithdrawsRequestsByEpoch[_user][<requestEpoch>]` and subtract the amount from `instantWithdrawClaimsByEpoch[<requestEpoch>]` (store the request epoch per user, analogously to `lastWithdrawRequest` for normal requests). Alternatively, recompute `defaultPendingClaimBasis`/`_defaultPrefundedInstantReserve` from outstanding receipt balances rather than a cumulative counter.
