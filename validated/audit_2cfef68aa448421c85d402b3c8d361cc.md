### Title
Same-mint emissions deposit can be sandwiched to steal a disproportionate share of distributed rewards - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
The MEV report describes attackers front-running a permissionless `distribute()`/`harvest()` call by staking immediately before the distribution and unstaking immediately after, capturing a share of rewards proportional to a momentarily inflated stake. marginfi's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction (permissionless "same-bank emissions deposit") has the same structural weakness: rewards are distributed instantly and pro-rata to whatever `total_asset_shares` exist at the moment the instruction executes, with no vesting, cooldown, or minimum holding period.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets **any caller** transfer tokens into a bank's liquidity vault and immediately mark up `asset_share_value` for all existing depositors, proportional to `total_asset_shares` at that instant: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

`total_asset_shares` and `asset_share_value` are read/written in the same instruction with no delay: the increase in value is realized synchronously and is claimable by any depositor holding shares at that moment, since `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` have no lockup, cooldown, or same-block restriction: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Unlike Kamino-wrapped deposits, which are blocked from occurring twice in the same transaction by Kamino's own "stale reserve" flag, plain marginfi bank deposit/withdraw have no such restriction, and can be freely combined in one atomic transaction: [5](#0-4) 

This reproduces the report's CASE 1/CASE 2 exploit pattern for `distribute()`: an attacker with capital (obtained via any external flash loan mechanism, or marginfi's own `lending_account_start_flashloan`/`end_flashloan` which explicitly allows borrowing without a risk check mid-transaction) can, in one atomic transaction:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank, inflating their share of `total_asset_shares`.
2. Trigger (or piggyback on someone else's) call to `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, which immediately marks up `asset_share_value` for the whole bank based on current share distribution.
3. Withdraw immediately, realizing a share of the reward proportional to shares they only held for a single transaction, at the expense of long-term depositors who should have received it.

Because the instruction is permissionless (`depositor` is any signer providing `emissions_funding_account` and paying the `amount`), the attacker doesn't even need someone else to trigger it — but the sandwich is most damaging when a third party (protocol/admin) funds a genuine reward distribution and an attacker front-runs by depositing beforehand and back-runs by withdrawing after, extracting value that should have accrued to genuine long-term depositors.

### Impact Explanation
This allows any user to redirect a disproportionate share of legitimately-funded reward distributions (`lending_pool_emissions_deposit`) away from genuine depositors toward a flash-capital attacker, diluting the intended recipients' rewards. The financial effect is a value-redirection/unfair-distribution issue against depositors of the targeted bank, scaling with the size of the reward deposit and the attacker's available capital via flash loans.

### Likelihood Explanation
The prerequisite conditions are already met on-chain: `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented and coded as permissionless, deposit/withdraw carry no lockup or same-tx restriction, and marginfi natively supports atomic same-transaction borrow/repay via its own flashloan instructions, in addition to composability with external flash-loan providers. Any protocol-run or admin-run emissions funding event is directly exposed to front-running by anyone monitoring the mempool/transaction ordering.

### Recommendation
- Snapshot `total_asset_shares` used for reward mark-up based on a time-weighted or pre-block state rather than the instantaneous value at the moment `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes.
- Introduce a minimum holding period (time-locked shares) before a deposit is eligible to receive freshly-deposited emissions, or vest emissions over time instead of an instantaneous share-value bump.
- Consider disallowing deposit and withdraw of the same bank within a single transaction, similar to Kamino's stale-reserve protection, to break the atomic sandwich pattern.
- Ensure emissions-deposit calls cannot be predictably front/back-run, e.g., by requiring private submission or batching with existing balances only.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker takes a flash loan (via marginfi's own `lending_account_start_flashloan`/`lending_account_end_flashloan`, or an external protocol) of asset X.
2. In the same transaction, attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` into Bank X with the borrowed funds, sharply increasing their share of `total_asset_shares`.
3. Within the same transaction (or immediately after, controlling ordering), attacker or a victim calls `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, which computes `bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares` using the shares that now include the attacker's inflated deposit.
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem their shares at the newly inflated `asset_share_value`, capturing a disproportionate cut of the emissions deposit.
5. Attacker repays the flash loan, retaining the extracted reward share, net of the marginal amount attributable to genuine depositors — mirroring CASE 1/CASE 2 in the referenced report.
