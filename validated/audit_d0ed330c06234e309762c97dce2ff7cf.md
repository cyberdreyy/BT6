### Title
Withdrawal succeeds against internal `unstaked` accounting while `paused` skips the real un-stake `Promise`, letting withdrawals drain other delegators' liquid NEAR - (`staking-pool/src/internal.rs`)

### Summary
`internal_withdraw` only checks the caller's own `account.unstaked` field and the unstaking-delay epoch, never the contract's actual liquid balance. `inner_unstake` credits `account.unstaked` purely as accounting and relies on the subsequent `internal_restake()` call (triggered from `unstake`/`unstake_all` in `lib.rs`) to issue the real `stake()` action that reduces the validator's locked balance and frees the matching real NEAR. When `paused == true`, `internal_restake` returns immediately [1](#0-0) , so that real un-stake never happens even though the internal ledger already shows the funds as `unstaked` and, after `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK`, withdrawable.

### Finding Description
The invariant that should hold is: `sum(account.unstaked)` for all accounts ≈ `env::account_balance()` (the contract's real liquid balance), maintained by `internal_restake()` re-syncing the validator's locked stake to `total_staked_balance` after every stake/unstake. Under pause this binding breaks:

1. `inner_unstake` moves value from `stake_shares` to `account.unstaked` and sets `unstaked_available_epoch_height`, purely as internal bookkeeping [2](#0-1) .
2. The caller of `unstake`/`unstake_all` in `lib.rs` also invokes `internal_restake()`, which is supposed to issue `Promise::new(current_account_id()).stake(self.total_staked_balance, ...)` to actually shrink the validator's locked balance so the freed NEAR becomes real liquid balance. While `paused`, this call is skipped entirely [3](#0-2) , so the real locked amount never decreases and no real NEAR is freed.
3. After the internal delay elapses, `internal_withdraw` performs no check against `env::account_balance()`; it only asserts `account.unstaked >= amount` and the epoch delay, then unconditionally fires `Promise::new(account_id).transfer(amount)` and decrements `last_total_balance` [4](#0-3) .
4. Because the transfer is fire-and-forget with no `assert_self()`/`is_promise_success()` callback to roll back state on failure, any account whose `unstaked` entry is "paper" (backed by no real liquid balance) can still race to withdraw and consume whatever real liquid NEAR the contract currently holds — NEAR that in reality belongs to other delegators' liquid deposits (e.g., funds deposited-but-not-staked, or unstaked balances that legitimately unlocked before the pause). Whoever calls `withdraw` first drains the shared liquid pool; later callers either get a silently failing `Transfer` receipt (state already debited, funds never received) or cannot withdraw at all despite the ledger showing they're entitled.

None of the existing guards catch this: `internal_ping`'s assertion only checks that total balance (`locked + unlocked`) hasn't decreased below `last_total_balance`, it says nothing about the split between locked and liquid balance, and `internal_withdraw` never consults `env::account_balance()` before transferring.

### Impact Explanation
Real NEAR leaves the staking-pool contract and is paid to whichever delegator withdraws first, while other delegators' internally-tracked `unstaked` balances are silently zeroed by later failed `Transfer` receipts without ever receiving funds — a direct loss of delegator NEAR not authorized by its rightful owner. This matches the Critical category: "NEAR ... moved out of a pool ... by a party not entitled to it" / "claims exceeding the NEAR actually held". The attack is repeatable across every pause window and scales with the amount of real liquid balance present in the contract at the time.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions: the pool owner (a legitimate, non-malicious action — e.g. during validator key rotation or an incident) must have called the owner-only pause function. This is a realistic, expected operational event, not attacker-controlled, but the exploitation itself requires no privilege: any delegator who has previously staked can call `unstake`/`unstake_all` while paused, wait out `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK`, and race to call `withdraw` before other legitimate liquid-balance holders do. Attacker cost is just gas and normal transaction fees; the technique is trivially repeatable in every future pause window on any pool using this exact contract.

### Recommendation
- In `internal_withdraw`, cap or gate withdrawal by the contract's actual liquid balance (e.g., assert `env::account_balance() >= amount` before transferring), or
- Disallow/queue `unstake` while `paused` so that `account.unstaked` can never be credited without the corresponding real un-stake `Promise` having been issued, or
- Add an `on_stake_action`-style callback for withdrawals that reverts the `account.unstaked` decrement (`assert_self` + `is_promise_success()`) if the outgoing `Transfer` fails, restoring the pre-image state.

### Proof of Concept
Using `near-sdk-sim`/`near-workspaces` with two delegator accounts against the deployed `staking-pool` contract:
```
1. deploy staking pool, owner stakes validator key.
2. delegator_A: deposit_and_stake(100 NEAR)   -> internal_restake actually locks 100 NEAR
3. delegator_B: deposit(10 NEAR) only          -> stays liquid (never staked)
   assert contract account_balance() >= 10 NEAR (B's liquid unstaked funds)
4. owner: pause_staking()
5. delegator_A: unstake_all()
   - assert account.unstaked == 100 for A (ledger)
   - assert env::account_balance() unchanged (still ~10 NEAR liquid, since internal_restake no-op'd)
6. advance epoch_height by NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK
7. delegator_A: withdraw(100 NEAR)
   - internal_withdraw's checks pass (account.unstaked >= 100, delay elapsed)
   - Promise::new(A).transfer(100) is dispatched against a contract that only has ~10 real liquid NEAR
   - assert this transfer receipt fails (insufficient real balance) OR drains B's 10 NEAR entirely, leaving contract with 0 liquid balance
8. delegator_B: withdraw(10 NEAR)
   - assert this either panics/fails to actually deliver funds to B, or B's promise fails silently while account.unstaked for B is already zeroed
   - assert B never receives their 10 NEAR despite passing all of internal_withdraw's checks
```
Binding under test: `sum(account.unstaked)` should equal `env::account_balance()` before and after step 5/7; the PoC demonstrates it diverges once `paused == true`, and that divergence is realized as B losing access to their own liquid deposit because A withdrew first.

```rust
    pub(crate) fn internal_restake(&mut self) {
        if self.paused {
            return;
        }
        // Stakes with the staking public key. If the public key is invalid the entire function
        // call will be rolled back.
        Promise::new(env::current_account_id())
            .stake(self.total_staked_balance, self.stake_public_key.clone())
            .then(ext_self::on_stake_action(
                &env::current_account_id(),
                NO_DEPOSIT,
                ON_STAKE_ACTION_GAS,
            ));
```

```rust
    pub(crate) fn internal_withdraw(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Withdrawal amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);
        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to withdraw"
        );
        assert!(
            account.unstaked_available_epoch_height <= env::epoch_height(),
            "The unstaked balance is not yet available due to unstaking delay"
        );
        account.unstaked -= amount;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        env::log(
            format!(
                "@{} withdrawing {}. New unstaked balance is {}",
                account_id, amount, account.unstaked
            )
            .as_bytes(),
        );

        Promise::new(account_id).transfer(amount);
        self.last_total_balance -= amount;
    }
```
