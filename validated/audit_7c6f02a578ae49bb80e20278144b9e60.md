The CDO `_deposit` already accounts for actual received balance. Let me check the credit vault's epoch deposit, withdraw, and borrower-funding paths where the transferred amount is trusted rather than measured.### Title
Fee-on-transfer underlying inflates NAV via 1:1 strategy-token minting in `IdleCreditVault.deposit` — (File: contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol)

### Summary
The credit vault assumes `safeTransferFrom` credits it the full `_amount`. `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit` correctly mints tranche shares from the measured balance delta (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal`), but then calls `IIdleCDOStrategy(strategy).deposit(_amount)` with the **nominal** amount, and `IdleCreditVault.deposit` mints `_amount` strategy tokens 1:1 while only receiving `_amount - fee`. NAV (`getContractValue` = strategyToken balance × price=1) is therefore inflated by the fee on every deposit, and funded-claim accounting (`collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`) books the nominal amount while the strategy's real balance is short by the transfer fee.

### Finding Description
In `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit` (contracts/IdleCDOCreditVault.sol:191-212):

```solidity
uint256 _preBal = _contractTokenBalance(_token);
_transferUnderlyingsFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount);
_minted = _mintSharesAtCurrPrice(_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal, ...);
// ...
IIdleCDOStrategy(strategy).deposit(_amount);   // nominal, not received
```

Then in `IdleCreditVault.deposit` (contracts/strategies/idle/IdleCreditVault.sol:596-617):

```solidity
underlyingToken.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount);
_mint(msg.sender, _amount);   // mints 1:1 with nominal _amount, not balance delta
```

If the vault underlying is a token that can charge a transfer fee (e.g., USDT, whose `basisPointsRate` is settable by its owner — the exact scenario in the reference report), two divergences appear per deposit of `X` with fee rate `f`:

1. Depositor → CDO: CDO receives `X·(1-f)`, mints tranche shares for `X·(1-f)` (correct).
2. CDO → strategy: `deposit(X)` pulls `X` from the CDO (the shortfall `X·f` is taken from other depositors' residual balance), the strategy receives `X·(1-f)` but mints `X` strategy tokens to the CDO.

`getContractValue` counts `balanceOf(idleCDO) = X` against real holdings of `X·(1-f)`, so `virtualPrice` and the AA/BB NAVs are inflated by `X·f` plus the second fee leg (`X·f` lost from the CDO residual). The same nominal-vs-received mismatch exists in `collectWithdrawFunds`/`collectInstantWithdrawFunds` (lines 398-430): `pendingWithdraws` is reduced by the nominal `_amount`, but the strategy holds `_amount - fee`, so the last claimants in `_transferFundedClaim`/`_transferDefaultRecovery` find an underfunded balance and their claims revert or strand other claimants' funds.

### Impact Explanation
Solvency invariant broken: accounted NAV exceeds real holdings by ~2× the transfer fee on every deposit and every epoch funding pull. An unprivileged depositor can deposit during the buffer phase, wait one epoch, and `requestWithdraw`/`claimWithdrawRequest` at the inflated `virtualPrice`, extracting more than they contributed at the expense of remaining LPs; repeated cycles drain the pool. On the withdraw side, funded receipts summing to `pendingWithdraws` exceed the strategy's actual balance, so late claimants' transfers revert — permanent freezing of their unclaimed principal equivalent to the cumulative fee shortfall. Loss scales linearly with TVL and fee rate; at a 1% USDT fee, each deposit round-trip leaks ~2% of the deposited amount from the pool.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires the vault to be deployed with a fee-on-transfer-capable underlying. Idle credit vaults (Clearpool/Pareto-style) use stablecoins such as USDC/USDT as pool currency, and USDT's fee is currently 0 but can be enabled by its owner at any time — the identical precondition accepted as Medium in the reference report. No guard stops it: there is no received-amount check in `deposit`, `collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, or `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, and `price()` is hardcoded to `oneToken`, so inflated strategy-token balances translate 1:1 into inflated NAV. Exploitation needs no privileged role — only a normal deposit.

### Recommendation
Measure actual received amounts everywhere:

- In `IdleCDOCreditVault._deposit`, pass the measured delta (`_contractTokenBalance(_token) - _preBal`) to `strategy.deposit` instead of `_amount`.
- In `IdleCreditVault.deposit`, mint strategy tokens on the post-transfer balance delta, not `_amount`.
- In `collectWithdrawFunds`, `collectInstantWithdrawFunds`, and `finalizeDefaultRecovery`, decrement `pendingWithdraws`/`pendingInstantWithdraws` and accrue `defaultRecoveryReserve` by `balanceOf` delta, and align `lossRecoveryPriceByEpoch`/`defaultRecoveryPrice` with received amounts.
- Alternatively, document and enforce (at factory/deploy time) that only non-fee-on-transfer underlyings are supported.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork test (mainnet, USDT at `0xdAC17...`), abusing `vm.store`/mock owner call or a mock FoT token with a configurable `basisPointsRate`:

```solidity
function testFoTNAVInflation() public {
    // deploy IdleCDOCreditVault + IdleCreditVault with FoT token (fee = 1%)
    fot.setFee(100);
    deal(address(fot), alice, 1_000e6);
    fot.approve(address(cdo), type(uint256).max);

    uint256 minted = cdo.depositAA(1_000e6);
    // CDO mints shares for 990e6 received
    assertEq(minted, /* 990e6 worth of tranche tokens */);

    // Strategy was credited 1_000e6 strategy tokens but holds 980.1e6 underlying
    assertEq(strategy.balanceOf(address(cdo)), 1_000e6);
    assertEq(fot.balanceOf(address(strategy)), 980.1e6);

    // NAV is inflated by ~19.9e6 vs real holdings
    assertGt(cdo.getContractValue(), fot.balanceOf(address(strategy)));
}
```

The assertion gap (`getContractValue` > actual underlying held) is the quantitative loss; a subsequent epoch rollover + `claimWithdrawRequest` lets the depositor redeem at the inflated `virtualPrice`, demonstrating the drain.
